# Workflow: Leader Cadence (Nhip Quan Ly Marketing)

> Vong quan ly cua Marketing Leader/CEO: chien luoc → ngan sach → brief → duyet output → bao cao tuan → retro. Moi quyet dinh co data, moi output qua gate duyet, moi campaign co bai hoc.

---

## Tong quan

```
Thoi gian: Chien luoc theo quy; brief theo campaign; review theo tuan
Skills su dung: 10 skill
Output: Ke hoach + budget + brief + feedback duyet + weekly report + retro
Tan suat: Quy / Campaign / Tuan
```

## Chi tiet tung buoc

### Theo quy / ky ke hoach

| Buoc | Skill | Output | Ghi chu |
|------|-------|--------|---------|
| 1 | `00-ke-hoach-mkt` | Ke hoach tong (hoac ban 1 trang cho SME) | Doc data ky truoc truoc khi lap |
| 2 | `61-budget-planning` | Phan bo ngan sach kenh/funnel/thang | Co quy du phong + nguong cat lo |
| 3 | `58-positioning` / `59-go-to-market` / `60-launch-playbook` | Khi ra san pham moi | Tuy tinh huong |

### Theo campaign

| Buoc | Skill | Output | Gate |
|------|-------|--------|------|
| 4 | `02-brief-chien-dich` | Brief + timeline phan cong | Team hieu khong can hoi lai |
| 5 | `64-team-brief` | Giao viec 1:1 + agenda hop | Moi task co owner + deadline + tieu chi done |
| 6 | `62-marketing-review` | Duyet content brief + ads copy | Approve / revise co ly do |
| 7 | `47-design-review` | Duyet visual | Cham diem theo checklist |

### Theo tuan

| Buoc | Skill | Output |
|------|-------|--------|
| 8 | `07-bao-cao-marketing` (che do weekly) | Weekly update CEO 1 trang: so vs target, winner/loser, quyet dinh can duyet |
| 9 | `13-phan-tich-du-lieu` | Decision log: quyet dinh + can cu + expected impact |

### Ket thuc campaign

| Buoc | Skill | Output |
|------|-------|--------|
| 10 | `63-campaign-retrospective` | What worked / what didn't / why + action co owner |
| 11 | Cap nhat Brand Hub + playbook | Bai hoc thanh tai san he thong (`34-ai-marketing-os`) |

## Tinh huong dac biet

| Tinh huong | Skill |
|------------|-------|
| So xau khong ro nguyen nhan | `03-danh-gia-hieu-suat` (root cause) |
| Khung hoang truyen thong | `66-crisis-playbook` + `15-social-listening` |
| Thue agency/freelancer | `67-agency-vendor-brief` |
| Danh gia nhan su marketing | `65-team-performance-review` |
| Theo doi doi thu dinh ky | `08-nghien-cuu-doi-thu` (che do snapshot thang) |

---

## Quy tac chat luong

- Weekly review chi chot 1-3 quyet dinh — nhieu hon la thieu uu tien.
- Khong duyet output khi thieu can cu (insight, data, brand voice).
- Retro bat buoc sau moi campaign — khong co retro thi khong scale duoc playbook.
- Bai hoc phai duoc ghi vao Brand Hub / knowledge, khong nam trong dau mot nguoi.
