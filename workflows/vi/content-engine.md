# Workflow: Content Engine (Vong Lap Content Thang)

> He thong content khep kin: tu nen tang brand voice den do luong va plan ky sau tu data. Ket qua ky truoc la input bat buoc cua ky sau.

---

## Tong quan

```
Thoi gian: Nen tang lam 1 lan; vong lap chay moi thang
Skills su dung: 12 skill (4 nen tang + 8 lap lai)
Output: Brand Voice + Calendar + Content san dang + Audit + Next plan
Tan suat: Nen tang 1 lan/quy review; vong lap moi thang
```

## Chi tiet tung buoc

### Giai doan 0 — Nen tang (lam 1 lan)

| Buoc | Skill | Output | Ghi chu |
|------|-------|--------|---------|
| 0.1 | `35-brand-voice` | `brand-voice-[brand]-[date].md` | Moi skill content sau nay tham chieu file nay |
| 0.2 | `09-insight-khach-hang` | Persona 3 tang + customer language | Insight la goc cua angle |
| 0.3 | `08-nghien-cuu-doi-thu` | Content gap doi thu | Tim khoang trong content |
| 0.4 | `01-lich-noi-dung` (phan strategy) | Content pillar + ti le | Chot pillar truoc khi len lich |

### Giai doan 1 — Lap ke hoach (ngay 1-3 thang)

**Skill:** `01-lich-noi-dung` → `36-content-brief`
**Input:** Pillar + KPI thang + bai hoc tu `40-next-content-plan` ky truoc
**Output:** Calendar thang + brief tung bai trong tuan dau

### Giai doan 2 — San xuat (hang ngay/tuan)

**Skill:** `37-caption-social`, `04-script-video`, `05-copy-quang-cao`, `14-email-marketing`, `38-seeding-plan`, `06-brief-ugc-egc`
**Input:** Content brief tung bai + brand voice
**Output:** Content san dang / san giao
**Quy tac:** Moi output doi chieu brand voice truoc khi trinh duyet.

### Giai doan 3 — Brief visual (sau khi copy duyet)

**Skill:** `42-brief-hinh-anh`, `43-brief-carousel`, `44-brief-video-editor`
**Input:** Copy da duyet + brand guideline
**Output:** Brief giao designer/editor → qua `47-design-review` truoc khi dang

### Giai doan 4 — Do luong (ngay 28-30)

| Buoc | Skill | Output |
|------|-------|--------|
| 4.1 | `39-content-audit` | Winner/loser theo pillar, format, hook |
| 4.2 | `07-bao-cao-marketing` | Bao cao content + community thang |
| 4.3 | `40-next-content-plan` | Plan thang moi tu data (70 giu / 20 toi uu / 10 thu moi) |

---

## Quy tac chat luong

- Khong san xuat khi chua co brief; khong brief khi chua co calendar; khong calendar khi chua co pillar.
- Moi bai phai truy ve 1 pillar + 1 insight; khong dang bai "cho co".
- Cuoi thang bat buoc chay audit — plan thang moi phai dua tren data, khong doan mo.
