# Customer Insight Framework — Hieu Khach Bang Tam Ly, Khong Phai Demographic

> Cap nhat: 2026-07 | Kien thuc nen tang — tang tu duy. SOP thuc thi xem `skills/vi/09-insight-khach-hang`.

## Phan 1: Consumer vs Shopper — hai vai tro khac nhau

### Consumer (nguoi dung san pham)
- **Ai:** nguoi thuc su su dung san pham/dich vu.
- **Quan tam:** chat luong, ket qua, hieu qua.
- **Dau:** san pham khong hieu qua, ket qua khong nhu ky vong.
- **Muon:** thay ket qua ro rang, chat luong tot.

### Shopper (nguoi mua hang)
- **Ai:** nguoi quyet dinh mua (co the la consumer hoac nguoi khac).
- **Quan tam:** gia, tien loi, bao hanh, review.
- **Dau:** chi tien ma khong biet co hieu qua khong.
- **Muon:** gia fair, review tot, minh bach.

**Vi du:** nu VP (shopper) mua my pham cho me (consumer) → 2 vai tro khac nhau → message phai khac.

## Phan 2: Insight theo hanh trinh pheu 6 buoc

### Buoc 1 & 2: Tiep can + Dung lai

**Khach dang nghi:** "Cai nay hay, lien quan den minh sao?"

Cau noi noi tam (vi du nu 25-40, lo mun):
- "Lai spam quang cao"
- "Mun minh co cach chua khong?"
- "Nguoi nay biet gi ve da toi"

Content nen:
- Interrupt thought: "90% cach chua mun cua ban sai".
- Show proof: hinh truoc/sau thuc te.
- Make relatable: "Toi cung bi mun 10 nam".

### Buoc 3 & 4: Theo doi + Tin tuong

**Khach dang suy:**
- "Trang nay hay, follow xem them."
- "Co dang tin khong?"
- "Chi phi bao nhieu, co rui ro khong?"

Cau noi noi tam:
- "So clinic ve benh, ep mua goi dat"
- "Khong biet co hieu qua khong"
- "Bac si co thuc su chuyen khong"

Content nen:
- Case study cu the (ten khach, anh truoc/sau).
- Chung chi, bang cap.
- Testimonial tu khach da di → tang trust.

### Buoc 5: Dan ve

**Khach dang dao dong giua "muon" va "so".**

Rao can lon nhat: "Khong biet buoc tiep theo la gi."

Content nen:
- CTA ro rang: "Click de booking lich tu van mien phi".
- Giam friction: "Tu van 30 phut, khong yeu cau thanh toan".
- Tao urgency: "Slot con lai hom nay: 2 cho".

### Buoc 6: Chot don

**Khach da quyet dinh mua.**

Content nen:
- Cam on, welcome ho.
- Set expectation: "Ban se nhan gi ke tiep".
- Gan ten nguoi ban/kenh (de track conversion).

## Phan 3: Persona 3 lop (vi du clinic da lieu)

### Lop 1: Noi dau chinh

**"Toi khong hieu da minh bi gi va so bi lua."**

Day khong phai insight kieu sach giao khoa — no phan anh noi so thuc su cua khach.

### Lop 2: Hanh vi quyet dinh

| Khia canh | Chi tiet |
|-----------|----------|
| **So sanh** | Xem 5-10 clinic truoc khi quyet |
| **Trigger** | Case study KH tuong tu, gia ro rang |
| **Timeline** | 2-3 tuan suy nghi truoc khi call |
| **Chia se** | Hoi ban be truoc khi di |

### Lop 3: Rao can

| Rao can | Cach khac phuc |
|---------|----------------|
| "Dat qua" | Entry offer 350K, thay ket qua ngay |
| "Khong biet dieu tri mat bao lau" | Phac do ro "8 tuan cam ket" |
| "So bi ep mua them" | Hien thi bang gia, khong hidden charge |
| "Khong co thoi gian" | Lich linh hoat, online consultation |

## Phan 4: Insight lam dinh vi nganh

### Sai lam pho bien: dinh vi theo dich vu
"Chung toi tri mun / nam / tre hoa" → qua rong, kho tao phan biet, khong du manh.

### Tu duy dung: dinh vi theo **van de tam ly lon nhat**
"Chung toi giup ban hieu chinh xac lan da cua minh — truoc khi ton tien sai cach."

Insight nay:
- **Rong:** cover mun, nam, tre hoa.
- **Manh:** "hieu + minh bach" = khac biet ro.
- **Actionable:** content huong den "giao duc + transparency".

## Phan 5: Cac insight pho bien theo nganh

### Beauty/Spa
- **Pain:** khong biet minh bi gi, so lua, khong hieu qua.
- **Trigger:** case study, gia minh bach, bac si credible.
- **Message:** "Soi da → chan doan → phac do ro rang".

### B2B Coaching
- **Pain:** khong biet bat dau tu dau, so loi, ton tien vo ich.
- **Trigger:** "Dung tool nay + hoc cach nay → ket qua sau 30 ngay".
- **Message:** "He thong + AI ho tro = khong can thue nguoi".

### SaaS/Software
- **Pain:** setup phuc tap, integration kho, support cham.
- **Trigger:** "Setup 30 phut, khong can developer".
- **Message:** "Tu 0 → hoat dong trong ngay".

## Tom tat

Insight khong phai demographic (tuoi, gioi tinh). Insight la **tam ly hanh trinh mua** — khach dang suy gi, so gi, muon gi o moi buoc. Insight manh nhat la **van de tam ly lon nhat** cua khach, rong du de build brand nhung cu the du de build content.
