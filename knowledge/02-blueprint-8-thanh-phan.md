# Blueprint 8 Thanh Phan — He Thong Marketing Hoan Chinh

> Cap nhat: 2026-07 | Kien thuc nen tang — tang tu duy. SOP thuc thi xem `skills/vi/00-ke-hoach-mkt`.

## Phan 1: 8 thanh phan bat buoc

Mot he thong marketing hoan chinh can 8 thanh phan. Thieu cai nao cung gay roi loan. Vi du minh hoa: clinic da lieu cho nu 25-40 (goi tat "Clinic A").

| # | Thanh phan | Cau hoi can tra loi | Vi du (Clinic A) |
|---|-----------|---------------------|------------------|
| 1 | **Thi truong** | Ngach nao? Du lon? | Da lieu nu 25-40 khu vuc mien Tay |
| 2 | **Khach hang** | Ai? Dau gi? Muon gi? So gi? | Nu VP lo mun + lao hoa, so bi lua |
| 3 | **San pham / Offer** | Ban gi? Khac doi thu o dau? | Tri mun cam ket 8 tuan, minh bach |
| 4 | **Kenh** | Kenh chu luc + ho tro? | Facebook Ads (reach) + Zalo OA (convert) |
| 5 | **Pheu chuyen doi** | Khach di bao nhieu buoc? | 6 buoc: tiep can → dung → follow → trust → dan → chot |
| 6 | **He thong noi dung** | May loai? Ai tao? Tan suat? | UGC/EGC/FGC/BGC, 5 bai/tuan, 4 pillar |
| 7 | **He thong do luong** | Do gi o moi tang? | KPI tuan: reach, CPL, conversion rate, ROAS |
| 8 | **Muc tieu doanh thu** | Can bao nhieu khach/thang? | 50 KH x 5tr AOV = 250tr doanh thu |

**Chu y:** thanh phan 6 & 7 la 2 thanh phan **SME hay bo qua nhat** — khong co thi khong biet toi uu cho nao.

## Phan 2: Cach doc Blueprint (tu day len)

### Sai lam pho bien: doc tu tren xuong
"Chon thi truong nao? → Da lieu. Chon kenh nao? → TikTok. Bay gio bat dau chay Ads."

**Van de:** chua tinh duoc can bao nhieu budget, bao nhieu content, bao nhieu nguoi trong team, conversion rate realistic la bao nhieu.

### Cach doc dung: doc tu **duoi len** (tu doanh thu)

**Buoc 1 — Muc tieu doanh thu:** "Nam nay muon 3 ty → 3 ty / 12 thang = 250 trieu/thang."

**Buoc 2 — Tinh so khach & AOV can:** "AOV trung binh 5 trieu → 250 trieu / 5 trieu = 50 khach/thang."

**Buoc 3 — Tinh conversion rate tu lead:** "Chuyen doi lead → khach = 40% → 50 / 0.4 = 125 lead/thang."

**Buoc 4 — Tinh CPL & budget:** "CPL benchmark nganh = 24K → 125 lead x 24K = 3 trieu budget/thang."

**Buoc 5 — Kiem tra bang reach:** "CPM = 40K. 125 lead / 5% conversion = 2,500 click tu ads → 2,500 / 2% CTR = 125K impression → 125K impression x 40K CPM / 1000 = ~5 trieu spend → doi chieu lai budget, dieu chinh gia dinh neu lech."

**Buoc 6 — Tinh kenh & content volume:** "De keo 125K impression, TikTok can bao nhieu bai? Trung binh 1 bai reach 25K → 125K / 25K = 5 bai/thang → ~1.25 bai/tuan → 1 nhan vien content lam duoc."

**Ket qua:** biet team can 1-2 nguoi (1 content, 1 sales), 5 bai TikTok/thang, budget ~3 trieu/thang — bang con so, khong bang cam giac.

## Phan 3: Chi tiet tung thanh phan

### Thanh phan 1: Thi truong (Market)

Cau hoi:
- Ngach nao? (beauty, B2B, coaching, dich vu)
- Du lon de kiem song khong? (can it nhat 1 ty market size/nam)
- Khach co san sang chi tien khong? (AOV bao nhieu)
- Bao nhieu doi thu, co phai red ocean khong?

Vi du:
- Dat: "Da lieu nu 25-40 tai 1 thanh pho lon" — ngach ro, du lon, san sang chi tien (dieu tri 3-50 trieu), it doi thu chuyen.
- Truot: "Online education cho sinh vien" — qua rong, qua dong doi thu, kho tao phan biet.

### Thanh phan 2: Khach hang (Customer)

**Khong phai demographic (tuoi, gioi tinh, thu nhap) — ma la psychographic (dau gi, so gi, muon gi).**

| Khia canh | Chi tiet | Vi du (Clinic A) |
|-----------|----------|------------------|
| **Noi dau chinh** | Van de lon nhat khach dang co | "Khong biet minh bi gi tren da, so bi ve benh" |
| **Noi so** | Cai gi ngan duong quyet dinh mua | "Chi tien my pham dat ma khong het", "Clinic dat qua" |
| **Ky vong** | Khach muon nhan duoc gi | "Chuyen gia nhin & noi that tinh trang", "Phac do ro rang" |
| **Trigger quyet dinh** | Cai gi lam khach quyet dinh mua | "Case study nam that co ket qua", "Gia ro rang tu dau" |

**Tai sao quan trong:** neu hieu "khach so bi lua" → khong dung claim "Tri mun 100% hieu qua" → thay bang "Phac do minh bach 8 tuan, cam ket tren giay".

### Thanh phan 3: San pham / Offer

**Offer architecture = cau truc san pham day du tu entry den flagship.**

| Tang | Ten | Muc dich | Gia | AOV | Vi du (Clinic A) |
|------|-----|----------|-----|-----|------------------|
| **Entry** | Dich vu entry | Cua vao, khach moi | Gia thap | 250K-600K | Goi tri mun co ban 350K |
| **Core** | Dich vu chinh | Nguon doanh thu | Gia trung | 3-6 trieu | Peel chuyen sau 2.9M |
| **Flagship** | Dich vu cao cap | LTV cao nhat | Gia cao | 10-50 trieu | Lieu trinh master 15-25M |
| **Retention** | Goi duy tri | Giu khach lau dai | Gia subscription | 500K-2M | Goi thang cham soc |

Nguyen tac:
- **Entry offer:** du re de quyet dinh nhanh (1-2 ngay) VA du tot de thay doi perception (khach thay hieu qua lan dau).
- **Core offer:** chot tai diem tiep xuc (phong kham sau soi da), khong chot qua inbox/ads.
- **Flagship offer:** khach da tin tuong sau, cam ket van ban, phac do 4-16 tuan.

### Thanh phan 4: Kenh (Channel)

Quy tac:
- **Chu luc (60% effort):** 1 kenh, duy tri deu, co plan ro rang.
- **Ho tro (25% effort):** 1 kenh, bo sung, khac loai hinh voi chu luc.
- **Nuoi brand (15% effort):** 1 kenh, organic, khong Ads.

Vi du: chu luc Facebook Ads TOFU (60%) + ho tro Zalo OA broadcast (25%) + nuoi brand TikTok ca nhan founder (FGC, 15%).

### Thanh phan 5: Pheu chuyen doi (Funnel) — 6 buoc

1. **Tiep can** — nguoi la thay noi dung lan dau qua thuat toan. *Do bang: Reach.*
2. **Dung lai** — noi dung cham dung noi dau. *Do bang: Watch time, Completion rate.*
3. **Theo doi** — nhan follow de xem them. *Do bang: Follower moi/tuan.*
4. **Tin tuong** — tuong tac sau, luu bai, nhan tin hoi. *Do bang: Save, DM, Comment chat.*
5. **Dan ve** — chuyen tu kenh ca nhan sang diem chot (landing page, Zalo OA, inbox). *Do bang: Click, Add to cart.*
6. **Chot don** — mua hang, dat lich, ky hop dong. *Do bang: Revenue, So don.*

**Chu y:** buoc 5 (dan ve) la buoc **bi bo qua nhieu nhat** — khong co CTA ro rang nen khach "thich ma khong mua".

### Thanh phan 6: He thong noi dung (Content System)

4 pillar noi dung:
- **Thu hut (30%)** — trend, giai tri, cau hoi goi to mo.
- **Chuyen mon (30%)** — kien thuc, insight, huong dan.
- **Chuyen doi (25%)** — offer, case study, urgency.
- **Ket noi (15%)** — community, hau truong, cam on.

**Quy tac vang:** khong 2 bai chuyen doi lien tiep. Moi bai ban can 2 bai gia tri di truoc.

4 nguon content:
- **UGC** (User Generated Content) — khach hang tu quay/viet, tin cay cao.
- **EGC** (Expert/Employee Generated Content) — chuyen gia/nhan vien viet, xay vi the.
- **FGC** (Founder Generated Content) — founder ca nhan, reach cao, trust manh.
- **BGC** (Brand Generated Content) — fanpage chinh thuc, kiem soat 100% nhung reach duoi 2%.

### Thanh phan 7: He thong do luong (Measurement)

KPI khong phai "doanh thu chung chung" ma KPI **theo tung tang pheu**:

| Tang pheu | KPI chinh | Target (vi du) | Benchmark nganh |
|-----------|-----------|----------------|-----------------|
| **TOFU (Tiep can)** | Reach, CPM, CTR | 50K reach/tuan | CPM 30-60K (beauty) |
| **MOFU (Lead)** | CPL, Qualify Rate | 125 lead/thang @ 24K CPL | CPL 80-200K (clinic) |
| **BOFU (Chot don)** | CPA, Close Rate | 50 don/thang, 40% close | Close 30-50% |
| **Retention** | Re-purchase Rate | 20% quay lai thang 2-3 | LTV gap 3x CAC |

**Ngon ngu dashboard:** khong dung jargon kho — dung tu CEO hieu. Vi du: "Tuan nay keo duoc 12K khach ghe, chot duoc 8 don" thay vi "Organic reach 12K, conversion rate 0.067%".

### Thanh phan 8: Muc tieu doanh thu (Revenue Goal)

```
Doanh thu muc tieu = 250 trieu/thang
                 / AOV trung binh = 5 trieu
                 = Khach can = 50 khach/thang
                 / Conversion rate = 40%
                 = Lead can = 125 lead/thang
                 x CPL = 24K
                 = Budget can = 3 trieu/thang
```

## Phan 4: Cac loi Blueprint pho bien

1. **Thieu he thong noi dung** — blueprint chi noi "dang content" nhung khong noi may loai, ti le, ai tao → team quay lai "dang bai theo cam hung".
2. **Thieu he thong do luong** — KPI chi la "doanh thu 250 trieu" → khong biet chi so nao dang lech, khong biet toi uu cho nao.
3. **Mau thuan giua offer architecture & funnel** — funnel 6 buoc nhung offer chi co 1 loai (core) → khach entry khong noi vao, khach flagship khong noi len cap.
4. **Chon kenh roi tinh, khong tinh roi chon** — "Chung toi chay TikTok" roi sau tinh so thi "can 10 bai/tuan ma team 1 nguoi lam khong kip".
