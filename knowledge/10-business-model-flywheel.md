# Business Model Flywheel — Thiet Ke Mo Hinh Doanh Thu Nhieu Tang

> Cap nhat: 2026-07 | Kien thuc nen tang — tang tu duy. Framework tong quat cho SME dich vu/tri thuc (dao tao, consulting, agency, dich vu chuyen mon). So lieu trong file la **vi du minh hoa** de thay ti le giua cac tang — thay bang so cua doanh nghiep ban.

## Phan 1: Flywheel front-end ↔ back-end

Mo hinh ben vung nhat cho SME dich vu la 2 tang san pham nuoi lan nhau:

### Front-end — san pham gia thap (canh cong)

- **Doi tuong:** ca nhan, chu doanh nghiep nho, nguoi moi.
- **San pham vi du:** khoa hoc ngan, workshop, e-learning, goi trai nghiem, audit mini.
- **Gia:** thap (vai tram K den vai trieu) — de quyet dinh nhanh.
- **Muc tieu:**
  - Xay thuong hieu + tao luu luong inbound.
  - Sang loc va nuoi tep khach cho back-end.
  - Tao case study va cong dong.
- **KPI vi du:** 50-100 khach front-end/quy, LTV muc tieu gap 2-3 lan gia ban dau.

### Back-end — dich vu gia cao (core revenue)

- **Doi tuong:** khach da tin tuong tu front-end, doanh nghiep co team.
- **Luong dien hinh 3 cap:**
  1. **Entry back-end** (goi chan doan / coaching ngan): thu nghiem quan he, gia tam trung.
  2. **Core back-end** (dong hanh 3-12 thang): kem team, setup he thong — nguon doanh thu chinh.
  3. **Flagship** (chuyen doi toan dien): nang cap ca phong ban/quy trinh, gia cao nhat.
- **Muc tieu vi du:** 8-12 client back-end/nam.

### Vong lap flywheel

```
Khach front-end gioi (hoc vien/khach le)
      |
duoc gioi thieu → hop dong back-end (doanh nghiep)
      |
doanh nghiep thay hieu qua → gui team tham gia front-end
      |
quay lai front-end tao demand moi + case study moi
```

Flywheel chi quay khi front-end du tot de tao trust, va back-end du sau de tao ket qua dang ke case study.

## Phan 2: 4 revenue tracks (vi du cho don vi trien khai AI Marketing OS)

### Track 1: Setup one-time (du an dong goi)

- **Dung cho:** khach muon setup he thong tu dau (vi du AI Marketing OS).
- **Deliverable vi du:** Brand Hub (6 files, 2 tuan) + 5 Projects setup (1 tuan) + bo skill files (2 tuan) + Notion master (1 tuan) + training team 1 ngay. Timeline ~6 tuan.
- **Pricing theo goi:** Starter (pham vi nho) / Team (day du + training) / Custom (them MCP + ongoing support).

### Track 2: Kickstart co-execution (bounded scope)

- **Dung cho:** khach da co he thong, can cung chay 2-3 campaign mau trong 8-12 tuan.
- **Muc tieu:** capability transfer — team khach hoc duoc, khong phu thuoc ben trien khai.
- **Nguyen tac:** khong phai agency (khong lam thay) — la partner (cung lam de hoc).
- **Pricing:** fixed scope, thoa thuan theo do phuc tap.

### Track 3: Monthly retainer (MRR)

- **Dung cho:** khach muon ongoing support, optimization, planning.
- **Scope:** weekly review + optimization; monthly strategy & planning; quarterly roadmap + upskilling.
- **Target vi du:** 5-10 retainer clients de co dong MRR on dinh.

### Track 4: Education productized (front-end scale)

- **Dung cho:** ca nhan, startup, doanh nghiep nho.
- **San pham:** khoa hoc theo batch, e-learning subscription, lop entry.
- **Vai tro:** kenh acquisition chi phi thap nhat cho 3 track tren.

**Diem mau chot:** 4 track khong tach roi — chung feed vao nhau. Education tao lead cho Setup; Setup tao nhu cau Kickstart; Kickstart chuyen thanh Retainer.

## Phan 3: Lo trinh doanh thu theo nam (pattern)

| Nam | Trong tam | Cau truc doanh thu dien hinh |
|-----|-----------|------------------------------|
| **Nam 1** | Build reputation + 2-3 case study | Chu yeu front-end/education; back-end moi bat dau |
| **Nam 2** | Scale back-end | Education giu on dinh; Setup + Kickstart tang manh |
| **Nam 3** | Them MRR | Ca 4 track chay; retainer tao nen doanh thu du doan duoc |

Quy tac: khong scale back-end truoc khi co case study; khong ban retainer truoc khi chung minh duoc gia tri qua du an dong goi.

## Phan 4: 4 nguyen tac khong vi pham

### Nguyen tac 1: Khong lam thay — chi coach
- **Tai sao:** lam thay tao dependency, khong scale, ton thoi gian founder.
- **Cach dung:** "Ban lam, toi guide. Cach nay ban hoc duoc, thay doi sau khong can toi."

### Nguyen tac 2: Lean team — khong tich luy fixed cost
- **Tai sao:** moi project + 1 FTE co dinh = doanh thu khong tang tuyen tinh.
- **Cach dung:** dung freelancer/contractor per-project, khong hire permanent khi chua du cong viec deu.

### Nguyen tac 3: No dependency — moi deliverable phai transfer duoc
- **Tai sao:** neu delivery phu thuoc 1 nguoi, doanh nghiep khong ban duoc, khong scale duoc.
- **Cach dung:** "Sau X tuan, team khach phai tu chay he thong mot minh." Moi thu phai co SOP/skill file di kem.

### Nguyen tac 4: Case study truoc khi scale
- **Tai sao:** 1 success story > 100 sales pitch.
- **Cach dung:** chon 2-3 client "proof" → lam that tot → lay testimonial + ket qua so → moi ban rong.

## Phan 5: Chu ky van hanh 5 buoc

1. **Acquire** — inbound tu education + content; outbound outreach tep khach cu/alumni.
2. **Diagnose** — 90 phut audit mien phi; identify pain, opportunity, cost; quyet dinh scope & pricing.
3. **Convert** — viet proposal, negotiation, ky hop dong.
4. **Deliver** — setup + coaching + handover; weekly review + optimization; monthly planning.
5. **Proof** — thu thap ket qua (KPI, revenue, testimonial); xin permission lam case study; share publicly → feed nguoc vao buoc Acquire.

## Phan 6: Cau truc team lean

### Core team (founder + 2 FTE)

| Role | Focus | Gio/tuan |
|------|-------|----------|
| **CEO/Founder** | Strategy, client relationship, closing | 40 |
| **Trainer/Consultant** | Coaching, setup he thong, delivery | 40 |
| **Operations** | Admin, billing, Notion, scheduling | 30 |

### Support (freelance per-project)
- Designer (Canva, landing page): tinh theo file.
- Content writer (long-form): tinh theo bai.
- Video editor (ads creative): tinh theo video.

## Phan 7: Metrics can track

- **Monthly:** khach front-end moi; ti le chuyen front-end → back-end (%); hop dong back-end moi; MRR tu retainer.
- **Quarterly:** doanh thu tung track; tong doanh thu vs target; so case study moi.
- **Annually:** tang truong YoY; nhu cau scale team; do san sang launch san pham moi.

## Phan 8: Risk & mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| **Founder bottleneck** | Train 1-2 trainer co the delivery doc lap |
| **Khach khong len duoc level** | Scope entry back-end ro rang, khong hua vuot kha nang |
| **Seasonal demand** (front-end giam theo mua) | Back-end retainer offset seasonal dip |
| **Market saturation** | Lo trinh mo rong: education → consulting → software/productized → dau tu |

## Tom tat

Business model flywheel = **front-end gia thap tao trust + tep khach → back-end gia cao tao doanh thu chinh → ket qua back-end tao case study nuoi lai front-end**. 4 revenue tracks feed vao nhau, khong tach roi. 4 nguyen tac (khong lam thay, lean team, no dependency, case study truoc scale) dam bao doanh thu tang ma khong phai tang team tuyen tinh.
