# K-C1 — Chien Luoc Chien Dich & He Thong Kenh

> Cap nhat: 2026-07 | Tang: Content | Dung cho: Content Lead, Campaign Manager, Marketing Manager.
> SOP thuc thi lien quan: `skills/vi/11-thiet-lap-kenh`, `02-brief-chien-dich`, `01-lich-noi-dung`.

## 1. Pheu 7 tang

```
Traffic → Raw Lead → Cold Lead → Warm Lead → Hot Lead → Customer → Loyal Customer
```

**Nguyen tac:** moi buoc co bottleneck khac nhau. Tim dung diem nghen → fix dung cho. **Dung do them traffic khi pheu dang ro** — do chi lam chi phi tang nhanh hon doanh thu.

Cach tim diem nghen: tinh CVR tung buoc, so voi benchmark, buoc nao lech xa nhat la diem nghen.

## 2. He thong kenh 4 tang

| Tang | Vai tro | Kenh |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — TRAFFIC | Keo nguoi moi | UGC Network + FGC Network + Brand Channels |
| 2 — TRUST | Xay niem tin | Website/Blog + Fanpage chinh + Landing page thu cap |
| 3 — CONVERSION | Chot don | Landing page chuyen biet + Messenger / Zalo OA |
| 4 — NURTURE | Nuoi duong & giu chan | Email MKT + Zalo OA + Facebook Group + Data Group |

### 3 nguon content tao traffic

| Nguon | Kenh | Vai tro |
|---|---|---|
| UGC (User Generated) | Threads, TikTok, Facebook, fanpage ve tinh | Reach rong, authentic |
| FGC (Founder Generated) | TikTok Founder, Facebook Founder, LinkedIn | Authority, trust |
| Brand Channels | TikTok brand, Threads brand, fanpage chinh | Consistent, owned media |

He thong nay tap trung **UGC + FGC**. EGC (employee generated) la lop bo sung tuy chon — chi mo khi team du lon va da co quy trinh duyet noi dung.

## 3. Mapping kenh theo giai doan funnel

| Giai doan | Kenh phu hop | Loai content | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | TikTok, Threads, Fanpage, Ads reach | Education, Story | Follow / Save |
| Consideration | Facebook, YouTube, Blog, Email | Case study, Review | Tim hieu them |
| Conversion | Landing page, Messenger, Zalo | Offer, Testimonial, FAQ | Mua ngay |
| Retention | Group, Email, Zalo OA | Community, Loyalty | Gioi thieu / Mua them |

Loi thuong gap: dung CTA "Mua ngay" o giai doan Awareness → CPMess cao, tep lanh khong chuyen doi.

## 4. Chien thuat tang traffic tu nhien

**Kenh Traffic:** he thong UGC/FGC + Seeding + SEO social + KOC booking + Ads + Lead magnet.

**Kenh Chuyen doi:** Email nurture + Group community + Webinar/Livestream + Chatbot + Direct marketing.

## 5. Lua chon kenh cho SME team 1-3 nguoi

| Kenh | Effort | Cost | Time to result |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok organic | Cao | Thap | 1-3 thang |
| Facebook Ads | Trung binh | Trung binh | Ngay |
| Threads | Thap | Thap | 1-2 tuan |
| Email MKT | Trung binh | Thap | 2-4 tuan |
| Seeding Group | Cao | Thap | 2-4 tuan |

**Nguyen tac chon kenh:**

1. Chon 1 kenh chu luc de master truoc.
2. Them 1 kenh bo tro sau khi kenh chu luc da on dinh.
3. **Khong mo kenh moi khi kenh cu chua co quy trinh** — kenh khong co quy trinh la kenh se chet trong 6 tuan.

Ket hop de xuat cho team 1-3 nguoi: 1 kenh organic (dai han) + Facebook Ads (ngan han, ra so ngay) + Email/Zalo (nurture).

## 6. Repurpose Workflow — 1 content thanh nhieu format

**1 Livestream (60-90 phut)** →
- 5-10 clip TikTok/Reels (30-60s)
- 3-5 quote → anh Threads/Facebook
- 1 thread text 10 diem
- 1 bai blog day du
- 1 email nurture tu noi dung chinh

**1 Blog post** →
- Thread text 10-15 y
- Infographic tom tat
- Video giai thich
- Email newsletter

**1 Case study** →
- Post dai Facebook (Before/After)
- Carousel Instagram
- Video testimonial
- Quote → anh
- Email social proof

**Nguyen tac repurpose:** san xuat 1 lan o dinh dang **nang nhat** (livestream, blog dai), roi cat nho — khong lam nguoc lai.
