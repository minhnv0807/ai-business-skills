# Phan 1 — Creative & Copy Strategy

## 1. Quy tac 80/20

**80% hieu qua ads den tu creative.** Creative phai dung doc lap nhu mot content — khong phu thuoc vao targeting de "cuu". Neu creative yeu, moi toi uu targeting chi lam giam thiet hai, khong tao ra ket qua.

## 2. 5 dang Hook hieu qua

| Dang hook | Cong thuc |
|---|---|
| Pain Hook | "Ban co dang [pain point that] khong?" |
| Benefit Hook | "Cach [doi tuong] [dat ket qua] chi trong [thoi gian]" |
| Curiosity Hook | "Hau het [doi tuong] khong biet dieu nay ve [chu de]" |
| Story Hook | "Thang truoc, toi [situation]..." / "Mot KH vua nhan: '[quote]'" |
| Pattern Interrupt | "DUNG LAI — Neu ban [condition], doc cai nay" |

## 3. Copywriting Frameworks

- **AIDA:** A (hook attention) → I (interest bang data/story) → D (desire bang benefit + proof) → A (CTA ro).
- **PAS:** P (dat ten van de) → A (khuech dai hau qua) → S (giai phap cu the).
- **BAB:** Before (KH co van de) → After (sau khi co giai phap) → Bridge (san pham la cau noi).

## 4. Ad Format theo Objective

| Objective | Format tot | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Video 6-15s, image 1080x1080 | Hook trong 2 giay dau |
| Traffic / Messages | Video 15-30s, single image + copy | Ho lo vua du de nguoi ta click |
| Conversions | Video testimonial 30-60s, carousel co offer | Khach that, ket qua that |

## 5. Copy theo TOFU / MOFU / BOFU

| Tang | Cau truc copy | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| TOFU | [Pain hook] + [Educate nhe] | Learn More / Watch More |
| MOFU | [Social proof / case study] + [Solution] | Sign Up / Download |
| BOFU | [Address objection] + [Strong offer + urgency] | Shop Now / Dang ky ngay |

# Phan 2 — Analytics Performance

## 1. Doc so theo thu tu (tu tren xuong)

| Layer | Nguoi dung | Chi so |
|---|---|---|
| Layer 1 | Lanh dao | Revenue, ROAS, CPO, CAC |
| Layer 2 | MKT Lead | CPMess, CPL, CVR Mess→Lead, CVR Lead→Order |
| Layer 3 | Executor | CPM, CTR, Frequency, Video view rate |

Luon doc tu Layer 1 xuong. Neu Layer 3 dep nhung Layer 1 xau → van de nam o offer / sale / gia, khong phai o ads.

## 2. Benchmark Viet Nam (tham chieu nhanh)

| Metric | Benchmark tot |
|---|---|
| CPM | 15K-50K VND |
| CTR | > 1.5% (image), > 2% (video) |
| Frequency | < 3 trong 7 ngay |
| CPMess | 15K-50K (tuy nganh) |

Bang benchmark day du theo nganh va theo tang chat luong: xem `references/benchmarks-vietnam.md`.

## 3. Tim bottleneck trong funnel

| Trieu chung | Nguyen nhan thuong gap |
|---|---|
| CPM cao | Audience qua hep / creative score thap |
| CTR thap | Hook yeu / visual khong bat mat |
| CPMess cao | Copy body yeu / offer chua hap dan |
| CVR Mess→Lead thap | Sales script yeu / sai tep |
| CVR Lead→Order thap | Trust chua du / gia / sai nac thang nhu cau |

Chan doan tu tren xuong: sua CPM truoc khi sua CTR, sua CTR truoc khi sua CPMess.

## 4. Khi nao Tat / Scale / Test

### TAT khi

- CTR < 0.5% sau 3-5 ngay
- CPMess > 3x benchmark
- Frequency > 5
- ROAS < 1 sau 7 ngay
- Zero conversion sau khi spend > 2x CPO target

### SCALE khi

- ROAS on dinh > target trong 5-7 ngay lien tiep
- CPO trending xuong
- Frequency < 3
- Audience chua saturate

**Cach scale:** tang 20-30% budget moi 3-4 ngay — khong tang dot ngot (tranh reset learning phase).

### TEST khi

- Creative da chay > 2 tuan
- Frequency > 3
- Performance plateau 5+ ngay

## 5. A/B Test Framework

**Thu tu uu tien test:** Hook/Headline → Visual format → Offer → Audience → Landing page.

**Quy tac:**
- 1 bien tai 1 thoi diem.
- Toi thieu 5-7 ngay chay.
- Toi thieu 50 conversions/variant truoc khi ket luan.

Test dung thu tu quan trong hon test nhieu: hook chiem phan lon bien dong ket qua.

## 6. Retargeting — tep nao, message nao

| Tep | Message |
|---|---|
| Video viewers 75% | Education + Proof |
| Website visitors (3 ngay) | Strong offer + Urgency |
| LP visitors khong convert | Address objection + FOMO |
| Inbox chua chot | Personal + Last chance |
| Past customers | Upsell / Cross-sell |

**Frequency cap retarget:** toi da 2-3 lan/ngay. Vuot nguong nay se gay phan cam va tang unfollow.
