## 1. Thiet lap ngan sach marketing

### Marketing nam o dau trong P&L

| Bao cao | Noi dung | Marketer quan tam |
|---|---|---|
| Balance sheet | Quy mo tai san vs nguon von | Khong truc tiep |
| Cash flow statement | Dong tien thu/chi | Gian tiep (timing chi ads) |
| Income statement (P&L) | Bao cao lai/lo | Day la bao cao Marketer nhin vao |

Ngan sach marketing la mot dong chi phi trong P&L — moi dong chi phai truy nguoc ve doanh thu no tao ra.

### 3 phuong phap thiet lap ngan sach

| Phuong phap | Cach tinh | Uu diem | Nhuoc diem |
|---|---|---|---|
| % Doanh thu | Ngan sach = X% doanh thu ky vong | Don gian, de duyet | Khong phan anh giai doan tang truong |
| Tinh nguoc tu muc tieu | Dat doanh thu → tinh nguoc CAC | Logic, co can cu | Can biet CAC lich su |
| Theo doi thu | Benchmark ngan sach nganh | Tranh under-invest | Khong phan anh dieu kien rieng |

Khuyen nghi cho SME: dung **tinh nguoc tu muc tieu** lam phuong phap chinh, dung **% doanh thu** lam tran kiem soat, dung **benchmark doi thu** de sanity-check.

### Cong thuc tinh nguoc tu doanh thu — 7 buoc

```
Buoc 1: Xac dinh Doanh thu muc tieu
Buoc 2: Doanh thu / AOV                  = So don can
Buoc 3: So don / CVR chot (30-50%)       = So Hot Lead can
Buoc 4: Hot Lead / CVR nurture (50-60%)  = So Warm Lead can
Buoc 5: Warm Lead / CVR thu lead (20-40%)= Tong Reach can
Buoc 6: Tong Lead x CPL                  = Ngan sach Ads toi thieu
Buoc 7: + Content + Tool                 = Total MKT Budget
```

Cac ti le CVR o tren la khoang tham chieu chung — luon thay bang so lich su cua chinh account neu co.

### 3 scenario khi lap ngan sach

| Scenario | CVR | CPL | Muc dich |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | Thap hon TB nganh | Cao hon TB | Truong hop xau nhat, du phong |
| Base case | Bang TB nganh | Bang TB | Ke hoach chinh de trinh duyet |
| Optimistic | Cao hon TB | Thap hon TB | Sau khi da toi uu 1-2 chu ky |

Lap plan theo **base case**, chuan bi dong tien theo **conservative**.

### Phan bo ngan sach theo kenh (tham chieu SME Vietnam)

| Kenh | % ngan sach |
|---|---|
| Meta Ads (FB + IG) | 50-60% |
| TikTok Ads | 15-20% |
| Google Ads / SEO | 10-15% |
| Content production | 10-15% |
| KOL / KOC / Seeding | 5-10% |
| Email / CRM tools | 2-5% |

## 2. He thong Objectives & KPIs — 3 tang

### Tang 1 — Business Objectives

4 loai: **Sales** (Volume & Value) | **Share** (thi phan) | **Growth** (tang truong) | **Profit** (loi nhuan).

### Tang 2 — Marketing Objectives

De tang Volume sales, co 4 don bay:

| Don bay | Y nghia |
|---|---|

| Frequency | Tan suat mua hang — mua lai nhieu lan hon |
| Consumption | Luong tieu thu moi lan mua — tang gio hang |
| Loyalty | Ti le trung thanh — giu chan khach cu |

### Tang 3 — Communication Objectives

Tang Awareness | Tang Key attributes (thuoc tinh thuong hieu muon duoc nho den).

## 3. KPI Framework theo 3 tang nguoi dung

### Tang CEO / Founder (hang thang / quy)

| KPI | Dinh nghia | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Doanh thu | Tong doanh thu tu MKT | Tu muc tieu kinh doanh |
| CAC | Chi phi de co 1 khach hang | < LTV / 3 |
| LTV | Gia tri vong doi khach hang | > 3x CAC |
| Payback period | Thoi gian hoan von CAC | < 6 thang voi SME |
| ROAS tong | Tong doanh thu / tong chi phi ads | > 3x |

### Tang Marketing Lead (hang tuan / thang)

| KPI | Dinh nghia |
|---|---|
| CPL | Chi phi de co 1 lead |
| CPO | Chi phi de co 1 don hang |
| CVR | Ti le chuyen doi theo tung buoc funnel |
| Organic traffic growth | Tang truong traffic tu nhien MoM |

### Tang Executor (hang ngay)

| KPI | Dinh nghia |
|---|---|
| Reach / Impression | Tong nguoi tiep can |
| CPM | Chi phi / 1000 luot hien thi |
| CTR | Ti le click / luot hien thi |
| CPMess | Chi phi / 1 tin nhan inbox |
| Engagement rate | Ti le tuong tac |

Nguyen tac doc so: doc tu tang tren xuong. Neu tang CEO xau nhung tang Executor dep → van de nam o conversion/sale, khong phai o ads.

## 4. OKR x KPI trong marketing

- **OKR** = cai nhin toan canh Business & Marketing — dinh huong quy.
- **KPI** = do luong chi tiet tung chien thuat / kenh — dieu hanh tuan.

Vi du (doanh nghiep dich vu, AOV ~3tr):

```
O: Tang doanh thu dong san pham chinh len 500tr/quy
  KR1: Dat 500 leads/thang voi CPL <= 200K
  KR2: Ti le chot don >= 30%
  KR3: AOV tang len 3.5tr (upsell)
    → KPI Meta Ads: CPMess <= 25K, CTR >= 2%
    → KPI Content: 40 bai/thang, ER >= 5%
```

## 5. LTV : CAC Ratio — nguong quyet dinh

| Ti le | Y nghia | Hanh dong |
|---|---|---|
| < 1:1 | Dang lo tren moi khach hang | Dung scale, fix product/price |
| 1:1 → 3:1 | Hoa von den kha thi | Toi uu conversion, tang AOV |
| > 3:1 | Healthy — co the grow | Bat dau scale |
| > 5:1 | Rat tot — scale manh | Tang ngan sach ads |

Growth rate = (Chi so hien tai - Chi so qua khu) / Chi so qua khu x 100%.
