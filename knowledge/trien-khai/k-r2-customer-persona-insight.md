# K-R2 — Customer Persona & Insight

> Cap nhat: 2026-07 | Tang: Research | Dung cho: Marketing Manager, Content Team, Planner.
> SOP thuc thi lien quan: `skills/vi/09-insight-khach-hang`, `35-brand-voice`, `36-content-brief`.

## 1. Cau truc Customer Persona day du — 5 tang

### Tang 1 — Nhan khau hoc

Gioi tinh | Do tuoi | Nghe nghiep / Level | Khu vuc | Thu nhap | Hon nhan | Hoc van.

### Tang 2 — Tam ly hoc

Gia tri theo duoi | Phong cach song | Phong cach mua sam | Dong luc mua | Rao can tam ly.

### Tang 3 — Hanh vi

Thoi diem mua | Cach tim hieu | Kenh mua pho bien | Dieu kien ra quyet dinh | Tan suat mua.

### Tang 4 — Need / Want / Aspiration

| Cap do | Noi dung |
|---|---|
| Need (chuc nang) | Ho can lam duoc gi cu the? |
| Want (cam xuc) | Ho muon cam thay the nao? |
| Aspiration (khat vong) | Ho muon tro thanh ai / dat duoc gi? |

### Tang 5 — Insight cot loi

```
Insight = Hanh vi quan sat duoc + Ly do that su dang sau
```

Phai la cau noi noi tam that cua KH, khong phai gia dinh cua marketer. Nguon lay: comment, inbox, review, phong van, transcript sale call.

## 2. 5 nac thang nhu cau — cau noi noi tam theo nac

| Nac | Trang thai | Cau noi noi tam | Cach marketing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Khong co nhu cau | "Minh on ma" | Khong target |
| 2 | Gap van de, chua nhan ra | "Toi thay bat on nhung khong biet tai sao" | Educate — dat ten van de |
| 3 | Nhan ra nhung chua cap bach | "Toi biet minh can lam gi do nhung chua gap" | Agitate — khuech dai hau qua |
| 4 | Muon giai quyet, chua chon | "Toi muon fix nhung khong biet chon ai" | Compare — khac biet hoa |
| 5 | San sang mua | "Toi san sang mua — chi can dung offer" | Close — offer ro rang |

**Quy tac phan bo ngan sach:** phan lon thi truong nam o nac 2-3, phan lon doanh thu ngan han nam o nac 4-5. Content phu nac 2-3, ads conversion phu nac 4-5.

## 3. Insight theo hanh trinh mua (AIDA)

### Awareness

- KH dang nghi: "Minh can mot phien ban tot hon cua chinh minh".
- Hanh vi: luot feed, xem video, doc bai chia se.
- Content phu hop: story, tinh huong relatable, "ban co dang gap...?"

### Consideration

- KH dang nghi: khong mua mon do — mua cam giac ma mon do mang lai.
- Hanh vi: so sanh, doc review, hoi nguoi quen.
- Content phu hop: so sanh, testimonial, behind-the-scenes.

### Conversion

- KH dang nghi: "Toi se mua neu no khien toi khong phai suy nghi them gi nua".
- Hanh vi: hoi gia, hoi bao hanh, hoi thoi gian giao.
- Content phu hop: offer, guarantee, urgency, FAQ.

### Retention

- KH dang nghi: "Neu toi thay hop, toi chang muon doi cho khac lam gi".
- Content phu hop: community, loyalty offer, UGC trigger.

## 5. Dau hieu insight manh vs yeu

| | Insight yeu | Insight manh |
|---|---|---|
| Do cu the | "KH muon chat luong tot, gia re" | Chi dung voi dung tep cua ban |
| Do bat ngo | Ai cung doan duoc | Lam team phai dung lai suy nghi |
| Kha nang khai thac | Ra duoc 1 cau slogan | Ra duoc >= 3 content angle |
| Phan ung KH | Khong phan ung | KH thot len "sao no hieu toi vay" |

**Test nhanh:** doc insight cho 1 KH that nghe. Neu ho gat dau va ke them chuyen cua ho → insight dung. Neu ho im lang → chua toi.
