## 1. Dinh nghia Content

Content = toan bo thong tin duoc tao ra va truyen tai toi KH muc tieu qua cac kenh — nham **giao duc, thuyet phuc, kich thich hoac nuoi duong** moi quan he — phuc vu muc tieu cu the theo tung giai doan hanh trinh KH.

### 4 hieu lam pho bien

| Hieu lam | Thuc te |
|---|---|
| Content = viet bai | Content la moi hinh thuc truyen tai: video, anh, livestream, email, tin nhan |
| Lam content phai sang tao | Hieu qua = gan gui + dung insight + dung thoi diem. Sang tao la thu yeu |
| Chi can content tot la du | Phai di kem he thong kenh + chien thuat phan phoi + CTA |
| Content chi de chay Ads | Content la xuong song thuong hieu dai han, ads chi khuech dai |

## 2. Content Strategy Framework — Pillar / Angle / Topic

```
Target Audience → Insight → Key Message
  → Pillar 01 / Pillar 02 / Pillar 03
      → Angle 1.1 / 1.2 / 1.3
          → Topic (bai viet / video cu the)
```

| Cap | Dinh nghia |
|---|---|
| Pillar | Chu de lon phan anh chuyen mon hoac gia tri cot loi cua brand |
| Angle | Goc nhin / cach tiep can cu the trong moi pillar |
| Topic | Bai viet / video cu the sinh ra tu moi angle |

Vi du (doanh nghiep dich vu B2B SME):

| Pillar | Angle | Topic vi du |
|---|---|---|
| Kien thuc chuyen mon | Chien luoc cho SME | "5 buoc lap ke hoach voi ngan sach 10 trieu" |
| | Cong cu & AI | "Cach dung AI de rut ngan thoi gian viet content" |
| Hanh trinh khach hang | Ket qua thuc te | "Sau 3 thang trien khai, khach hang X dat duoc gi" |
| Founder Story | Goc nhin nghe | "Sai lam toi tung mac khi lam marketing" |

**Quy tac:** 3 pillar la du cho SME. Moi pillar 2-3 angle. Neu qua 5 pillar → thuong hieu bi loang, khan gia khong nho ban ve chuyen gi.

## 3. 3 loai content cot loi + ti le

### Education (40%)

- Muc tieu: build authority, thu hut audience nac 2-3.
- Framework: Problem → Solution → CTA.
- Format: "X dieu can biet", "Huong dan A-Z", "Tai sao X xay ra".

### Social Proof (30%)

- Muc tieu: tang trust, day KH tu nac 3 len nac 4-5.
- Framework: Story → Transformation → Invite.
- Format: case study Before/After/How, testimonial that, review.

### Connection / Story (20%)

- Muc tieu: build community, loyalty, tang share.
- Framework: Opinion → Explanation → Discussion.
- Format: behind the scenes, founder story, quan diem ve nganh.

### Promotion (10%)

- Direct offer, CTA manh, deadline ro rang.

Neu Promotion vuot 20% keo dai, engagement organic thuong tut trong 3-4 tuan.

## 4. He thong UGC / FGC

| Loai | Nguon | Uu diem | Cach kich hoat |
|---|---|---|---|
| UGC (User Generated) | Khach hang, cong dong | Authentic, trust cao, organic reach | Review request, challenge, hashtag |
| FGC (Founder Generated) | Founder / Leader | Authority, khac biet hoa, personal brand | Lich dang co dinh, template noi dung |

**Tai sao FGC quan trong:** con nguoi tin con nguoi hon tin brand. FGC la trust builder tu nhien ma doi thu khong copy duoc.

## 5. Hook Formula theo Platform

### TikTok / Reels (3-5 giay dau)

- "Sai lam lon nhat khi [lam X] la..."
- "[So] dieu [doi tuong] can biet ve [chu de]"
- "Toi da [lam X] va day la ket qua sau [thoi gian]"
- "POV: Ban la [doi tuong] va ban dang [tinh huong]"
- "Dung lam [X] nua. Lam cai nay thay the."

### Facebook / Threads (dong dau tien)

- Cau hoi: "Ban co biet [X] khong?"
- Bold claim: "[X] toi hoc duoc sau [Y nam]"
- Story hook: "Hom qua co 1 khach hang nhan cho toi..."
- Contradiction: "Toi da sai ve [X] trong suot [Y nam]"

### Email (subject line)

- To mo: "[Ten], ban da bo lo dieu nay?"
- Benefit: "Cach tang doanh thu 3x ma khong tang ngan sach ads"
- Urgency: "Con 24 gio — [offer]"
- Personal: "Toi muon thanh that voi ban ve [X]"

**Test hook:** viet 5 hook cho cung 1 topic, doc to len. Hook nao khien nguoi nghe hoi "roi sao nua?" thi giu.
