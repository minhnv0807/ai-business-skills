## 1. Fullstack Marketing vs Growth Marketing vs Brandformance

| Tieu chi | Fullstack Marketing | Fullstack Growth Marketing | Brandformance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tu duy goc | Bao phu toan bo cac mang/kenh | Thiet ke & toi uu he thong tang truong | Brand tao ra Performance, Performance nuoi lai Brand |
| Muc tieu | Marketing chay on dinh, du kenh | Tang truong ben vung, scale duoc | Vua xay thuong hieu vua ra so |
| Trong tam dieu hanh | Hoat dong & kenh | Nut that & don bay | Brand Foundation + Performance Layer |
| KPI | Kiem soat hieu qua trien khai | Ra quyet dinh tang truong | Brand metrics + Revenue metrics |
| Khi gap van de | Lam them / mo them kenh | Go bottleneck / doi cau truc | Toi uu ca Brand lan Performance |
| Phu hop voi | Team da on dinh, van hanh tot | Startup, SME can scale nhanh | SME muon ben vung dai han |

**Dinh nghia Fullstack Marketer** — nguoi co kha nang:
- Hieu thi truong, dinh huong chien luoc (market research, positioning, targeting).
- Thiet ke he thong marketing theo hanh trinh khach hang (funnel, kenh, noi dung, automation).
- Van hanh va do luong hieu qua (data analytics, conversion, CAC, LTV).
- Biet dung cong nghe & cong cu de scale (AI, CRM, Ads, automation).
- Bam sat muc tieu ban hang, trai nghiem khach hang va kha nang van hanh noi bo.

## 3. Phan khuc khach hang — 5 nac thang nhu cau

| Nac | Mo ta | Cach tiep can |
|---|---|---|

| 2 | Gap van de nhung chua nhan ra | Educate — dat ten van de |

| 5 | Biet ro van de & giai phap | Close — offer & CTA |

## 4. SVE Model

**SVE = Solution x Value x Education**

| Tru cot | Noi dung |
|---|---|
| Solution | USP, loi the canh tranh, cach giai quyet van de KH |
| Value | Gia tri cot loi, ky vong, vuot ky vong, gia ca |
| Education | Cach chia se gia tri thuong hieu toi khach hang |

```
Traffic → Raw Lead → Cold Lead → Warm Lead → Hot Lead → Customer → Loyal Customer
```

| Thanh phan | Vai tro |
|---|---|
