## 1. Landing Page so cap vs thu cap

| | So cap | Thu cap |
|---|---|---|
| La gi | Trang chuyen dung ban hang / thu data | Social (Fanpage, TikTok) hoac micro-content |
| URL | Rieng biet | Khong co URL rieng |
| CTA | 1 duy nhat | Dan ve LP chinh hoac Messenger |
| Dung khi | Chay ads, launch san pham | Content marketing, retargeting |

SME moi bat dau co the chay bang LP thu cap (fanpage + Messenger). Khi spend ads vuot nguong on dinh thi bat buoc phai co LP so cap de do duoc CVR.

## 2. Cau truc Landing Page 8 section

| # | Section | Noi dung bat buoc |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | HERO | Headline loi ich + sub-headline + visual + primary CTA |
| 2 | PAIN POINT | 3-5 bullet mo ta van de KH gap |
| 3 | SOLUTION | Feature → Benefit mapping — focus benefit, khong liet ke feature |
| 4 | SOCIAL PROOF | So lieu ("500+ KH") + testimonial that + logo KH |
| 5 | HOW IT WORKS | 3-5 buoc don gian |
| 6 | OFFER | Gia ro + bonus + deadline + guarantee |
| 7 | FAQ | 5-7 cau address rao can cuoi |
| 8 | FINAL CTA | Lap lai CTA + urgency + trust signals |

Nguyen tac: 1 landing page = 1 CTA. Moi CTA them vao lam giam CVR.

## 3. Headline Formula

- **Formula 1:** "[So] [Ket qua cu the] trong [Thoi gian] — Khong can [Rao can]"
- **Formula 2:** "Danh cho [Doi tuong] muon [Ket qua] ma khong [Pain point]"
- **Formula 3:** "Cach [Doi tuong] [Dat ket qua] ngay ca khi [Dieu kien bat loi]"

### Testimonial tot phai co du 5 yeu to

1. Anh that
2. Ten that
3. Ket qua cu the (co so)
4. Truoc / Sau
5. Dung tep muc tieu (nguoi doc phai thay minh trong do)

Testimonial thieu so lieu chi la loi khen — khong tao duoc conversion.

## 4. Content Ads theo TOFU / MOFU / BOFU

### TOFU (Cold Audience) — khong push product

- Message: dat ten van de.
- CTA: Learn more / Follow.

### MOFU (Warm Audience) — build trust

- Message: giai phap + proof.
- CTA: Tim hieu / Dang ky free.

### BOFU (Hot Audience) — convert

- Message: offer cu the + urgency.
- CTA: Mua ngay / Dang ky ngay.

Loi thuong gap: chay offer BOFU cho tep cold → CPMess cao gap 2-3 lan ma khong ra don.

## 5. Email Welcome Sequence — 5 email

| Email | Thoi diem | Muc dich | Noi dung chinh |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Ngay sau opt-in | Deliver lead magnet + welcome | Gui tai lieu + gioi thieu brand |
| E2 | Ngay 2 | Build trust | Cau chuyen / ly do founder lam viec nay |
| E3 | Ngay 4 | Educate | Insight / tip mien phi co gia tri that |
| E4 | Ngay 6 | Social proof | Case study + testimonial |
| E5 | Ngay 8 | Soft pitch | Gioi thieu offer + CTA |

Khong pitch truoc E4. Chuoi welcome pitch qua som lam tang unsubscribe rate.

## 6. Zalo OA Broadcast Framework

- **Tan suat:** toi da 2-3 lan/tuan.
- **Mix noi dung:** 40% Value | 30% Story/Update | 20% Social proof | 10% Offer.
- **Thoi diem gui tot (VN):** sang 7:00-8:30 | trua 11:30-12:30 | toi 20:00-21:30.

Theo doi unfollow rate sau moi broadcast — day la tin hieu som nhat cho biet tan suat hoac noi dung dang sai.

## 7. Nurture Sequence cho Hot Lead chua mua

```
Ngay 1: Follow-up ca nhan hoa
Ngay 3: Gui them social proof
Ngay 5: Address rao can cu the (gia / thoi gian / niem tin)
Ngay 7: Last chance offer / deadline
```

Neu sau ngay 7 van khong mua → chuyen ve **Warm Lead**, tiep tuc nurture bang content, khong tiep tuc push offer.

## 8. Checklist chat luong conversion content

- [ ] Headline noi ro ket qua, khong noi ve san pham
- [ ] Moi section tra loi 1 rao can cu the cua KH
- [ ] Social proof co so lieu that
- [ ] Chi co 1 CTA duy nhat tren trang
- [ ] Offer co deadline hoac gioi han ro rang
- [ ] FAQ address dung 5-7 cau hoi KH thuong hoi sale
