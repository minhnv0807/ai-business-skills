# K-C4 — UGC & Creator System

> Cap nhat: 2026-07 | Tang: Content | Dung cho: Content Lead, Community Manager.
> SOP thuc thi lien quan: `skills/vi/06-brief-ugc-egc`, `38-seeding-plan`, `18-referral-program`.

## 1. Phan loai nguon content tu nguoi that

| Loai | Nguon | Uu diem | Nhuoc diem |
|---|---|---|---|
| UGC (User Generated) | Khach hang that | Authentic cao, chi phi thap, social proof manh | Kho kiem soat chat luong |
| FGC (Founder Generated) | Founder / Leader | Authority, personal brand | Phu thuoc 1 nguoi |
| KOC (Key Opinion Consumer) | Creator dung that san pham | Trust cao hon KOL, cost thap hon | Reach nho hon KOL |
| KOL (Key Opinion Leader) | Nguoi anh huong lon | Reach rong | Chi phi cao, ROI kho do |

Thu tu uu tien cho SME ngan sach han che: **UGC → FGC → KOC → KOL**.

## 2. UGC Brief chuan (gui khach hang)

```markdown
XIN CHAO [TEN],

Cam on ban da tin tuong [Brand]! Khong can hoan hao — chi can that la du.

Ban co the chia se ve:
- Van de gap truoc khi dung [san pham]?
- Trai nghiem su dung thuc te?
- Ket qua sau khi dung?

Format:
- Video 30-60s doc 9:16, HOAC
- Anh 2-3 tam, HOAC
- Caption 3-5 cau tu nhien

Dang: tag @[brand] + hashtag #[...]
Thuong: [Voucher / Featured / Tang san pham]
```

Cau "khong can hoan hao" la yeu to lam tang ti le tra bai — KH ngai nhat la so lam khong dep.

## 3. KOC / KOL Brief chuan

```markdown
THONG TIN: San pham | Timeline | Platform | Dang content
MUC TIEU: [Awareness / Leads / Conversion] — KPI: [Views / Clicks / Code dung]

CAN CO
- [Key message 1]
- [Key message 2]
- Tag @brand + hashtag
- Disclosure (#ad) theo quy dinh nen tang

TONE: [Authentic / Fun / Professional]

KHONG DUOC
- [Dieu 1]
- [Dieu 2]

DELIVERABLES
- [So] video/post
- Preview truoc [X] ngay
- Khong xoa bai trong [X] thang
```

## 4. Seeding Strategy — 3 loai

### Loai 1 — Organic Seeding (khong tra tien)

Quy trinh 4 buoc:

1. **Research group:** size > 5000 thanh vien, activity > 10 posts/ngay.
2. **Warm-up 2-4 tuan:** join, comment, react — chua nhac ten brand.
3. **Seeding:** post gia tri cao, soft mention o cuoi.
4. **Track:** UTM links + hoi KH "biet qua dau".

Ti le an toan: **Value : Promotion = 4:1**. Tan suat: 1-2 lan/tuan/group.

### Loai 2 — Paid Seeding (KOC / KOL)

Tieu chi chon: ER > 3% | tep followers khop ICP | content style phu hop brand.

### Loai 3 — Community-Activated Seeding

Kich hoat thanh vien community chia se ra ngoai bang: challenge + incentive + template san.

## 5. Seeding vao Facebook Group — 4 phase

### Phase 1 — Research (1 tuan)

Tieu chi group: size > 5000 | activity > 10 posts/ngay | rules cho phep share.

### Phase 2 — Warm-up (2-4 tuan)

Join group → comment/reply cau hoi 3-5 lan/tuan → like, react → **khong de cap brand**.

### Phase 3 — Seeding Content (ongoing)

Dang bai hieu qua: cau hoi mo | chia se kinh nghiem (soft mention) | mini guide | case study ngan.

### Phase 4 — Do luong

UTM tracking | hoi "biet minh qua dau" trong form | track so DM den tu group.

## 6. Nhung dieu KHONG lam khi seeding

- Khong spam link san pham truc tiep.
- Khong post promotional ma khong co gia tri.
- Khong dung tai khoan moi khong co lich su hoat dong.
- Khong copy-paste cung noi dung sang nhieu group.
- Khong fake comment tao hoi thoai gia.
- Khong tag nguoi khong lien quan.

Vi pham cac diem tren khong chi bi ban khoi group ma con lam giam trust cua brand voi dung tep muc tieu.

## 7. Chi so danh gia he thong UGC/Creator

| Chi so | Y nghia | Muc tham chieu |
|---|---|---|
| UGC submission rate | % KH gui bai / tong KH duoc moi | 5-15% |
| Cost per UGC asset | Chi phi (voucher/qua) / 1 asset | Re hon nhieu lan asset studio |
| ER cua bai KOC | Do phu hop tep | > 3% |
| DM/lead tu group | Hieu qua seeding | Track theo tuan |
