# K-P-REPORT — Cau Truc Bao Cao Performance

> Cap nhat: 2026-07 | Tang: Performance | Dung cho: Media Buyer, Performance Lead, Marketing Manager.
> SOP thuc thi lien quan: `skills/vi/07-bao-cao-marketing`, `03-danh-gia-hieu-suat`, `21-audit-ads-performance`.

## 1. Triet ly bao cao — 3 nguyen tac

1. **So truoc — giai thich sau.** Nguoi doc phai thay ket qua trong 10 giay dau.
2. **Thang than — khong spin.** So xau viet thang, kem nguyen nhan va cach fix.
3. **Action-oriented.** Moi nhan xet phai kem "vay lam gi tiep".

Bao cao khong co action item khong phai bao cao — do la bang so lieu.

## 4. Dashboard cho lanh dao — metric can co

| Chu ky | Metric |
|---|---|
| Weekly (thu 2) | Doanh thu tuan vs target, tong spend vs budget, CPL + ROAS tong, leads moi |
| Monthly | Revenue vs target (chart), CAC trend, ROAS theo kenh, Top 5 campaign |

Nguyen tac: dashboard lanh dao khong qua 1 man hinh. Chi tiet de o dashboard team.

## 5. Loi thuong gap khi viet bao cao

| Loi | Hau qua | Cach sua |
|---|---|---|
| Chi liet ke so, khong ket luan | Nguoi doc tu suy dien, ra quyet dinh sai | Moi bang kem 1 cau verdict |
| Giau so xau hoac lam mo | Mat long tin, van de am i | Viet thang + kem nguyen nhan + fix |
| Khong so voi ky truoc | Khong biet dang tot len hay xau di | Luon co cot "vs ky truoc" |
| Action item khong co owner/deadline | Khong ai lam | Bat buoc du 3 cot: Action / Owner / Deadline |
| Bao cao qua dai | Lanh dao khong doc | Weekly <= 1 trang, Monthly <= 3 trang |

## 6. Checklist truoc khi gui bao cao

- [ ] Snapshot nam o dau, doc duoc trong 10 giay
- [ ] Moi metric co Target, Actual va so sanh voi ky truoc
- [ ] Diem nghen duoc goi ten cu the, khong noi chung chung
- [ ] Moi action item co owner va deadline
- [ ] So lieu da doi chieu voi nguon goc (Ads Manager / CRM / sheet ban hang)
- [ ] Khong co so nao trong bao cao ma nguoi viet khong giai thich duoc
