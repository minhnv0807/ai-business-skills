## 1. Quy trinh Content Production

```
IDEA → HOOK → MESSAGE → CTA → BRIEF → PRODUCTION → REVIEW → REPURPOSE → PUBLISH
```

Khong bo qua buoc BRIEF. Brief thieu la nguyen nhan so 1 cua viec phai lam lai asset.

## 2. Frameworks viet body

### PAS (Problem → Agitate → Solution)

- **P:** dat ten van de KH dang gap — dung ngon ngu cua KH, khong dung thuat ngu.
- **A:** khuech dai hau qua neu khong giai quyet.
- **S:** trinh bay giai phap ro rang.

### AIDA

- **A:** hook attention ngay dong dau.
- **I:** xay dung interest bang data / story / fact.
- **D:** tao desire bang benefit + proof.
- **A:** CTA ro rang o cuoi.

### BAB (Before → After → Bridge)

- **Before:** tinh huong hien tai cua KH.
- **After:** tinh huong sau khi dung giai phap.
- **Bridge:** san pham / dich vu la cau noi giua hai trang thai.

Chon framework: PAS cho tep nac 2-3, BAB cho social proof, AIDA cho content conversion.

### Sizing chuan 2026

| Platform | Format | Kich thuoc | Ty le |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook Feed | Post vuong | 1080x1080 | 1:1 |
| Instagram Post | Doc | 1080x1350 | 4:5 |
| Instagram Story / TikTok | Story | 1080x1920 | 9:16 |
| Banner web | Header | 1920x600 | ~3:1 |

### 5 dang bo cuc anh

| Dang | Mo ta | Dung cho |

## 5. Repurpose Framework — 1 thanh nhieu

**1 LIVESTREAM (60-90 phut):**
- 5-10 clip TikTok/Reels (30-60s)
- 3-5 quote → anh Threads/Facebook
- 1 thread text 10 diem
- 1 bai blog day du
- 1 email nurture tu noi dung chinh

**1 BLOG POST:**
- Thread 10-15 y
- Infographic tom tat
- Video giai thich
- Email newsletter

**1 CASE STUDY:**
- Post dai Facebook (Before/After)
- Carousel Instagram
- Video testimonial
- Quote → anh
- Email social proof

## 6. Checklist truoc khi publish

- [ ] Hook nam trong 3-5 giay dau (video) hoac dong dau tien (text)
- [ ] Message chinh khong bi loang boi thong tin phu
- [ ] CTA duy nhat, dung voi giai doan funnel
- [ ] Size dung platform, khong bi crop mat text
- [ ] Mau / font dung brand guideline
- [ ] Da chuan bi ke hoach repurpose cho asset nay
