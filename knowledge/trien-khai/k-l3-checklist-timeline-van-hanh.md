# K-L3 — Checklist & Timeline Van Hanh Marketing

> Cap nhat: 2026-07 | Tang: Strategy & Plan | Dung cho: Leader, Marketing Manager.
> SOP thuc thi lien quan: `skills/vi/02-brief-chien-dich`, `62-marketing-review`, `07-bao-cao-marketing`.

## 1. Checklist Marketing Activities la gi

**Dinh nghia:** tap hop cac hoat dong marketing se lam — tu hang muc chinh toi chi tiet.

**Cong thuc:**

```
He thong kenh + Ngan sach + Muc tieu/KPIs
  → Checklist Activities
    → Timeline trien khai
```

**Luu y:** checklist phai xay dung SAU KHI da xac dinh he thong kenh, ngan sach va nguon luc. Lam nguoc lai se ra danh sach viec khong kha thi.

## 2. Checklist theo 4 giai doan van hanh

### PLANNING — truoc khi trien khai

- [ ] Xay dung chan dung khach hang tu du lieu tho
- [ ] Thu thap tu nguon: comment, group, inbox, seeding, feedback
- [ ] Phan tich content & ads cua doi thu
- [ ] Xac dinh tep khach hang uu tien (SOM scoring)
- [ ] Xac dinh kenh chu luc phu hop voi hanh trinh KH

### EXECUTION — trong khi trien khai

**A/B test & toi uu ads:**
- [ ] Tim insight qua testing ngan sach thap truoc khi do tien lon
- [ ] Doc so Meta/TikTok danh gia hieu qua
- [ ] Loai bo content nhieu tuong tac nhung khong ra ket qua

**Phan tich hanh vi KH & toi uu pheu:**
- [ ] Phac hoa lai chan dung KH sau khi co data thuc te
- [ ] Tim "diem nghen" trong hanh trinh khach hang
- [ ] Phan khuc tep bang CRM hoac Google Sheet
- [ ] Toi uu tung buoc nho trong hanh trinh

**Phan tich hieu qua tung kenh:**
- [ ] Doc chi so tong quan chien dich hang tuan
- [ ] Phan tich hieu qua tung kenh (Ads / Seeding / Content / Email / Group)
- [ ] Tim content win → nhan ban
- [ ] Tim chien thuat win → scale

### REPORT — sau moi chu ky

- [ ] Tong hop data tu nhieu kenh vao 1 Google Sheet/Dashboard
- [ ] Dashboard cho lanh dao: ngan gon — ra quyet dinh nhanh
- [ ] Dashboard cho team: sau — bam sat cong viec
- [ ] Data report gon nhe theo tung chien dich

### OPTIMIZE — sau khi co data

- [ ] Du doan hieu qua neu tang/giam ngan sach
- [ ] Remarketing nguoi da mua / Retargeting nguoi da tuong tac
- [ ] Tan dung feedback de chay ads/seeding
- [ ] Toi uu noi dung theo nhom phan hoi

## 3. Timeline Campaign Phases

| Giai doan | Ten | Thoi gian | % Ngan sach | Muc tieu | KPI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Teasing | 1-2 tuan | 10-15% | Tang nhan dien, testing | CPM, Reach |
| Phase 2 | Bung nhe | 2-3 tuan | 20-25% | Thu lead, toi uu vong 1 | CPL, CPMess |
| Phase 3 | Bung manh | 2-4 tuan | 40-50% | Scale ngan sach, tang toc | ROAS, CPO |
| Phase 4 | Duy tri | 1-2 tuan | 15-20% | Vet dich, retarget | CVR retarget |

Khong nhay tu Phase 1 sang Phase 3. Moi phase phai co ket luan (creative nao win, audience nao win) truoc khi tang ngan sach.

## 4. Weekly Review Framework

**Moi thu Hai — Marketing Weekly Review:**

1. Ket qua tuan truoc vs KPI: dat / khong dat
2. Top 3 dieu hoc duoc
3. Top 3 dieu can dieu chinh
4. Priority tuan nay

**Format bao cao tuan (doc trong 3 phut):**

```
KET QUA TUAN [N]
- Doanh thu: [X] | Target: [Y] | %: [Z%]
- Leads: [X] | CPL: [Y]
- ROAS: [X]

INSIGHT CHINH
- [Dieu work tot nhat]
- [Dieu can cai thien]

TUAN TOI
- [Priority 1]
- [Priority 2]
```

## 5. Checklist Leader hang tuan

**Ve Performance:**
- [ ] ROAS tong vs target
- [ ] CPL hien tai vs benchmark
- [ ] Ti le chot don (Hot Lead → Customer)

**Ve Content:**
- [ ] So bai dang vs ke hoach
- [ ] Content nao dang work

**Ve Team:**
- [ ] Task nao dang bi block?
- [ ] Can quyet dinh gi tu leadership?
