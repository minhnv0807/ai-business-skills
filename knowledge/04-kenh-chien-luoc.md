# Chien Luoc Kenh & 4 Tieu Chi Chon Kenh

> Cap nhat: 2026-07 | Kien thuc nen tang — tang tu duy. SOP thuc thi xem `skills/vi/11-thiet-lap-kenh`.

## Phan 1: REACH vs CONVERT — khac nhau goc re

### Sai lam: focus 1 kenh duy nhat
"Chung toi chay TikTok" → dung TikTok vua loi traffic vua chot don.

**Van de:** TikTok reach cao nhung conversion path dai. Khong the dung cung 1 kenh lam 2 viec khac nhau.

### Tu duy dung

| Loai kenh | Vai tro | Hanh dong | Benchmark | Vi du |
|-----------|---------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| **REACH** | Loi traffic, tang awareness | Video hook, trend, giai tri, educate | 1-3% conversion sang click | TikTok, Reels, Instagram |
| **CONVERT** | Chot don, keo decision | Offer, urgency, CTA ro, testimonial | 5-20% conversion sang purchase | Zalo OA, Website, Email, Inbox |

**Ket luan:** he thong kenh phai co **ca REACH va CONVERT** — kenh reach loi traffic, kenh convert chot tien.

## Phan 2: 4 tieu chi chon kenh chu luc

### Tieu chi 1: Khach hang tap trung o kenh nay khong?

Cach kiem chung:
- Hoi khach hien tai: "Ban biet chung toi tu dau?"
- Check Instagram/Facebook Insights → audience demographics.
- Theo doi doi thu → ho chay kenh nao.

Vi du: neu 70% khach tu Facebook → Facebook la uu tien.

### Tieu chi 2: Doanh nghiep co loi the thuc su khong?

- Sai: "Chung toi chay TikTok vi trend."
- Dung: "Chung toi chay TikTok vi team quay video tot / founder co charisma."

Cach danh gia loi the:
- **Content skill:** quay video tot hay viet bai tot?
- **Founder appeal:** founder co personality de xay FGC khong?
- **San pham visual:** san pham co "photogenic" khong?

Neu khong co loi the → chon kenh khac noi ban de thang hon.

### Tieu chi 3: Co du nguon luc duy tri khong?

Tinh toan:
- 1 bai TikTok = 2-4 tieng (quay + edit).
- Can 5 bai/tuan = 10-20 tieng/tuan = gan 1 FTE.
- Team 2 nguoi khong the chay 2-3 kenh chu luc dong thoi.

**Quy tac:** team 1 nguoi chay tot 1 kenh > team 3 nguoi chay binh binh 3 kenh.

### Tieu chi 4 (nang cao): Conversion Capability — path tu content den chot don

- Sai: "Kenh nao co khach minh chay do."
- Dung: "Path tu content → interest → lead → chot don ngan khong?"

Vi du:
- **TikTok path:** video → khong CTA truc tiep → follower → DM → inbox → tu van → chuyen Zalo → chot = **7 buoc, 2-3 tuan**.
- **Zalo OA path:** Zalo message → offer truc tiep → chot = **2 buoc, 1 ngay**.

Ket luan: TikTok = REACH tot, conversion path dai. Zalo OA = reach thap, conversion path ngan. Phai co **ca hai** trong he thong.

## Phan 3: 3 option chien luoc kenh

### Option 1: Social + Founder (TikTok/Reels + FGC)

- **Khi chon:** team co loi the quay video, founder co charisma, khach tre.
- **Cau truc:** chu luc TikTok brand (60% effort) + FGC founder (25%) + ho tro Zalo OA (15%).
- **Uu:** reach cao, FGC trusted, khong can budget quang cao lon.
- **Nhuoc:** content production ton thoi gian, conversion path dai, can sales team.
- **Budget tham khao:** 5 trieu/thang Ads + 5-8 trieu/thang team = 10-13 trieu.

### Option 2: SEO + Content Hub

- **Khi chon:** content high-value, khach search Google, B2B, dich vu.
- **Cau truc:** chu luc Blog + SEO (60%) + ho tro email nurture (25%) + organic social mentions (15%).
- **Uu:** long-term, organic traffic tang dan, lead quality cao.
- **Nhuoc:** ROI cham (6-12 thang moi thay ket qua), can noi dung chat luong cao.
- **Budget tham khao:** 3-5 trieu/thang content + SEO tools = 5-8 trieu.

### Option 3: Community-Led Growth

- **Khi chon:** brand co trust cao, product word-of-mouth, cong dong manh.
- **Cau truc:** chu luc Facebook Group + nurture (60%) + ho tro email + referral (25%) + organic word-of-mouth (15%).
- **Uu:** lead quality cao, LTV cao, cost per lead thap.
- **Nhuoc:** rat cham, can community manager, khong scale nhanh.
- **Budget tham khao:** 2-3 trieu/thang community management = 2-5 trieu.

## Phan 4: He thong kenh day du theo tang pheu

### TOFU — kenh loi traffic

- **UGC Network:** khach tao & share.
- **FGC Network:** founder personal (TikTok, FB ca nhan).
- **Kenh ve tinh:** TikTok brand, Instagram Reels.
- **Ads boost:** paid promotion khi organic khong du.

KPI vi du: reach 50K/tuan, CPM 40K.

### MOFU — kenh xay trust & nurture

- **Website/Blog:** kien thuc chuyen sau.
- **Email:** nurture sequence, offer.
- **Zalo OA:** broadcast, Q&A.
- **Messenger:** chat, advice.

KPI vi du: 125 lead/thang @ CPL 24K.

### BOFU — kenh chot don

- **Landing page:** offer + CTA ro.
- **Zalo chot:** personal message, negotiation.
- **Messenger:** chat sales.
- **Livestream ban:** real-time, urgency.

KPI vi du: 50 don/thang, close rate 40%.

## Phan 5: Campaign phases & budget allocation

| Phase | Muc tieu | Duration | Budget | Audience |
|-------|----------|----------|--------|----------|
| **Teasing** | Launch, awareness | Tuan 1-2 | 10-20% | Cold + Warm |
| **Bung nhe** | Test creative, lead gen | Tuan 2-3 | 20-30% | Warm + engaged |
| **Bung manh** | Scale, maximize lead | Tuan 4-6 | 40-50% | Proven audience |
| **Duy tri** | Keep brand warm, retarget | Tuan 7+ | 10-20% | Engaged, cart abandons |

## Tom tat

Chien luoc kenh khong chon theo "trend" ma theo 4 tieu chi: khach o dau, loi the gi, nguon luc bao nhieu, conversion path bao lau. Phai co kenh REACH (loi traffic) + kenh CONVERT (chot tien). 1 kenh khong the lam ca 2 viec.
