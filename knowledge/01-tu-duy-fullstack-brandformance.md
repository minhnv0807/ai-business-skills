# Tu Duy Fullstack Marketing & Brandformance

> Cap nhat: 2026-07 | Kien thuc nen tang — tang tu duy. SOP thuc thi xem `skills/vi/`.

## Phan 1: Benh cot loi cua SME marketing Viet Nam

5 trieu chung ma 80% SME mac phai:

1. **Dang bai theo cam hung** — khong theo ke hoach, khong theo strategy, khong do luong.
2. **Khong biet kenh nao ra tien** — chay Ads 50 trieu/thang nhung khong track duoc CPL, ROAS, hay lead tu dau.
3. **Dan trai qua nhieu kenh** — team 2-3 nguoi ma chay 5-6 kenh cung luc, chat luong tut doc, khong ai responsible.
4. **Khong co pheu chuyen doi** — traffic co nhung khong biet chuyen thanh lead/customer nhu the nao.
5. **Thieu he thong do luong** — lam marketing roi khong biet KPI la gi, target la gi, dat hay khong.

**Ket qua:** chi phi marketing tang +30-40%/nam nhung doanh thu khong tang tuong xung. Team met, CEO that vong.

## Phan 2: Fullstack Marketing — tu day ky nang sang tu duy he thong

### Dinh nghia sai (tranh nham)
"Fullstack" KHONG bang biet tat ca (content, ads, code, design, analytics).

### Dinh nghia dung
**Fullstack Marketing = co tu duy he thong** ap dung xuyen suot 3 tru cot:

```
PLAN → EXECUTE → ANALYZE → OPTIMIZE
   |       |         |         |
CONTENT  CONTENT  CONTENT  CONTENT
PERFORM  PERFORM  PERFORM  PERFORM
STRATEG  STRATEG  STRATEG  STRATEG
```

- **Content:** noi dung xuat ban, lich content, content pillar, nguon content (UGC/EGC/FGC).
- **Performance:** ads, CPL, ROAS, lead gen, retargeting, media plan.
- **Strategy:** market research, positioning, offer, funnel design, KPI setting.

Moi quy/thang phai di qua vong lap nay it nhat 2 lan. Do moi goi la Fullstack.

## Phan 3: Brandformance — khong phai "brand roi moi ads"

### Sai lam pho bien
- "Chung toi lam brand" → chay content, xay uy tin, khong chot don.
- "Khi da co brand roi thi chay ads" → chot don.

**Ket qua:** mat 6-12 thang "xay brand" ma doanh thu khong ghi nhan, co dong tuc toi, campaign bi cat ngan sach.

### Tu duy dung — Brandformance
**Moi content / moi ads phai vua xay brand VUA keo hanh vi chuyen doi cung luc.**

- Content CO ban — nhung ban bang cach: tao niem tin → giao duc → giai quyet → dan dat hanh vi (khong hard-sell).
- Content KHONG ban truc tiep — khach khong thay "Mua ngay" hay "Discount 50%" o buoc 1. Ban bang hanh trinh 6 buoc.
- Muc tieu cua Brandformance: **tang vua awareness vua conversion cung luc** — tu cung 1 bai content/campaign.

### Cong thuc

```
Content/Ads = Brand message (xay trust, tao nhan thuc)
            + Offer message (tao urgency, dan hanh dong)
            + Data/Proof (case study, ket qua that)
```

## Phan 4: Tai sao AARRR kho ap dung cho SME Viet Nam

### AARRR la gi
Awareness → Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Revenue → Referral.

Framework nay sinh ra cho SaaS, noi:
- User tu sign up (khong can tu van vien).
- User tu dung (khong can sales).
- User tu tra tien qua app (khong can phone call).

### Thuc te SME Viet Nam (beauty, dich vu, coaching)
- Khach can **inbox hoi** — mot nguoi ban hang phai tra loi 100+ tin nhan/ngay.
- Khach can **tu van vien chot** — khong the tu dong.
- Khach can **remarketing nhieu lan** — 1 lan cham khong du.
- Khach mua **theo quan he, theo loi khuyen**, khong phai theo conversion rate.

**Ket luan:** dung AARRR lam SME confuse vi framework khong map duoc vao thuc te kinh doanh cua ho.

## Phan 5: Nguyen tac vang cua Fullstack Marketing

### 1. Commit vao 1 framework duy nhat
Khong xen ke AARRR + Funnel 6 buoc + TOFU/MOFU/BOFU cung luc — se confuse.

**Chon framework:** dung **Funnel 6 buoc** (Tiep can → Dung lai → Theo doi → Tin tuong → Dan ve → Chot don) — vi no map 100% vao hanh vi khach thuc te.

### 2. Plan tu doanh thu, khong tu kenh
- Sai: "Chung toi chay TikTok, Facebook, Zalo, Email, SEO."
- Dung: "Chung toi can 300 trieu doanh thu → 50 khach x 6 trieu AOV → 40% conversion = 125 lead → CPL 24K → budget 3 trieu."

### 3. Do luong tu tung tang pheu, khong chi doanh thu
- Sai: "KPI cua thang la 500 trieu."
- Dung: "KPI TOFU: 50K reach, CPM 40K | KPI MOFU: 200 lead, CPL 24K | KPI BOFU: 50 don, CPA 120K."

### 4. Content tao nen 70% ket qua, Ads tao 30%
Neu content yeu, Ads manh co nao cung khong cuu duoc. Nguoc lai, content tot, Ads yeu van co ket qua.

## Phan 6: Kenh REACH vs kenh CONVERT — sai lam pho bien

### Sai lam
"Chung toi focus 1 kenh duy nhat — TikTok."

Van de: TikTok la kenh REACH (loi traffic) nhung convert capability yeu (path tu video den chot don rat dai).

### Tu duy dung
He thong kenh phai co **kenh REACH + kenh CONVERT** cung luc:

| Loai kenh | Vai tro | Vi du | Hanh dong chinh |
|-----------|---------|-------|-----------------|
| **REACH** | Loi traffic, tang awareness | TikTok, Facebook Reels, Instagram | Video hook, trending, giai tri |
| **CONVERT** | Chot don, keo decision | Zalo OA, Website, Inbox, Email | Offer, urgency, CTA ro rang |

**Vi du:** kenh TikTok keo traffic 100K/thang → convert 10-15% = 10-15K click sang Zalo OA → chot 5-10% = 500-1500 don.

## Phan 7: 4 tieu chi chon kenh chu luc

### Tieu chi 1: Khach hang tap trung o kenh nay khong?
Vi du: khach nu 25-40 tap trung tren Facebook Reels va TikTok → TikTok/Facebook la uu tien.

### Tieu chi 2: Doanh nghiep co loi the thuc su khong?
- Sai: "Chung toi chay TikTok vi trend."
- Dung: "Chung toi chay TikTok vi team minh quay video tot, co sang tao, hoac founder co charisma."

Neu khong co loi the, chon kenh khac de thang hon.

### Tieu chi 3: Co du nguon luc duy tri khong?
Team 2 nguoi → khong the chay 3 kenh. Chon 1, chay on dinh tot hon chay 3 nhung be.

### Tieu chi 4 (nang cao): Conversion Capability — path tu content den chot don ngan khong?

Vi du:
- **TikTok:** video → khong co CTA website → follow/DM → inbox → tu van → Zalo → chot don = **7 buoc, 2-3 tuan**.
- **Zalo OA:** nhan message → offer truc tiep → chot ngay = **2 buoc, 1 ngay**.

**Ket luan:** TikTok reach cao nhung conversion path dai. Zalo OA reach thap nhung conversion path ngan. Phai co **ca hai** trong he thong.

## Phan 8: Mau thuan noi tai thuong gap (can lam ro)

### Mau thuan 1: "Content khong ban" vs "Vua xay brand vua ban"
- Content **khong ban cung** = khong noi "Mua ngay, discount 50%".
- Content **ban mem** = tao niem tin, goi nhu cau, dan dat theo hanh trinh.
- **Brandformance** = ket hop 2 cach: tao brand trust tu content + ban bang offer + he thong phia sau.

### Mau thuan 2: "Focus 1 kenh" vs "1 kenh chinh + 1 ho tro + 1 nuoi brand"
- **Giai doan 0-1 (validate):** 1 kenh duy nhat, khong chia suc.
- **Giai doan 1-10 (scale):** 1 kenh chinh (60% effort) + 1 ho tro (25% effort) + 1 nuoi brand (15% effort).

Neu team 2-3 nguoi van o giai doan 0-1, **dung** chay 3 kenh.

## Tom tat

Fullstack Marketing khong phai la biet tat ca. No la **tu duy he thong**: tu muc tieu doanh thu, thiet ke pheu → chon kenh → san xuat content → chay ads → do luong → toi uu → lap lai. Brandformance la triet ly: **moi hanh dong marketing vua xay brand vua keo conversion** — cung luc, cung bai content.
