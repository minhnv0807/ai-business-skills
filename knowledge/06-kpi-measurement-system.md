## Phan 1: KPI nguoc tu doanh thu (reverse KPI)

### Cong thuc tinh

```
Doanh thu muc tieu (250 trieu/thang)
  / AOV trung binh (5 trieu)
  = Khach can (50 khach)
  / Conversion rate (40%)
  = Lead can (125 lead)
  x CPL benchmark (24K)
  = Budget can (3 trieu)
```

### 3 scenario

| Scenario | Conv rate | Lead | Budget | Ghi chu |
|----------|-----------|------|--------|---------|
| **Tot nhat** | 50% | 100 | 2.4M | Neu moi thu dep |
| **Thuc te** | 40% | 125 | 3.0M | Ke hoach co ban |
| **Te nhat** | 30% | 166 | 4.0M | Du phong |

Lap plan voi **scenario thuc te**, du phong cho te nhat.

## Phan 2: KPI theo tung tang pheu

### TOFU (Tiep can — Awareness)

| KPI | Benchmark | Action |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| **Reach** | 50K/tuan (vi du) | Duoi 30K → tang budget |
| **CPM** | 30-60K | >80K → thay creative |
| **CTR** | 0.8-2% | <0.5% → creative yeu |
| **Video completion** | 50%+ | <30% → video qua dai |

### MOFU (Tuong tac — Lead Gen)

| KPI | Benchmark | Target |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| **Lead/tuan** | — | 30-35 lead/tuan (vi du) |
| **CPL** | 12-24K (nganh AOV thap) | <20K scale, >25K toi uu |
| **Qualify rate** | 40-60% | Bao nhieu lead du dieu kien |
| **Cost per qualified lead** | CPL / qualify rate | Gia thuc te cua 1 lead chat |

### BOFU (Chuyen doi — Sale)

| KPI | Benchmark | Target |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| **CPA** | CPL / close rate | Tuy nganh |
| **Close rate** | 30-50% | Bao nhieu lead → khach |
| **ROAS** | 3-5x | Doanh thu / spend |
| **AOV** | Tuy nganh (3-6M clinic) | Moi khach chi trung binh bao nhieu |

### Retention

| KPI | Target |
|-----|--------|
| **Re-purchase rate** | 20%+ quay lai thang 2-3 |
| **LTV** | Gap 3x CAC |
| **Referral rate** | % khach gioi thieu ban be |

## Phan 5: Decision framework — weekly review (moi thu 2)

**Buoc 1: Check CPL**
- CPL < 20K? → Giu & co the scale +20%.
- CPL 20-25K? → Theo doi.
- CPL > 25K? → Optimize hoac pause.
(Nguong tren la vi du cho CPL target ~20K — chinh theo nganh.)

**Buoc 2: Check Frequency**
- Frequency < 2.0? → Fresh, giu.
- Frequency 2.0-2.5? → Start fatigue, test creative moi.
- Frequency > 2.5? → Pause creative nay.

**Buoc 3: Check Conversion**
- Close rate tang vs tuan truoc? → Good.
- Close rate giam? → Sales issue (khong phai Ads).

**Buoc 4: Quyet dinh**
- Tang budget campaign nao (scale winners).
- Toi uu campaign nao (change creative).
- Pause campaign nao (CPL qua cao).

## Phan 6: Cac loi do luong pho bien

### Loi 1: Chi track lead, khong track sale
- **Van de:** 1000 lead nhung 10 khach → khong biet issue o dau (Ads hay Sales).
- **Fix:** track full funnel: impression → lead → sale → repeat purchase.

### Loi 2: Benchmark voi nganh, khong benchmark voi chinh minh
- **Van de:** CPL 25K (nganh benchmark 15K) → panic, tat campaign.
- **Giai:** CPL ban thang truoc la 30K → 25K da tot hon, giu.

### Loi 3: KPI qua nhieu
- **Van de:** track 50 chi so → confused, khong biet action nao.
- **Fix:** 3-4 KPI chinh + 5-6 KPI supporting. Cu don gian.

### Loi 4: Dashboard khong day len shared place
- **Van de:** CEO khong thay, khong co context → quyet dinh sai.
- **Fix:** weekly dashboard share voi CEO/stakeholders.

## Tom tat

KPI khong phai "so dep" — ma la **tin hieu ra quyet dinh**. Tinh nguoc tu doanh thu de biet budget realistic. Do luong tung tang pheu, khong chi doanh thu chung chung. Benchmark la guide, quan trong nhat la trend cua chinh minh.
