# Pheu 6 Buoc & He Thong Noi Dung

> Cap nhat: 2026-07 | Kien thuc nen tang — tang tu duy. SOP thuc thi xem `skills/vi/01-lich-noi-dung`, `skills/vi/04-script-video`.

## Phan 1: Pheu 6 buoc — hanh trinh khach hang

### Buoc 1: Tiep can — nguoi la thay noi dung lan dau

- **Hanh dong khach:** scrolling, thuat toan day noi dung sang. Khach chua biet ban.
- **Nhiem vu:** content phai **stop-the-scroll** — thu gi trong 0.5 giay khien ho dung lai.
- **Do bang:** Reach, Impression.
- **Benchmark:** CPM 30-60K (beauty), 1-3% nguoi thay → click.

### Buoc 2: Dung lai — noi dung cham dung noi dau

- **Hanh dong khach:** dung lai, xem het hoac vai giay, doc tieu de, xem hinh.
- **Cach cham dung noi dau:**
  - Kien thuc sai: "Ban dang hieu sai ve da minh"
  - FOMO: "90% khach den day truoc do deu lam sai"
  - Ket qua that: "Nam giam sau 8 tuan — hinh truoc/sau"
  - Giai tri: trend, funny, relatable
- **Do bang:** Watch time, Video completion rate, Time on page.
- **Benchmark:** completion rate 50%+ la tot (beauty/coaching).

### Buoc 3: Theo doi — nhan follow de xem them

- **Hanh dong khach:** "Page/channel nay hay, follow de xem tiep."
- **Nhiem vu:**
  - Dang deu (lich content dung).
  - Tra loi comment/DM trong 2-4 gio dau (tang algorithm).
  - Tao thoi quen cho follower (moi T2 la insight, T4 la case study).
- **Do bang:** follower moi/tuan, follow rate. **Target:** +50-200 follower/thang (organic).

### Buoc 4: Tin tuong — tuong tac sau, luu bai, nhan tin hoi

- **Hanh dong khach:** luu bai doc lai, nhan tin rieng hoi them, comment, tag ban be, share story/group.
- **Day la tin hieu chin muoi nhat — khach dang suy tinh mua.**
- **Nhiem vu:**
  - Tra loi DM nhanh (trong 1 gio neu co the).
  - Noi dung phai co gia tri thuc su (khong clickbait).
  - Xay vi the (khach phai thay ban biet gi, khong phai "sales" suot).
- **Do bang:** DM reply rate, Save rate, Share rate, Comment quality.

### Buoc 5: Dan ve — chuyen tu kenh ca nhan sang diem chot don

**Buoc BI BO QUA NHIEU NHAT** — khach "thich ma khong mua" vi khong biet buoc tiep theo la gi.

- **Diem chot don co the la:** website/landing page, Zalo OA, email, inbox rieng, nhom Facebook private.
- **Cac hinh thuc dan:**
  - "Click link o bio xem chi tiet"
  - "Reply HELLO de nhan phac do"
  - "Inbox minh de duoc tu van mien phi"
  - "Scan QR de vao Zalo OA"
- **Do bang:** CTR, cost per click, conversion rate. **Benchmark:** CTR 0.5-2% la tot (khi da co trust du).

### Buoc 6: Chot don — mua hang, dat lich, ky hop dong

- **Chu y:** gan ten kenh hoac nguoi ban voi don hang de sau nay biet kenh nao sinh tien.
- **Do bang:** Revenue, so don, AOV, CPA, close rate. **Benchmark:** close rate 30-50% (lead tu ads).

## Phan 2: 4 nguon noi dung (UGC / EGC / FGC / BGC)

### UGC — User Generated Content (khach hang tao)

- **Uu diem:** tin cay cao nhat (nguoi that, ket qua that); reach tot (algorithm uu tien UGC); cost thap.
- **Dung o tang pheu:** tiep can, nuoi duong, chuyen doi (review).
- **Cach keo UGC:** contest "share story cua ban"; tang voucher khach tot nhat de quay video; tao hashtag, yeu cau tag.
- **Benchmark:** ~1/20 khach san sang tao UGC. Team 2 nguoi → 1 bai UGC tot/tuan la thanh cong.

### EGC — Expert/Employee Generated Content (chuyen gia/nhan vien tao)

- **Uu diem:** kiem soat duoc chat luong; xay vi the chuyen gia (credibility); reach on dinh.
- **Dung o tang pheu:** tuong tac (MOFU), nuoi duong.
- **Format:** carousel giao duc, video huong dan tung buoc, bai long-form chuyen sau.
- **Benchmark:** team 1 nguoi content → 2 bai EGC/tuan.

### FGC — Founder Generated Content (ca nhan founder)

- **Uu diem:**
  - **Reach cao nhat** — thuat toan 2026 uu tien content ca nhan gap 5-10x fanpage.
  - **Trust manh nhat** — khach tin nguoi that hon logo.
  - **Loi the SME** — tap doan lon khong co founder authentic nhu vay.
- **Dung o tang pheu:** tiep can, tuong tac, nuoi duong.
- **Format:** hau truong, cau chuyen ca nhan, Q&A truc tiep, insight nganh, livestream.
- **Data 2026:** employee advocacy = reach gap 5.61x, lead gap 7x so voi fanpage chinh thuc.
- **Benchmark:** founder danh 5-10 tieng/tuan cho FGC = 2-3 bai/tuan.

### BGC — Brand Generated Content (fanpage chinh thuc)

- **Uu diem:** kiem soat 100% brand message; luu tru permanent.
- **Nhuoc diem:** reach thap — duoi 2% follower (2026); can Ads bo tro.
- **Dung o tang pheu:** chuyen doi (BOFU), thong bao chinh thuc.
- **Benchmark:** BGC = ~20% noi dung, can Ads. Khong nen chi chay BGC.

## Phan 3: 4 content pillar

| Pillar | Muc dich | Ti trong | Buoc pheu | Nguon phu hop |
|--------|----------|----------|-----------|---------------|
| **Thu hut** | Dua nguoi moi vao | 30% | Tiep can + Tuong tac | FGC + UGC |
| **Chuyen mon** | Xay vi the | 30% | Tuong tac + Tin tuong | EGC + FGC |
| **Chuyen doi** | Ra don | 25% | Nuoi duong + Chuyen doi | UGC + BGC |
| **Ket noi** | Xay quan he | 15% | Giu chan + Repeat | FGC + EGC |

### 3 quy tac vang

1. **Khong 2 bai chuyen doi lien tiep** — khach met moi, mute account.
2. **Moi bai ban can 2 bai gia tri di truoc** — xay trust roi moi ban.
3. **Cung chu de, khac format qua kenh** — Instagram carousel, TikTok video, email newsletter (content tai su dung).

## Phan 4: Truc noi dung (content pillar design)

**Cong thuc:** Truc noi dung = Muc tieu truyen thong x Van de khach hang x The manh thuong hieu.

Vi du:
- Muc tieu: "Tang reach tu 0 → 50K/thang"
- Van de KH: "Khong biet da minh bi gi, so bi lua"
- The manh: "Bac si co chung chi quoc te, minh bach gia"

→ **Noi dung xoay quanh:** "Kien thuc skincare dung" + "Chung minh chuyen gia" + "Mo ta minh bach quy trinh".

**KHONG nen:** toan claim "Mun het 100%", "Cong nghe dan dau the gioi" (chung chung, fake-looking).

## Phan 5: AI content 2026 — hieu qua thuc te

| Loai | Hieu qua | Giai thich |
|------|----------|-----------|
| **100% con nguoi** | Tot | Authentic, reach tot, trust cao |
| **AI-assisted (nguoi + AI)** | Tot, ngang 100% nguoi | AI ho tro outline, nguoi viet & edit → van authentic |
| **100% AI** | Bi han che | Thuat toan phat hien, giam reach 30-50%, khach thay "mui AI" |

**Ket luan:** dung AI de **ho tro** (brainstorm, outline, draft) nhung nguoi **phai chinh sua & them giong rieng** truoc khi dang.

## Tom tat

Pheu 6 buoc la hanh trinh hop ly cua khach. He thong noi dung la cong cu dan khach qua tung buoc. 4 nguon content + 4 pillar + lich noi dung = he thong hoan chinh. Khong co thi quay lai "dang bai theo cam hung" — va ket qua khong ra tien.
