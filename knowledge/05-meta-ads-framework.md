# Meta Ads Framework — Van Hanh Paid Ads Dung Cach

> Cap nhat: 2026-07 | Kien thuc nen tang — tang tu duy. SOP thuc thi xem `skills/vi/21-audit-ads-performance`, `skills/vi/05-copy-quang-cao`.

## Phan 1: Campaign structure (TOFU/MOFU/BOFU)

### TOFU — Traffic / Awareness
- **Muc tieu:** loi traffic tu cold audience.
- **Audience:** lanh hoac am (video views, engagement 1-3 thang).
- **Creative:** hook manh, stop-the-scroll, educate, entertain.
- **KPI chinh:** CPM, CTR (0.8-2% la tot).
- **Budget split:** 30-40% tong budget.

### MOFU — Lead Gen
- **Muc tieu:** chot lead (email, phone, inbox).
- **Audience:** am (engaged, interacted 7-30 ngay).
- **Creative:** educate + offer, goi hung thu, giai phap.
- **KPI chinh:** CPL, lead quality.
- **Benchmark:** 12-24K VND/lead (nganh AOV thap), 80-200K (clinic/dich vu AOV cao).
- **Budget split:** 35-45% tong budget.

### BOFU / Retarget — Conversion
- **Muc tieu:** chot don tu warm audience.
- **Audience:** nong (website visitors, add-to-cart, engaged 1-7 ngay).
- **Creative:** urgency, proof, testimonial, uu dai.
- **KPI chinh:** CPA, ROAS.
- **Budget split:** 15-25% tong budget.

## Phan 2: Creative formula thuc chien

### Hero creative pattern

```
[Specific Identity] + [Pain/FOMO] + [Named Tool/Method] + [Free CTA]
```

**Vi du thuc chien (nganh dao tao marketing):**
"Marketer SME + dang bi AI thay the + Claude/ChatGPT + nhan tai lieu mien phi"
→ ket qua thuc te mot chien dich lead magnet: CPL 11K, 272 leads, frequency 1.87 (on dinh, chua fatigue).

### Khong nen
- Claim chung chung ("Hieu qua 100%", "Cong nghe dan dau").
- Qua ban (BOFU tu lan dau tiep xuc).
- Bo CTA ro rang.

### Nen
- Cu the + giai quyet pain.
- Proof bang case study/ket qua that.
- CTA clear: "Click link", "Inbox", "Reply".

## Phan 3: CPL benchmark tiers (decision rules)

### Benchmark CPL theo nganh VN 2025

| Nganh | CPL trung binh | Target scale |
|-------|----------------|--------------|
| **F&B** | 15-40K | <20K |
| **Fashion** | 30-80K | <50K |
| **Beauty/Spa** | 80-200K | <100K |
| **Clinic** | 150-300K | <200K |
| **Giao duc** | 50-150K | <80K |
| **B2B** | 200-600K | <400K |

### Decision rules hang tuan (vi du nganh dich vu local, CPL target ~15K — can chinh theo AOV)

| CPL | Frequency | Action |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| **<12K** | <2.0 | WIN — scale ngay 20-30% |
| **12-15K** | <2.0 | Acceptable — theo doi |
| **15-18K** | 2.0-2.5 | Optimize — thay creative |
| **>18K** | >2.5 | PAUSE — replace hoac stop |

Nguyen tac ap dung: lay CPL target cua nganh minh lam moc, chia tiers theo ti le tuong tu (duoi 80% target = win, 80-100% = theo doi, 100-120% = optimize, tren 120% = pause).

## Phan 4: Creative fatigue & frequency management

Frequency = so lan trung binh 1 nguoi thay ad trong ky.

| Frequency | Trang thai | CTR trend | Hanh dong |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| <1.5 | Fresh | Tang | Giu, co the scale |
| 1.5-2.0 | Toi uu | Stable | Theo doi |
| 2.0-2.5 | Fatigue bat dau | Giam | Test creative moi |
| >2.5 | Fatigue nang | Xuong manh | Dung, replace creative |

Cach tranh fatigue:
- Rotate 3-4 creative hang tuan.
- A/B test 2 message khac nhau.
- Refresh copy, hinh anh, hook video.

## Phan 6: Cac loi pho bien khi chay ads

### Loi 1: Chay BOFU tu lan dau tiep xuc
- **Van de:** conversion rate 0.1%, CPA rat cao.
- **Fix:** TOFU 3 tuan truoc, xay trust, roi moi chay BOFU.

### Loi 2: Dung cung 1 creative qua lau
- **Van de:** frequency cao, CTR giam, CPL tang.
- **Fix:** rotate creative hang tuan, A/B test.

### Loi 3: Khong track conversion, chi track lead
- **Van de:** lead nhieu nhung chot it, khong biet nguyen nhan.
- **Fix:** setup pixel, track full funnel: impression → lead → sale.

### Loi 4: Campaign bi cat ngan sach giua chung
- **Van de:** learning phase bi gian doan, campaign khong toi uu.
- **Fix:** giu minimum daily budget >= (daily lead target x CPL).

## Tom tat

Meta Ads la cong cu de scale traffic. Nhung content yeu, Ads manh co nao cung khong cuu. TOFU/MOFU/BOFU khac nhau goc re — khong the dung 1 creative cho ca 3. Frequency la signal — khi frequency cao, creative dang met, can thay. CPL benchmark la guide, nhung quan trong nhat la so voi chinh account ban thang truoc.
