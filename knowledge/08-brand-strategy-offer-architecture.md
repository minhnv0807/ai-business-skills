## Phan 1: Brand architecture — quyet dinh nen tang

### Bai toan cap do 1: ten thuong hieu duy nhat

- **Sai:** 3 ten song song (vi du: X Spa, X Beauty, X Clinic) tren cung thi truong → CPM cao, CTR thap, customer confuse.
- **Dung:** 1 ten chinh + co the chia nhanh dich vu ben duoi → brand recognition tang, cost quang cao giam.

### Single Brand vs Portfolio

**Single Brand (1 ten chinh)**
- Vi du: 1 clinic gom ca dieu tri + cham soc.
- Loi: nhan dien manh, chi phi marketing thap.
- Nhuoc: moi dich vu anh huong credibility cua ten chung.
- Khi chon: start-up, 1-2 dich vu.

**Portfolio Brand (nhieu sub-brand)**
- Vi du: Unilever (Omo, Lipton, Hellmann's).
- Loi: moi dich vu rieng positioning.
- Nhuoc: ton budget marketing gap doi.
- Khi chon: tap doan, nhieu dong san pham khac nhau.

**Khuyen cao:** SME nen dung Single Brand trong 2-3 nam dau.

## Phan 2: Dinh vi (Positioning) — cong thuc Promise + Proof + Path

### Promise (hua gi)
Cai ban cam ket, dua tren customer insight.

Vi du (clinic da lieu):
- Sai: "Lam dep hieu qua".
- Dung: "Giup ban hieu chinh xac lan da cua minh truoc khi ton tien sai cach".

### Proof (chung minh bang)
Cach ban chung minh loi hua la that:
- Case study cu the (anh truoc/sau that).
- Bac si co chung chi.
- Khach review 5 sao.
- Gia ro rang, khong hidden charge.

### Path (dan duong nhu the nao)
Khach se trai qua gi de nhan duoc promise:
1. Soi da + chan doan (mien phi).
2. Phac do dieu tri ro rang.
3. Dieu tri theo phac do.
4. Ket qua sau 8 tuan.

## Phan 3: Key message (thong diep then chot) — 3 cau hoi

- **Q1: Ai la khach muc tieu?** Vi du: nu 25-40, ban ron, so bi lua.
- **Q2: Van de chinh cua ho?** Khong biet da minh bi gi, so chi tien sai cach.
- **Q3: Tai sao chon chung toi, khong phai doi thu?** Vi chung toi minh bach, chan doan cu the, khong ep mua.

## Phan 4: Offer architecture (cau truc san pham)

### 4 tang offer

| Tang | Ten | Gia | AOV (vi du) | Muc dich |
|------|-----|-----|-------------|----------|
| **Entry** | Offer de tiep can | Gia thap | 300-600K | Cua vao |
| **Core** | Offer chinh | Gia trung | 3-6M | Nguon doanh thu |
| **Flagship** | Offer cao cap | Gia cao | 10-50M | LTV cao |
| **Retention** | Goi duy tri | Gia subscription | 500K-2M/thang | Giu khach |

### Nguyen tac tung tang

**Entry offer:**
- Du re de quyet dinh nhanh (1-2 ngay).
- Du tot de thay doi perception (khach thay ket qua).
- **Khong phai "cau khach"** — phai la real value.

**Core offer:**
- Nguon doanh thu chinh.
- Chot tai diem tiep xuc (tai co so, sau buoi tu van/soi da).
- Khong chot qua ads hoac inbox chi bang offer (convert te).

**Flagship offer:**
- Khach da tin tuong sau.
- Cam ket van ban, phac do/lo trinh 4-16 tuan.
- Gia cao nhung LTV cao vi repeat + referral.

**Retention:**
- Goi thang sau khi dung dich vu chinh.
- Giu khach khong "boc hoi" sau khi van de da on.
- Tang LTV, tao referral loop.

## Phan 5: Brand personality & tone

5 chieu kich brand personality:

| Chieu | The hien | Vi du |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Chuyen mon ↔ Gan gui** | Formal vs Casual | Bac si chuyen nhung vui tinh, khong "cao lanh" |
| **Premium ↔ Binh dan** | Luxury vs Accessible | Gia cao nhung khong xa cach |
| **Giao duc ↔ Giai tri** | Educational vs Fun | Viet bai ky thuat nhung khong boring |
| **Trust ↔ Innovation** | Conservative vs Bold | Phuong phap proven nhung luon update cong nghe |
| **Me-focused ↔ Community-focused** | Self vs Others | "Toi" vs "Chung ta" trong message |

Vi du profile (clinic da lieu): chuyen mon 80/gan gui 20; premium 70/binh dan 30; giao duc 60/giai tri 40.

Cach dung: cham diem tung chieu theo ti le %, ghi vao brand guideline, ap dung nhat quan cho moi content va creative.

## Phan 6: Positioning map (danh gia vi tri)

```
           TRUST
             ^
             |
   [Brand A] *   [Clinic X]
             |
  PREMIUM ---+---> BINH DAN
             |
        [Spa Y]
             |
             v
          GIAI TRI
```

Cach doc:
- Brand A: Premium + Trust → co hoi mo: "binh dan nhung tin tuong" (chua ai chiem).
- Clinic X: Premium + Trust → direct competitor, can khac biet bang proof/path.
- Spa Y: Binh dan + Giai tri → khac target, khong phai doi thu truc tiep.

Cach lam: chon 2 truc quan trong nhat voi khach (vi du gia va trust), cham vi tri minh + 3-5 doi thu, tim o trong chua ai chiem.

## Tom tat

Brand strategy bat dau tu quyet dinh **ten thuong hieu duy nhat**. Sau do la **Promise + Proof + Path** — hua gi, chung minh bang gi, dan khach the nao. Offer architecture la cach ban **ban**: entry re de thu → core de kiem tien → flagship de tao LTV cao → retention de giu khach. Tat ca phai aligned voi insight khach.
