## 7. Nguong canh bao va nguong tat

Bang 4 muc o tren dung de danh gia; bang nay dung de **ra quyet dinh dung/tiep**:

| Chi so | Canh bao — soat lai | Phai tat / thay |
|--------|---------------------|-----------------|
| CPMess | >60K | >100K |
| CTR | <0.8% | <0.5% |
| ROAS | <1.5x | <1x |
| CPL (giao duc) | >250K | >400K |
| Frequency | >3.5 | >5.0 |

### Decision rules theo target (chinh xac hon nguong tuyet doi)

Nguong tuyet doi chi dung khi chua co target rieng. Khi da co target tu `10-tinh-kpi-nguoc`, dung quy tac tuong doi:

| Hanh dong | Dieu kien |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Scale** (+20-30%) | CPL thap hon target >=20% trong 3 ngay lien tiep, HOAC ROAS > target x1.3 trong 3 ngay |
| **Pause** | CPL cao hon target >=50% trong 2 ngay, HOAC frequency >3.5 kem CTR giam >30%, HOAC ROAS <1x sau khi da chi >=5tr |
| **Ket luan test** | Sau >=30 conversion hoac >=7 ngay — cai nao den truoc. Budget test 50–100K/ngay, chay toi thieu 3 ngay |

Chi tiet van hanh: `55-scaling-ads` (scale), `21-audit-ads-performance` (chan doan), `19-ab-test-setup` (test).

## Ghi chu

- So lieu la **trung vi** (median), khong phai trung binh — loai tru outlier.
- Bien dong theo mua: Tet (+30–50% CPM), Black Friday (+20–30% CPM), he (+10–15%).
- CPMess thay doi theo nganh, dia ly, va chat luong creative — dung so lieu nay lam **diem xuat phat**, khong phai muc tieu tuyet doi.
- **Trend cua chinh account quan trong hon benchmark nganh.** Benchmark de biet minh dang o dau; xu huong 4 tuan gan nhat cua chinh minh moi la can cu ra quyet dinh.
- Benchmark theo muc gia san pham (vi du dao tao/dich vu): san pham <2tr → CPL 80–150K, CPO 400–800K, ROAS 3–5x; san pham 2–5tr → CPL 150–300K, CPO 800K–1.5tr, ROAS 2–4x; consulting 1:1 → CPL 200–500K; free lead magnet → CPL 20–60K. Day la **vi du minh hoa** tu du lieu van hanh mot don vi cu the — doi chieu voi so cua chinh ban truoc khi dung lam target.
- Cap nhat benchmark moi 6 thang.
