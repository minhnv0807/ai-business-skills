# Thu Vien Template Van Hanh Marketing

> Chi muc CAU TRUC cua 28 template van hanh marketing (xlsx/docx thuc chien, da chuan hoa ten va bo attribution).
> Muc dich: AI doc file nay la du de tu xuat bang/sheet/cot dung chuan — khong can mo file goc.
> Cach dung: xac dinh nhom → doc "Sheet & cot chinh" → goi skill o muc "Skill tai tao" → xuat markdown table hoac CSV theo bo cot chuan o Muc 7.

---

## 0. Quy uoc chung xuat hien trong hau het template

- **Ma mau o:** vang = nguoi dung dien · xanh = cong thuc tu tinh · xam = benchmark/co dinh khong sua. Khi xuat markdown, ghi chu `[dien]`, `[tu tinh]`, `[benchmark]` sau ten cot.
- **Trang thai task:** `Chua` · `Dang lam` · `Xong` · `Blocked`. Uu tien: `Cao` · `TB` · `Thap`. Trang thai content: `Draft` · `Review` · `Da duyet` · `Live`.
- **Danh gia dat KPI:** Dat (>=90%) · Gan dat (70-89%) · Chua dat (<70%). De xuat kenh: Scale ↑ · Giu → · Giam ↓ · Kill ✕ · Test moi ★.
- **Nhip dien:** target dien 1 lan dau ky · so thuc te dien moi thu 6 · flag do khi lech >20% target.
- **Ke thua:** moi tang ke thua tang tren (nam → quy → thang → tuan). Khong nhap lai ICP/positioning/tone o tang duoi — copy tu tang tren.

## 1. Planning nam · quy · theo kenh

### 1.1 Ke hoach marketing nam

- **Muc dich:** khung chien luoc nam — muc tieu, tru cot, ngan sach, KPI, lich chien dich, nhip van hanh.
- **Khi nao dung:** 1 lan/nam, truoc ky kinh doanh moi; la nguon ke thua cho moi ke hoach quy.
- **Sheet & cot chinh:** `Tong quan` (Muc tieu | KPI do | Con so cu the | Baseline | Thoi han | Trang thai; Tru cot | Mo ta | Quy trien khai | Owner | KPI thanh cong) · `Nen tang` (ICP, cau dinh vi, UVP <15 chu, tone of voice, kenh uu tien — copy tu audit) · `Brandformance` (Vai tro | Nguoi | Kenh phu trach | KPI chinh) · `Ngan sach nam` (Hang muc | % KH | So tien | Q1-Q4) · `Lich chien dich` (Ten | Big idea | Kenh | KPI | Budget | Owner | Trang thai) · `KPI tracker` (3 nhom: Brand health / Revenue & efficiency / Customer value, cot Target nam + T1..T12) · `Nhip van hanh` (Khi nao | Hoat dong | Noi dung | Ai tham gia | Thoi luong).
- **Quy tac trong template:** chon 3-4 tru cot, moi quy tap trung 1-2. Chia target theo quy Q1 20% · Q2 25% · Q3 30% · Q4 25%. Du phong ngan sach giu co dinh 10%. Lech target >20% giua quy → dieu chinh ngay. North Star = Revenue x Brand equity; ROAS toi thieu 3x; MER = tong revenue / tong chi phi MKT.
- **Skill tai tao:** `00-ke-hoach-mkt` (chinh) + `61-budget-planning` + `10-tinh-kpi-nguoc` + `58-positioning`.

### 1.2 Ke hoach marketing theo quy

- **Muc dich:** dich muc tieu nam thanh 3 thang co lich tuan, chien dich va ngan sach cu the.
- **Khi nao dung:** 4 lan/nam, dau moi quy; ~80% noi dung ke thua tu ke hoach nam.
- **Sheet & cot chinh:** `Tong quan quy` (Theme quy 1 cau; Muc tieu | KPI | Target quy | Baseline quy truoc | Tang truong; Dieu chinh tu quy truoc: kenh tang NS / kenh cat / thong diep doi) · `Nen tang` (ke thua) · `Chien dich quy` (3 block: Ten | Big idea/Hook | Tru cot gan voi | Timeline | Kenh & Budget | KPI) · `Ngan sach quy` (Kenh | % phan bo | So tien | T1 | T2 | T3) · `Lich 3 thang` (T1 Build · T2 Push · T3 Convert, moi thang 4 tuan: Tuan | Brand lam gi | Performance lam gi | Output can co | KPI tuan | Budget) · `KPI tracker quy`.
- **Quy tac trong template:** target thang = target quy / 3. Mid-quarter check: lech >20% → tang paid / doi creative / check bottleneck. Giu 10% du phong.
- **Skill tai tao:** `00-ke-hoach-mkt` + `61-budget-planning` + `10-tinh-kpi-nguoc` + `54-media-plan`.

### 1.3 Ke hoach tong quan + ngan sach nam (ban tinh CAC)

- **Muc dich:** tinh nguoc tu doanh thu muc tieu ra so khach, so lead, CAC toi da va ngan sach thang.
- **Khi nao dung:** dau nam, khi can chung minh con so cho ban lanh dao.
- **Sheet & cot chinh:** `Muc tieu & KPI` (Doanh thu nam truoc | Doanh thu muc tieu | Chi phi | Ti le chi phi/doanh thu | AOV | So khach hang | % tang truong; ti le chuyen doi Order/SQL, SQL/Lead; bang 12 thang: Doanh so | % tang truong MoM | Chi phi | So khach | SQL | Lead) · `Ngan sach` (chi phi co dinh: CPC, GDN, Facebook, SEO, hoi thao, email, social; chi phi phat sinh: KOL, quan ly KOL, qua tang; retainer: chuyen gia PPC/SEO, thiet ke, chien luoc, bao tri web, tool; dong Tong thang | Gioi han thang | Chenh lech) · `Marketing tong quan` (Danh muc | Chien dich | Hoat dong | Thang 1-12, cay danh muc: Tiep thi tra phi / Content / Automation / Offline) · `Marketing thang` (Chien dich | Hoat dong | Dau viec | Nguoi phu trach | Deadline | Tinh trang | Tuan 1-4 | KPI | Ket qua thuc te).
- **Quy tac trong template:** tach ngan sach thanh 2 khoi — "chi de co khach hang" (conversion) vs "xay dung thuong hieu" (branding), vi du split 75-85% / 15-25%. CAC toi da = doanh thu TB/khach x ti suat sinh loi. So lead can = so khach moi / ti le chuyen doi.
- **Skill tai tao:** `10-tinh-kpi-nguoc` (chinh) + `61-budget-planning` + `00-ke-hoach-mkt`.

### 1.4 Ke hoach marketing san pham moi

- **Muc dich:** brief ra mat san pham/dich vu moi — tu SWOT san pham den ngan sach kenh.
- **Khi nao dung:** truoc launch san pham moi 4-8 tuan.
- **Sheet & cot chinh:** `Ke hoach tong quan` 10 phan — I Thong tin san pham (Ma SP | Ten | Gia ban | Tinh nang | SWOT) · II Thi truong muc tieu · III Doi thu canh tranh (doi thu hang dau | diem manh | so sanh gia | so sanh tep KH) · IV Chan dung khach hang (nhan khau hoc + insight: moi quan tam, noi dau + kenh thong tin su dung) · V Muc tieu #1-3 · VI Thong diep (Big Idea | Slogan | mo ta) · VII Chien luoc tiep thi · VIII Kenh tiep thi (Nen tang | Mo ta | KPI) · IX Dinh huong noi dung (Loai | Mo ta | So luong) · X Ngan sach · `Quan ly noi dung` · `Theo doi KPI` (Tong bai dang | Luot tim kiem tu nhien/tra phi | Luot hien thi | Lead | Chuyen doi | Doanh thu).
- **Skill tai tao:** `59-go-to-market` + `60-launch-playbook` + `02-brief-chien-dich` + `31-offer-design`.

### 1.5 Ke hoach email marketing

- **Muc dich:** lap chien dich email, ghi log gui va do KPI theo cong thuc co dinh.
- **Khi nao dung:** moi chien dich email/nurture; KPI doc cuoi thang.
- **Sheet & cot chinh:** `Noi dung chien dich` (Ten | Thoi gian | Tep data gui | So luong data | Dac diem | Muc tieu | Tan suat gui; bang Noi dung gui | Ma chien dich | Hinh anh | Link dinh kem | Trang thai) · `Quan ly chien dich` (Ngay gui | Gio gui | Ma chien dich | Tep gui | Tieu de | Noi dung | Link | Da gui | Luot mo | Khong mo | Luot click | Huy dang ky | Bi tra lai) · `Theo doi KPI` (Chi so | Thuc te | Muc tieu | % KPI | Danh gia).
- **Quy tac trong template:** ma chien dich dang `T1.CD1.CT1` (thang.chien dich.content). Sau moi chien dich xoa lead bounced + unsubscribed; sau 2 tuan loc theo open de ra tep chat luong; bo sung email moi 1 lan/thang. Cong thuc: Open = mo/gui thanh cong · Click = click URL/gui thanh cong · Unsub = huy/gui thanh cong · Bounce = loi/gui thanh cong. Nguong VN thuc te lay tu `benchmarks-vietnam.md`, khong dung target 50% open cua template goc.
- **Skill tai tao:** `14-email-marketing` + `49-html-email-template` + `10-tinh-kpi-nguoc`.

### 1.6 Bang du kien kinh doanh — tai chinh & dong tien theo kenh

- **Muc dich:** tinh loi nhuan/ROI/von xoay vong cua 1 kenh ban truoc khi rot ngan sach.
- **Khi nao dung:** truoc khi mo kenh moi (livestream, san TMDT, ban FB, F&B giao hang).
- **Sheet & cot chinh:** 1 sheet/kenh, cot = 4 kich ban so don/ngay (vi du 30/50/100/200 don). Dong co dinh: Doanh so | Thue GTGT | Phi san | Phi extra | Gia von | Hoa hong nhan vien | Chi phi nhan su | Chi phi dau tu | Chi phi/don hang | Ads | Chi phi that thu (hoan don) | Chi phi dong goi | Tong chi phi | Loi nhuan. Khoi phu: von dau tu, dong tien quang cao, tong von 1 thang, so vong xoay von, ROI.
- **Quy tac trong template:** moi kenh mot bo ti le rieng (gia von 35-55%, ads 10-28%, hoan don 3-18%, phi san 6-7%). Day la bang DU KIEN — bat buoc hieu chinh theo nganh hang truoc khi dung.
- **Skill tai tao:** `17-pricing-strategy` + `61-budget-planning` + `10-tinh-kpi-nguoc`.

## 2. Campaign execution

### 2.1 Ke hoach trien khai chien dich chi tiet (Build → Push → Convert)

- **Muc dich:** chay 1 chien dich 3 thang voi phan cong theo ngay giua Brand / Performance / CRM.
- **Khi nao dung:** chien dich lon co nhieu doi phoi hop, chay lien tuc 12 tuan.
- **Sheet & cot chinh:** `Brief chien dich` (Ten | Quy/Thang | Tru cot | Owner | Ngay bat dau/ket thuc | Budget tong | Budget paid; ke thua Big idea, ICP, pain point, positioning, tone; Hook 1-3 + loai hook Pain/Curiosity/Result; DO 1-4 va DON'T 1-4; bang asset: Asset | Kenh dung | Kich thuoc/Format | So luong | Deadline | Owner | Trang thai) · `Ke hoach 3 thang` (Hang muc | Brand lam gi | Performance lam gi | CRM lam gi | KPI do | Output phai co) · `Lich tuan T1/T2/T3` (Ngay | Thu | Brand | Performance | CRM | Output | KPI check) · `Tracking tuan` (KPI | Target | T1..T12, nhom Brand / Performance / Funnel / CRM) · `Danh gia thang` · `Budget tracker` (Kenh | Budget plan | T1-T3 actual | Budget con | ROAS avg).
- **Quy tac trong template:** T1 Build = setup, launch, test creative, keo awareness · T2 Push = scale hook win, tat hook thua, toi uu CPL · T3 Convert = chot don, retarget warm, upsell. Nguong can thiep: CPL tang >50% vs tuan truoc → check creative/audience/landing ngay · ROAS <1.5x → pause kenh, chuyen budget sang kenh >3x · CTR giam >30% vs tuan 1 → creative fatigue, thay ngay · Lead rate <1% → sua landing page. Danh gia thang = 5 cau hoi bat buoc, toi da 3 thay doi cho thang sau.
- **Skill tai tao:** `02-brief-chien-dich` (chinh) + `41-campaign-asset-list` + `54-media-plan` + `61-budget-planning` + `63-campaign-retrospective`.

### 2.2 Campaign playbook mua cao diem

- **Muc dich:** chien dich mua vu co ngay cao diem co dinh — nang ve insight, offer va kich ban truoc khi chay.
- **Khi nao dung:** chien dich theo mua (he, Tet, back-to-school), chuan bi truoc 60 ngay.
- **Sheet & cot chinh:** `Campaign Brief` (Big idea 1 cau | Offer chinh | Nhom khach uu tien | OMTM tuan nay | Kich ban dang theo; MOC KHONG THE DOI: Moc | Deadline | Nguoi chiu TN | Trang thai | He qua neu tre; BACKUP PLAN: Tinh huong xau | Dau hieu | Hanh dong 24h | Ai quyet dinh) · `Insight & KH` (cau hoi tho | ket qua tho dan nguyen van | insight 1 cau | dung vao dau) · `Offer & Thong diep` (Offer Canvas 3 tang: huu hinh / cau truc / tam ly; cham diem 4 tieu chi x 5 diem, tong <15 → lam lai offer) · `Muc tieu` (3 kich ban x1.5 / x2.5 / x4; dong: doanh thu, AOV, so don, ti le chot, lead can, CVR ads, traffic, CPL, ngan sach ads, ROAS toi thieu) · `Timeline` (T-60 chien luoc → T-45 san xuat → T-30 test → T-14 lam nong → cao diem → giu chan) · `Kenh` (Kenh | Vai tro | Loai noi dung | Tan suat | Chi so do | Nguong dung) · `Ngan sach` · `Content Plan` (lich 14 ngay: Ngay | Tang | Hook 3 giay | Loai ND | CTA | Kenh | Gio dang) · `Funnel Tracking` · `Content Performance` · `Ads Tracking` · `Weekly Review` · `Tong ket`.
- **Quy tac trong template:** offer quyet dinh 60-70% kha nang chot. Ngan sach theo duong cong V: Tuan 1 test 15% · Tuan 2 toi uu 25% · Tuan 3 peak 40% (quy tac cung, khong cat) · Tuan 4 giu chan 20%. Phan bo trong chien dich: ra don 60% · remarketing 25% · thu nghiem 15%. Quyet dinh ads: ROAS >3x + CTR >1.5% → tang 20-30%/ngay · ROAS 2-3x → giu them 2 ngay · ROAS <2x hoac CTR <1% sau 3 ngay → kill ngay. Ngay 1-7 test da dang, ngay 8-14 chi scale bai thang; bai kem sau 48h → dung. Weekly review chi duoc ra 1 quyet dinh duy nhat. Tong ket khong muon hon 48h sau khi ket thuc.
- **Skill tai tao:** `02-brief-chien-dich` + `31-offer-design` + `09-insight-khach-hang` + `55-scaling-ads` + `63-campaign-retrospective`.

### 2.3 Campaign plan gon (1 chien dich, 3 sheet)

## 3. Tracking & Dashboard

### 3.1 Dashboard tracking theo pheu (organic + paid, da kenh)

- **Muc dich:** 1 file duy nhat theo doi tu lead don le den pheu thang va so sanh hieu qua kenh.
- **Khi nao dung:** lien tuc — Lead Log dien hang ngay, Weekly KPI dien hang tuan (~15 phut).
- **Sheet & cot chinh:** `Config` (KPI muc tieu thang, gross margin, break-even ROAS = 1/margin, danh sach kenh: Ten kenh | Loai paid/organic | Ads platform) · `Lead Log` (ID | Ngay vao | Ho ten | So dien thoai | Kenh nguon | Loai tuong tac | Trang thai | Ly do khong chot | Doanh thu neu chot | Sales phu trach) — sheet quan trong nhat, moi dong 1 lead · `Weekly KPI` (pheu theo thang: Reach | Tong leads | Organic leads | Paid leads | Organic lead ratio | Qualified leads | Qualify rate | Khach chot | Close rate | Doanh thu | Ads spend | Blended ROAS | CAC | Repeat rate; nhap tu Ads Manager: Kenh | Spend | Impressions | Clicks | CTR | CPM | Leads | CPL | Q.Leads | ROAS) · `Pheu tong quan` (Tang pheu | Thuc te | KPI muc tieu | Dat % | vs thang truoc | Trang thai) · `Content da kenh` (Kenh | Followers | Reach | Impressions | Like+Cmt+Share+Save | ER% | CTR% | Follower growth%; tracking video: Views | Watch time TB | Completion rate | Shares | Saves; email: Campaign | Ngay gui | Danh sach | Gui thanh cong | Open | CTR | Unsub | Doanh thu) · `Performance Ads` (CPL | CPQL | CAC | ROAS | Break-even ROAS theo kenh; lich su 6 thang) · `Trend Chart`.
- **Quy tac trong template:** hoi "biet qua kenh nao?" voi moi inbox/cuoc goi — khong co Lead Log thi khong co attribution. Mau: xanh la = dat KPI · cam = 80-99% · do = chua dat.
- **Skill tai tao:** `13-phan-tich-du-lieu` (chinh) + `53-tracking-setup` + `21-audit-ads-performance` + `03-danh-gia-hieu-suat`.

### 3.2 Tracking hieu qua 12 tuan da phong ban

- **Muc dich:** theo doi song song Content · Ads · CRM · Automation · Sales trong 1 chu ky chien dich 12 tuan.
- **Khi nao dung:** dien moi thu 6, 30 phut, kem review tuan.
- **Sheet & cot chinh:** `Dashboard` (Hang muc | KPI chinh | Target thang | Thuc te | Dat % | Xu huong ↑↓→ | Trang thai Tot/Canh bao/Kem; canh bao tuan: KPI lech >20%? kenh/creative can thay? bottleneck o dau? dieu chinh can approve?) · `Content` (nhom: Organic reach & awareness / Engagement / Video performance / SEO & Blog / Content production; cot KPI | Target | Benchmark | T1..T12) · `Ads` (Performance tong / Meta / Google / TikTok / Creative performance / Landing page & CVR) · `CRM` (Lead pipeline / Email nurture / Sales handoff / Chuyen doi & doanh thu / Retention & LTV) · `Automation` (Trigger performance / Workflow nurture / Email automation / Health check) · `Sales support` (Chat luong lead / Sales pipeline / Feedback loop Sales→Marketing / Sales enablement) · `Review tuan` (12 tuan, moi tuan 6 cau).
- **Quy tac trong template:** nguong noi trong bang — CPL tang >50% → thay creative ngay · ROAS <1.5x → pause kenh · MER <2x → xem lai toan chien luoc · frequency >3.5 → audience saturation · CTR Meta <1% → creative kem · Quality Score <5 → landing page co van de · CVR landing <1% → LP co van de · form submit rate <50% → form qua dai · handoff sales <4h, ti le trong 4h >90%, lead bi drop = 0 · trigger fire rate >99%, form submit → email D0 <5 phut. Review tuan gioi han toi da 3 thay doi.
- **Skill tai tao:** `13-phan-tich-du-lieu` + `62-marketing-review` + `21-audit-ads-performance` + `39-content-audit`.

### 3.3 Bo metrics organic (brand health · doanh thu · organic vs paid)

## 7. Bo cot chuan de skill xuat CSV / markdown table

Ba bang duoi day la dang chuan hoa cua toan bo template tren. Moi skill khi xuat bang phai dung dung ten cot va dung thu tu nay de output cua cac skill khac nhau ghep duoc voi nhau.

### 7.1 Campaign tracker — 1 dong = 1 tuan cua chien dich

```csv
Tuan,Giai doan,Ngan sach KH,Chi tieu thuc te,Luy ke,% Budget da dung,Reach,Click,Lead,CPL,Don,Doanh thu,ROAS,Creative win,Quyet dinh
```

| Cot | Y nghia / quy tac |
|-----|-------------------|
| Tuan | `T1W1` … `T3W4` — thang + tuan trong thang |
| Giai doan | `Build` · `Push` · `Convert` (hoac Test · Toi uu · Peak · Giu chan voi chien dich 4 tuan) |
| Ngan sach KH | So ke hoach dat tu dau ky, khong sua giua chung |
| Chi tieu thuc te / Luy ke | Dien moi thu 6; luy ke de phat hien overspend som |
| % Budget da dung | `Luy ke / tong budget` — canh bao khi lech >20% so voi % thoi gian da chay |
| CPL | Tang >50% vs tuan truoc → check creative, audience, landing ngay |
| ROAS | <1.5x → pause kenh, chuyen budget sang kenh >3x |
| Creative win | Ten/ID creative co CTR cao hon phan con lai >20% |
| Quyet dinh | Chi 1 quyet dinh/tuan: `Scale ↑` · `Giu →` · `Giam ↓` · `Kill ✕` · `Test ★` |

### 7.2 Content calendar — 1 dong = 1 bai/video

```csv
Ngay,Gio dang,Kenh,Pillar,Angle,Tieu de,Hook,Format,Tang pheu,CTA,Nguoi lam,Deadline san xuat,Trang thai,Link da dang,Reach,ER%,Danh gia
```

| Cot | Y nghia / quy tac |
|-----|-------------------|
| Pillar | Thuoc bo 3-5 pillar da chot; tong ti le pillar phai bang 100% |
| Angle | Goc trien khai cu the trong pillar (vi du: storytelling, tips, khach hang chung thuc) |
| Hook | 3 giay dau / dong dau — bat buoc, khong de trong |
| Format | `Video ngan` · `Carousel` · `Single image` · `Story` · `UGC` · `Long-form` |
| Tang pheu | `TOFU` · `MOFU` · `BOFU` |
| Trang thai | `Draft` → `Review` → `Da duyet` → `Live` |
| Deadline san xuat | Brief truoc 5-7 ngay; review truoc dang 24h |
| Reach · ER% · Danh gia | Dien sau khi dang 48-72h; `Thang` → scale, `Kem` → dung |

### 7.3 KPI dashboard — 1 dong = 1 chi so

```csv
Nhom,Chi so,Cong thuc,Nguon so,Tan suat,Baseline,Target,Thuc te,% Dat,Benchmark,Xu huong,Trang thai,Hanh dong
```

| Cot | Y nghia / quy tac |
|-----|-------------------|
| Nhom | `Brand` · `Content` · `Ads` · `Funnel` · `CRM` · `Sales` · `Tai chinh` |
| Cong thuc | Viet ro cach tinh, vi du `ER = (like+cmt+share+save)/reach` — khong de trong |
| Nguon so | Ten platform/sheet lay so, de nguoi khac lap lai duoc |
| Tan suat | `Ngay` · `Tuan` · `Thang` · `Quy` |
| % Dat | `Thuc te / Target` |
| Benchmark | Lay tu `benchmarks-vietnam.md` (VN) hoac `benchmarks-global.md` (Global) — khong tu dat |
| Xu huong | `↑` · `→` · `↓` so voi ky truoc |
| Trang thai | `Dat` (>=90%) · `Gan dat` (70-89%) · `Chua dat` (<70%) |
| Hanh dong | Bat buoc dien khi trang thai `Chua dat`; 1 hanh dong cu the + nguoi chiu trach nhiem |
