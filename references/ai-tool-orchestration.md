# Phoi Hop AI Tool Cho Marketer

> Quy trinh dung nhieu cong cu AI de lap va van hanh ke hoach marketing — ai lam gi, theo thu tu nao, luu o dau.
> Viet theo VAI TRO cong cu, khong khoa vao thuong hieu cu the: moi ten tool ben duoi deu co the thay bang cong cu tuong duong.
> Nguyen tac goc: AI khuech dai chat luong tu duy dau vao — thong tin dau vao kem thi output kem, khong tool nao cuu duoc.

---

## 1. Vai tro tung tool

| Vai tro | Tool dai dien (hoac tuong duong) | Manh nhat o | KHONG dung de | Deliverable giao cho |
|---------|----------------------------------|-------------|---------------|----------------------|
| **Ideation & san xuat nhanh** | ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok | 20-30 caption/lan · 20 y tuong trong 2 phut · tim trend & hashtag tuan nay · dich nhanh · anh reference/mood board | Phan tich sau, quyet dinh chien luoc, giu ngu canh brand qua nhieu phien | Caption bien the, hook list, y tuong, trend brief, anh tham khao |
| **Long-form · phan tich · he thong** | Claude (Projects, Skills, Artifacts, File) | Ke hoach quy/nam co tinh nguoc KPI · phan tich data ads tim nguyen nhan goc · viet SOP/checklist · review content hang loat theo brand voice · dashboard tuong tac | Sinh 30 caption trong 10 phut, tao hinh anh, hoi trend tuan nay | Ke hoach, brief, SOP, bao cao, phan tich funnel, dashboard |
| **Doc nguon & tai lieu co trich dan** | NotebookLM, hoac RAG/notebook tuong duong | Rut insight tu bao cao 50-100 trang · gop 5-10 tai lieu thanh 1 ban tom tat · onboarding nguoi moi tu SOP · tong hop feedback khach hang | Sang tao noi dung moi, lap ke hoach, suy luan ngoai pham vi tai lieu da upload | Insight report, tom tat nghien cuu, Q&A noi bo |
| **Design & video** | Cong cu design/AI image, cong cu dung video | Visual thuc te, chinh sua theo brand kit | Thay the brief — van phai co brief tu tang phan tich | Asset cuoi cung |
| **Data & quang cao** | Ads Manager, GA4, CRM, sheet, ket noi MCP/connector | Nguon so goc, export CSV | Khong de AI tu bia so khi chua co export | So lieu dau vao cho phan tich |

**Quy tac chon tool:** bat dau tu tool don gian nhat du giai quyet viec can lam. Output cua tool nay la input cua tool tiep theo. Khong phai viec nao cung can du ca 3 tang — mot cuoc chat don gian doi khi la du.

**Neu chi duoc dung 1 tool:** tool ideation → nhanh nhung ke hoach hoi hot va mat ngu canh giua cac phien · tool long-form → ke hoach tot va nhat quan nhung san xuat hang loat cham, khong co anh, khong biet trend · tool doc nguon → tong hop tai lieu rat tot nhung khong viet duoc noi dung, khong lap ke hoach.

## 2. Quy trinh lap ke hoach marketing voi AI

Ba tang, chin buoc. Tang 1-2 lam 1-2 lan/nam; tang 3 lam moi quy hoac moi chien dich.

### Tang 1 — Nghien cuu

**B1. Nghien cuu thi truong & khach hang.**
Input: bao cao nganh, review, inbox, comment, data ban hang noi bo. Lam: tool ideation uoc luong quy mo thi truong (chi la con so tham khao, khong phai su that) → tool long-form phan tich do khop van de-giai phap tu review dan vao → tool doc nguon tong hop 3 cau hoi insight cot loi. Gate: moi insight phai truy nguoc ve mot cau noi that cua khach hang, khong tu nghi ra.

**B2. Nghien cuu doi thu.**
Chi lam SAU khi da xac dinh vung khach hang kha thi va do khop san pham. Lam: bang doi thu 3 tang (truc tiep / gian tiep gan / gian tiep xa) + positioning map, tu thu vien quang cao, social listening, review tieu cuc cua doi thu. Gate: muc tieu la tim khoang trong doi thu bo lai — khong sao chep doi thu.

### Tang 2 — Chien luoc (danh it nhat 45-60 phut, khong lam nhanh)

**B3. Phan khuc → chan dung → dinh vi → hanh trinh khach hang → he thong kenh.**
Lam: chia 3 phan khuc → chot 1-2 persona uu tien → cau dinh vi → hanh trinh 4-5 giai doan → gan kenh theo vai tro. Gate: moi kenh 1 vai tro (keo traffic / chot don / giu khach), toi da 3 kenh. Chay 5-6 kenh cung luc thi khong kenh nao du nguon luc.

### Tang 3 — Ke hoach chi tiet

**B4. Muc tieu & KPI — tinh nguoc.**
Bat dau tu doanh thu muc tieu, khong bat dau tu "nen chay kenh nao". Cong thuc va vi du trong `references/kpi-formulas.md`. Gate: toi da 3 KPI, kem nguong canh bao cho tung KPI. Chua tung trien khai thi khong dat KPI qua chi tiet.

**B5. Kenh & ngan sach.**
Split tham khao 70-20-10 (kenh da chung minh / mo rong / test). Gate: moi kenh phai co nguong cat lo ghi san truoc khi chay, khong quyet dinh trong luc dang lo.

**B6. Ke hoach content & performance.**
Lam: tool ideation ra 3 pillar + 10 angle (5 TOFU + 3 MOFU + 2 BOFU) + 5 hook → tool long-form dung lich 4 tuan kem quy tac scale/kill. Gate: dung bo cot chuan content calendar trong `references/marketing-templates-library.md`.

**B7. Nghiem thu & phuong an du phong.**
Buoc bi bo qua nhieu nhat. Lam: moc nghiem thu theo tuan (15% - 40% - 70% - 100% target thang), bang 4 kich ban ket qua, va 3 kich ban rui ro kem hanh dong 24h. Gate: ke hoach khong co phuong an du phong se bi bo xo ngay lan dau so lieu xau.

**B8. Timeline · phan cong · tracking.**
Lam: timeline 30-90 ngay chia 3 pha (chuan bi/test tuan 1-2 → toi uu tuan 3-6 → scale tuan 7-12), task board theo nguoi, lich review. Dashboard chi theo doi 5 chi so hang ngay — khong nhieu hon, khong it hon.

**B9. Tong hop 1 trang.**
Gop tat ca thanh ke hoach 1 trang. Chuan chat luong: nguoi ra quyet dinh doc 15 phut la hieu. Phan thieu du lieu phai ghi ro "Can bo sung: [...]" thay vi dien so doan.

### Nhip van hanh sau khi launch (~90 phut/tuan cho team 2-5 nguoi)

| Khi nao | Thoi luong | Lam gi |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| Thu 2 sang | 30 phut | Doc so tuan truoc, tool long-form phan tich, chot uu tien tuan |
| Thu 3-5 | 45 phut | San xuat noi dung bang tool ideation, review lai bang tool long-form theo brand voice |
| Thu 6 chieu | 15 phut | Doi chieu voi moc nghiem thu B7, luu ket qua vao kho tai lieu |

**Ky luat doc so:** ads can it nhat 3 ngay hoc va toi thieu 500 luot tiep can moi nhom quang cao truoc khi ket luan. Ngay 1-3 chi xem CPM/CTR/frequency. Ngay 4-7 xem CPL. Tuan 2-3 xem ROAS/CR, khong tang ngan sach qua 20% moi lan. Frequency >2.5 la dau hieu creative fatigue.

## 3. Dung Project cho team marketing

Mo hinh Project (Claude Projects hoac khong gian lam viec tuong duong co bo nho dung chung) gom 3 lop:

**1. Custom instruction — vai tro & luat tra loi.** Noi ro AI dong vai tro gi cho thuong hieu nao; bat buoc doc Brand Hub, persona va Campaign Log truoc khi tra loi; luat output: tieng Viet, uu tien bang/bullet thay vi doan van dai, moi de xuat phai kem so lieu va nguon, thieu thong tin thi hoi chu khong doan, va **khong duoc bia so lieu neu khong co trong knowledge file**.

**2. Skill files — quy trinh chuan.** Dat cac SKILL.md hay dung nhat vao Project de moi nguoi trong team goi ra output cung dinh dang. Khi output cua skill sai, sua custom instruction hoac knowledge file — **khong sua skill**.

**3. Knowledge files — bo nho thuong hieu.**

| File | Bat buoc? | Cap nhat khi nao |
|------|-----------|------------------|
| Brand Hub / Brand Brief (dinh vi, USP, tone, do/don't) | Bat buoc | Khi dinh vi hoac thong diep thay doi |
| Chan dung khach hang / persona | Bat buoc | Sau moi 2-3 chien dich |
| Campaign Log — KPI cu, cai gi thang, cai gi thua | Bat buoc | Sau moi chien dich |
| Bao cao nghien cuu thi truong & doi thu | Bat buoc | Moi quy hoac khi thi truong bien dong |
| SOP & quy trinh team | Khuyen nghi | Khi doi nhan su hoac doi quy trinh |
| Benchmark nganh dang dung | Khuyen nghi | Moi 6 thang |

**Gioi han thuc te:** ~20 file/Project. Vuot nguong nay chat luong truy xuat giam va team khong con biet file nao la ban that. Uu tien skill va tai lieu dung hang tuan; tai lieu tra cuu it dung thi de o kho ngoai va upload theo phien.

**Quy tac Brand Hub — 1 ban that, day di khap noi.** Brand Hub la file goc duy nhat. Khi Brand Hub doi (dinh vi, tone, USP, do/don't, benchmark), phai day ban moi vao **tat ca** Project dang chay cua thuong hieu do trong cung ngay, va ghi phien ban + ngay cap nhat ngay dau file. Moi Project la mot ban sao doc lap — khong tu dong dong bo. Neu team quan ly nhieu thuong hieu: moi thuong hieu 1 Project rieng, dat ten `[Ten thuong hieu] — Marketing [Nam]`, khong dung chung 1 Project cho nhieu brand.

**Quy uoc dat ten knowledge file:** `brand-hub.md` · `persona-[phan-khuc].md` · `campaign-log-[nam].md` · `research-doi-thu-[quy].md` · `ket-qua-[nam]-[thang].md`. Ten file la mot phan cua bo nho — ten mo ho thi AI trich dan sai.

**Hieu chinh Project moi:** test bang 5 cau hoi thuc te, chinh custom instruction 2-3 vong den khi output on dinh, roi moi mo cho ca team dung.

**Khi nao mo chat moi:** moi chien dich mot chat rieng trong Project; het chien dich thi ket xuat ket luan thanh knowledge file roi dong chat. Khong keo dai mot chat qua nhieu chien dich — ngu canh cu se lam AI de xuat lai thu da that bai.

**Khi nao KHONG can Project:** phan tich mot file CSV le, doc feedback mot dot — chat + upload file la du.

## 4. Checklist khai thac thong tin truoc khi lap ke hoach

Hoi theo nhom, moi lan toi da 4 cau. Thieu nhom nao thi ghi "chua co" — khong doan.

**Business.** Nganh va dia ban? Doanh thu/thang hien tai? Team bao nhieu nguoi, ai lam duoc gi? Dang dung tool/he thong nao (sheet, ads manager, CRM)? Nam vua roi cai gi ra tien, cai gi dot tien, cai gi bat buoc phai doi?

**Khach hang.** Khach la ai (tuoi, nghe, thu nhap, dia ly)? Van de cu the va muc do cap bach? Hien ho dang giai quyet bang cach nao, ton bao nhieu? Ho dung tu ngu gi de mo ta van de (lay tu review/inbox that)? Dieu gi cuoi cung khien ho chot? Ly do pho bien nhat khien ho hoi roi bien mat? Phan khuc nao dang chuyen doi tot nhat va vi sao?

**San pham / offer.** San pham, goi va gia? AOV? Ti le chot lead→don? USP so voi lua chon thay the? Offer hien tai co khop persona uu tien khong? Chinh sach bao hanh/doi tra/uu dai?

**Canh tranh.** 3-5 doi thu truc tiep? Ho dang chay thong diep va offer gi? Review tieu cuc cua ho lo ra diem yeu nao? Khoang trong nao chua ai phuc vu tot?

**Ngan sach / KPI.** Tong ngan sach thang va quy? Doanh thu muc tieu va moc thoi gian? CPL/CPM/CTR uoc tinh hien tai? ROAS toi thieu chap nhan duoc? Nguong dung/cat lo o tuan 1 va tuan 2? North Star metric la gi va nut that hien tai nam o dau?

**Kenh.** Dang chay kenh nao, ket qua ra sao? Persona thuc su o kenh nao? Vai tro tung kenh (keo traffic / chot don / giu khach)? Kenh nao dot nay KHONG lam va vi sao?

**Brand / voice.** Tone of voice? Tu ngu cam dung? Cau dinh vi hien tai? Rang buoc visual (logo, mau, font, vung an toan)?

**Timeline & nguon luc.** Ngay bat dau va do dai chien dich? Cac moc review? Nang luc san xuat (quay duoc video hay chi co anh, designer co san khong)? Deadline tung deliverable?

## 5. Anti-pattern

- **Dung 1 tool lam tat ca.** Bat tool phan tich di san xuat 30 caption, hoac bat tool ideation di lam ke hoach quy — ca hai deu ra ket qua duoi chuan. Chia viec theo bang o Muc 1.
- **Dung du 3 tang cho moi viec nho.** Nguoc lai cua loi tren: quy trinh nang ne, team bo cuoc sau 2 tuan. Bat dau tu tool don gian nhat du dung.
- **Paste lai context moi chat.** Moi lan phai giai thich lai brand tu dau la dau hieu chua thiet lap Project/knowledge file. Ton thoi gian va moi lan ke lai mot kieu khac nhau → output khong nhat quan.
- **Khong luu output.** Ke hoach, phan tich, bai hoc nam rai rac trong chat cu. Cuoi quy khong ai truy lai duoc da quyet dinh gi va vi sao. Moi ket qua co gia tri phai thanh file trong Brand Hub trong 48h.
- **De AI tu bia so lieu.** Hoi trend tuan nay hoac benchmark nganh ma khong cung cap nguon → AI se dien so nghe hop ly. Luon dua export that (Ads Manager, GA4, CRM) hoac benchmark tu `references/benchmarks-vietnam.md`; custom instruction phai co dong cam bia so.
- **Dang thang output AI.** Mat ca tinh thuong hieu va thinh thoang sai su that. Output AI la ban nhap — nguoi phai bien tap va doi chieu thuc te.
- **Nghien cuu doi thu truoc khi xac dinh khach hang.** Sai thu tu → sao chep doi thu thay vi tim khoang trong.
- **Bo qua buoc nghiem thu & du phong.** Ke hoach khong co kich ban xau se bi bo ngay khi so lieu xau.
- **Tu dong hoa khi quy trinh thu cong chua chay on.** Tu dong hoa mot quy trinh sai = nhan ban cai sai. Thang 1 lam tay, thang 2-3 ban tu dong, tu thang 4 moi tu dong hoa — nguoi van duyet output.
- **San xuat hang loat lien tuc.** Chat luong tut manh sau 2-3 gio lam viec lien tuc; 5-6 bai lam ky hon 20 bai lam voi.
- **Sua skill khi output sai.** Nguyen nhan gan nhu luon nam o custom instruction hoac knowledge file thieu/cu.
- **Doi tool thay cho noi chuyen voi khach hang that.** AI tong hop duoc cai da co; no khong tao ra insight moi tu hu khong.
