# B2B Lead Gen Playbook

## ICP worksheet

| Field | Question |
|-------|----------|
| Segment | What companies do we sell to? |
| Economic buyer | Who can approve purchase? |
| User | Who uses it day to day? |
| Trigger | What signal shows need now? |
| Pain | Does the problem cost money, time, or risk? |
| Exclusion | Who should not enter the list? |

## Prospecting branches

| Branch | Signals |
|--------|---------|
| SaaS | tech stack, hiring, funding, competitor usage |
| B2B service | industry, growth stage, content/activity, visible pain |
| Local SMB | reviews, website quality, maps category, location |
| Export/B2B trade | buyer/importer type, certification, trade show, MOQ |

## Cold email rules

- 1 email = 1 idea = 1 CTA.
- Open with real relevance: trigger, role, company change, public proof.
- Talk about pain/outcome, not feature lists.
- Make the reply easy: "Worth a look?" / "Should I send the 3 ideas?"
- Follow up with added value, not just "bumping this".

## CRM stages

| Stage | Entry criteria |
|-------|----------------|
| New | New lead, untouched |
| Working | Outreach in progress |
| Engaged | Replied, clicked, or showed interest |
| MQL | Fit + intent above threshold |
| SQL | Sales confirmed need/budget/timeline |
| Opportunity | Deal value and next step exist |
| Closed Won/Lost | Final outcome |

## Compliance guardrails

- Do not buy lists with unclear source.
- Include opt-out in outbound.
- Respect GDPR/CAN-SPAM/CASL/PDPA by market.
- Do not automate scraping on platforms that forbid it.
- Verify emails before volume sends to protect domain reputation.
