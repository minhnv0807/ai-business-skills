# Offer Framework

## Fast diagnosis

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| Many inquiries, few buyers | Outcome is unclear | Rewrite the result as a concrete metric or milestone |
| Buyers say "let me think" | Risk is high or proof is weak | Add proof, guarantee, demo, or pilot |
| Buyers say it is expensive | Perceived value is too low | Add objection-handling bonuses or support |
| Buyers purchase then refund | Promise is too broad or onboarding is weak | Tighten claims and add a first milestone |
| Entry plan sells but premium does not | Ladder lacks upgrade logic | Separate premium by speed, support, or done-for-you delivery |

## Guarantee types

| Type | Best for | Warning |
|------|----------|---------|
| Money-back | Digital products, courses, templates | Define conditions clearly |
| Milestone | Coaching, consulting, agencies | Commit to process unless you control the outcome |
| Service credit | SaaS, B2B services | Easier to operate than refunds |
| First-result | Tools, training, onboarding | Define a small first result |
| Satisfaction | Premium services | Scope revisions carefully |
| Pilot-to-paid | High-ticket B2B | Useful for long sales cycles |
| Cancellation promise | Subscription | Reduces fear of lock-in |
| No guarantee | Luxury, custom, enterprise | Requires strong proof and positioning |

## Bonus rules

A good bonus answers an objection:

- "I do not have time" -> templates, checklist, done-for-you setup.
- "I do not know where to start" -> onboarding call, 7-day roadmap.
- "I am not sure it fits my industry" -> examples and variants.
- "I might do it wrong" -> review session or QA checklist.

Do not use bonuses only to inflate fake value.

## Honest scarcity

Use scarcity only with real constraints:

- Capacity: limited delivery slots.
- Cohort: fixed live start date.
- Inventory: physical stock.
- Seasonal: real event or deadline.
- Price increase: real date and reason.
- Bonus expiry: bonus requires time or resources.

Avoid evergreen fake countdowns and repeated "today only" claims.

## Naming formulas

Use:

- Outcome + audience: "90-Day Founder Pipeline Sprint"
- Mechanism + outcome: "AI Sales Page Engine"
- Stage + result: "First 100 Leads System"
- Category + differentiator: "Done-With-You Shopify Launch"

Avoid hype without proof: revolutionary, game-changing, secret, hack, 10x.
