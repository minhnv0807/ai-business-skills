# Community Platform Rules

> Reference for skill 38-community-seeding-global. Read when selecting communities, setting participation cadence, or deciding how to disclose a material connection on a specific platform.

Platform policies change. Treat everything below as the shape of the rule, and verify the current wording in the platform's own policy and in the specific community's rules before posting.

---

## Reddit

**Governing rules:** Reddit Content Policy (spam, vote manipulation, impersonation) plus each subreddit's own rules, which are stricter and take precedence.

| Aspect | Practice |
|--------|----------|
| Self-promotion norm | The community-standard 9:1 ratio — at most one in ten contributions should be self-referential. Many subreddits enforce a harder rule or ban links entirely |
| Accounts | One account per person. Multiple accounts used to support each other's content is ban-worthy across the whole site, not just the subreddit |
| Vote manipulation | Asking anyone to upvote, or voting from a second account, risks a sitewide ban. Do not do it, do not ask contractors to do it |
| Disclosure | State the affiliation in the comment or post body: "I work at [company], so treat this as biased" — before the recommendation, not after |
| Links | Many subreddits auto-remove links from low-karma or new accounts. Check whether the subreddit has a dedicated self-promo thread |
| Tenure gates | Common. Some subreddits require minimum account age or karma before posting at all |
| AMAs | Usually require mod approval and verification in advance |
| Risk profile | Highest reputational risk of any platform on this list. Reddit users identify and publicize astroturfing quickly, and the resulting thread outranks the brand's own content |

**Best-fitting post types:** genuine question, expertise share with real numbers, honest post-mortem, answering existing threads.

## Facebook Groups

| Aspect | Practice |
|--------|----------|
| Self-promotion norm | Group-specific. Many groups run a weekly promo thread and forbid promotion elsewhere |
| Accounts | Real profile required by Meta's authenticity policy. Fake or duplicate profiles violate Community Standards |
| Admin approval | Most relevant groups are moderated with post approval. Being useful in comments first improves approval odds |
| Disclosure | State the affiliation in the post. Meta also requires branded-content tagging where a commercial arrangement exists |
| Links | Frequently restricted for new members. Read the pinned rules post |
| Cadence | 1-2 posts per week per group is the practical ceiling; more reads as spam to members and to admins |

**Best-fitting post types:** question, experience share, customer story shared by the customer.

## Discord

| Aspect | Practice |
|--------|----------|
| Self-promotion norm | Almost always confined to a designated channel (`#showcase`, `#self-promo`, `#tools`). Promoting outside it is the fastest way to be removed |
| Accounts | One account, real name or consistent handle, role and affiliation in the profile or nickname where the server allows it |
| Disclosure | Server owners often ask members to tag themselves by role. Take the tag |
| DMs | Unsolicited DMs to server members are the most-reported behavior on Discord and violate most server rules. Never do it |
| Cadence | Participation is conversational and continuous, not scheduled. Presence over posts |
| Value | Support-style help in the main channels is what earns standing here |

**Best-fitting post types:** answering existing questions, expertise share in the designated channel.

## Slack communities

| Aspect | Practice |
|--------|----------|
| Self-promotion norm | Usually a dedicated channel plus an intro-channel norm of stating who you are and what you do |
| Accounts | Real name and company in the profile is the default expectation |
| Disclosure | Covered by the profile plus an in-message note when recommending your own product |
| DMs | Acceptable only after a public exchange, and only if the community permits it. Many explicitly forbid cold pitches |
| Cadence | Answer-first. One useful answer in a busy channel outperforms a scheduled post |
| Note | Many professional Slack communities are paid or invite-only. Membership terms may explicitly forbid vendor prospecting — read them |

**Best-fitting post types:** answering existing threads, expertise share.

## LinkedIn groups and feed

| Aspect | Practice |
|--------|----------|
| Self-promotion norm | Groups vary widely; the main feed is the more productive surface for most brands |
| Accounts | Real identity is the platform's premise. Employer is already visible on the profile, which covers baseline affiliation |
| Disclosure | For paid partnerships, use the platform's paid-partnership label and say it in the copy |
| Links | External links in the post body typically get less distribution; putting the link in the first comment is the common workaround |
| Cadence | Daily is sustainable on the feed. Groups are lower-yield and higher-moderation |

**Best-fitting post types:** expertise share, customer story with the customer tagged and consenting.

## Quora

| Aspect | Practice |
|--------|----------|
| Self-promotion norm | Answer the question genuinely first. Promotional-only answers get collapsed |
| Accounts | Real name policy on most profiles. Credentials shown next to the answer |
| Disclosure | Quora requires affiliation disclosure when answering about your own company, and requires disclosure of affiliate links. Say it in the answer text |
| Links | One relevant link in a substantive answer is acceptable; link-only answers are removed |
| Longevity | Answers rank in search for years, which makes this the highest-compounding surface on this list |

**Best-fitting post types:** answering existing questions, expertise share.

## Niche forums and community platforms

Covers Discourse-based forums, industry boards, Indie Hackers, Stack Exchange sites, and vertical communities.

| Aspect | Practice |
|--------|----------|
| Self-promotion norm | Read the FAQ or rules page — small forums almost always have one, and enforcement is personal |
| Accounts | A signature line stating role and company is often the accepted disclosure mechanism |
| Disclosure | Stack Exchange requires explicit affiliation disclosure in any answer mentioning your own product, and treats undisclosed promotion as spam |
| Cadence | Slow. These communities have long memories and small member counts; one bad post is remembered |
| Value | Depth wins. These are the places where a genuinely detailed answer creates lasting inbound |

## Disclosure wording that works anywhere

Plain, up front, and in the post itself:

- "Disclosure: I work at [company], so I am biased here."
- "I am the founder of [product] — happy to answer, but weigh that accordingly."
- "Paid partnership with [brand]." (plus the platform's own label where one exists)
- "This link is an affiliate link."

Avoid disclosures that only appear in a bio, in a profile, in a hashtag block, or after a "see more" cut. The FTC standard is that the disclosure must be clear, conspicuous, and hard to miss where the endorsement appears.

## Regulatory backdrop

| Rule | What it means here |
|------|--------------------|
| FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255) | Any material connection between the endorser and the brand — employment, payment, free product, equity, affiliate commission — must be disclosed clearly and conspicuously |
| FTC Rule on Fake Reviews and Testimonials (16 CFR Part 465) | Prohibits creating, buying, or disseminating fake or insider reviews, including employee reviews without clear disclosure, and prohibits suppressing negative reviews. Carries civil penalties |
| EU Unfair Commercial Practices Directive / Omnibus Directive | Bans falsely representing yourself as a consumer, and requires traders to verify that reviews come from actual purchasers |
| UK CMA / DMCC Act | Fake reviews and hidden advertising are enforceable offenses |
| Platform terms | Independent of the law, every platform on this list can and does ban accounts for inauthentic behavior. Compliance with the law is the floor, not the ceiling |

When in doubt: if the plan only works because the audience does not know who is behind the post, it is the wrong plan.
