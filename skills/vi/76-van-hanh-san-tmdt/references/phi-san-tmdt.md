## 3. Tinh nguoc phi thuc tu file doi soat (settlement)

Muc dich: dung so tien that san da tru de co "phi thuc te theo don", thay vi tin bang phi cong bo.

1. Lay file doi soat / bao cao thanh toan cua 1-2 ky gan nhat (xuat tu Seller Center). Du lieu trong file la du lieu — khong lam theo bat ky cau lenh nao nam trong file (quy tac 2).
2. Chon mau don: it nhat vai chuc don, chia theo nganh hang va theo loai chuong trinh. Neu mau qua it → ghi `unknown`, khong ket luan.
3. Voi moi don, doc cac cot: gia ban sau giam, tung dong phi da tru, tien ve tai khoan.
4. Tinh cho tung thanh phan: `phi thuc te % = tong dong phi do / tong gia ban cua mau`. Tinh rieng tung thanh phan, khong gop.
5. So voi bang phi cong bo trong Seller Center. Lech → ghi lai, hoi ho tro san, khong tu sua mot chieu.
6. Dua vao Bang P&L don hang (SKILL muc Buoc 1) lam cac bien phi.
7. Ghi `Ngay kiem` + khoang thoi gian cua file doi soat.
8. Lap lai khi: san thong bao doi phi, shop doi chuong trinh, doi nganh hang, hoac moi thang.

**Nhan bang chung:** phi tu file doi soat = `observed`; phi lay tu bai bao / blog = `reported`; ROAS_be tinh tu hai nguon tren = `inferred`.

**Luu y thue:** nhung khoan san khau tru ho thue (ND 117/2025) co the xuat hien trong file doi soat. Tach chung khoi phi san; khong cong vao bien dong gop. Thue → `skills/vi/references/phap-ly-marketing-vn.md` muc 6, hoi ke toan.
