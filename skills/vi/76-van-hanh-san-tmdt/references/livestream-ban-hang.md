### Uu dai trong phien

- Chi dung uu dai thuc su co tren he thong. Khong "chi con vai suat" khi con nhieu; khong dem nguoc gia ao.
- Moi uu dai: ten, dieu kien, thoi han, so luong thuc, nguoi bat.
- Tinh bien dong gop sau uu dai cho tung SKU truoc live (xem `mega-sale-va-voucher.md` muc 2); SKU am → khong dua vao live.

| Gio | Nguoi xem | Nguoi xem trung binh dong thoi | Click vao san pham | Don | Doanh thu thuan | Hoan / huy | Ghi chu |

- Ghi chu: doan nao tang / giam, ly do co the (`inferred`, ghi ly do).

## 3. Danh gia sau live

1. **Quan sat:** doanh thu thuan, don, hoan / huy, tang theo gio, tin nhan chua tra loi.
2. **P&L thuc:** tinh lai bien dong gop theo don that (phi tu file doi soat — `phi-san-tmdt.md`). Phien lo / lai?
3. **Claim va gia:** xem lai bang ghi hinh de kiem co claim / gia nao lech su that khong. Co → sua kich ban, bao nguoi chiu trach nhiem.
4. **Chat:** cau hoi lap lai → cap nhat FAQ, mo ta san pham, kich ban.
5. **Hanh dong tiep:** giu / sua / bo SKU; doi gio live; doi host — moi thay doi 1 khoi cho duyet (quy tac 7).
6. **Luu:** retro 1 trang cho phien nay; mau tiep theo.
