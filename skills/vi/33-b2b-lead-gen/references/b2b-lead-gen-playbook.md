# B2B Lead Gen Playbook

## ICP worksheet

| Truong | Cau hoi |
|--------|---------|
| Segment | Ban cho nhom cong ty nao? |
| Economic buyer | Ai co quyen mua? |
| User | Ai dung hang ngay? |
| Trigger | Dau hieu nao cho thay ho can ngay? |
| Pain | Van de co ton tien/thoi gian/rui ro khong? |
| Exclusion | Ai khong nen dua vao list? |

## Prospecting branches

| Branch | Tin hieu |
|--------|----------|
| SaaS | tech stack, hiring, funding, competitor usage |
| B2B service | industry, growth stage, content/activity, pain visible |
| Local SMB | reviews, website quality, maps category, location |
| Export/B2B trade | buyer/importer type, certification, trade show, MOQ |

## Cold email rules

- 1 email = 1 y tuong = 1 CTA.
- Mo dau bang relevance that: trigger, role, company change, public proof.
- Noi ve pain/ket qua, khong ke le tinh nang.
- De nguoi nhan de tra loi: "Worth a look?" / "Should I send the 3 ideas?"
- Follow-up them gia tri, khong chi "bump".

## CRM stages

| Stage | Entry criteria |
|-------|----------------|
| New | Lead moi, chua cham |
| Working | Dang outreach |
| Engaged | Da reply/click/book interest |
| MQL | Fit + intent du nguong |
| SQL | Sales xac nhan co nhu cau/budget/timeline |
| Opportunity | Co deal value va next step |
| Closed Won/Lost | Ket thuc |

## Compliance guardrails

- Khong mua list khong ro nguon.
- Them opt-out trong email outbound.
- Ton trong GDPR/CAN-SPAM/CASL/PDPA theo thi truong.
- Khong tu dong scrape nen tang cam bot neu khong co quyen.
- Verify email truoc khi gui hang loat de bao ve domain.
