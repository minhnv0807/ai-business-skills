# Ngon ngu nguoi that — viet brief cham dung noi dau doanh nghiep Viet

> **Doc truoc khi render bat ky brief nao cua skill 20.** Variant la khung noi dung; cau chu cuoi gui khach phai viet lai theo file nay.
> **Ap dung cho:** moi brief khach dien (20 variants + brief tuy bien), va cac form hoi khach khac trong repo.
> **Thi truong:** Viet Nam 2025–2026.

---

## 1. Vi sao brief "giong AI" thi hong

Brief la cuoc noi chuyen dau tien giua agency va chu doanh nghiep. Chu DN doc thay "dung bung minh" thi moi dien that, dien ky.

| Brief giong AI | Chu DN phan ung | Ket qua cho agency |
|---|---|---|
| "Vui long cung cap thong tin ve dinh vi thuong hieu" | Thay bi tra hoi, khong biet tra loi | Tick "Chua ro" het, dien qua loa hoac bo giua chung |
| Hoi khai niem: muc tieu, USP, persona, KPI | Phai di hoi nguoi khac hoac doan | Du lieu chung chung, ke hoach cung chung chung |
| Mo dau bang thu tuc, bang bieu | Chua thay agency hieu minh | Chua tin, chua ke that |
| Hoi bang tinh huong that ("Khach inbox hoi gia xong co hay im luon khong?") | Gat gu, tra loi ngay, ke them | Co noi dau that, co nguyen lieu noi dung that |

## 2. Bay nguyen tac

1. **Mo bang noi dau, khong mo bang thu tuc.** Phan dau brief la "Anh/chi dang gap chuyen nao?" — 5–8 tinh huong that cua nganh de tick. Vua cho chu DN thay minh duoc hieu, vua la ban chan doan cho agency.
2. **Hoi bang tinh huong, khong hoi bang khai niem.** Thay "Muc tieu kenh" bang "Sau 3 thang, anh/chi mong nhat dieu gi xay ra?" — lua chon la canh cu the nhin thay duoc ("Khach moi toi noi 'em xem video chi tren TikTok nen toi'").
3. **Lua chon la cau noi that cua nguoi trong nghe.** Viet dung cach chu DN noi voi nhau: "khach seen roi im", "dang hoai khong ai xem", "nhan vien nghi keo theo khach". Khong viet "ty le chuyen doi thap".
4. **Moi phan co 1 cau "vi sao em hoi" dong cam.** "Em hoi truoc chuyen nay vi moi noi dau mot cho khac nhau" — thay cho "Muc dich: thu thap du lieu".
5. **Hoi ca cam xuc, khong chi so lieu.** Noi so (len hinh bi che, noi sai bi phat, bi boc phot), noi met (dung may ca ngay, thue 2–3 ben roi), noi mong (khach nhac ten tiem minh). Day la nguyen lieu cho noi dung va cho cach tu van.
6. **Tu doi thuong; giu thuat ngu chu DN dung hang ngay, doi thuat ngu agency.** Giu: inbox, chot don, live, KOC, seeding, bop tuong tac, mua thap diem, khach quen. Doi: TOFU, persona, USP, dinh vi, tru cot noi dung, KPI, conversion.
7. **Cu the, co hinh anh, co con so doi thuong.** "Vai tram luot xem", "khach 99k gan nha", "thang 7 mua mua vang khach" — nguoi doc hinh dung duoc ngay. Con so chi dung de mo ta hien trang, KHONG dung de hua ket qua.

**Gioi han bat buoc:**
- **Dong cam, khong hu doa.** Goi dung noi dau, khong phong dai noi dau de ep mua goi to.
- **Than thien, khong suong sa.** Khong tieng long qua da, khong teencode, khong emoji. Xung ho giu nhu variant (anh/chi – ben em); giong trung tinh Bac–Nam, "nhe" dung vua phai.
- **Khong che doi thu, khong che khach.** Noi dau viet o goc "minh dang gap", khong phai "khach kho tinh", "doi thu choi xau".

## 3. Tu dien doi giong

| Tranh (giong AI / giong agency) | Thay bang (giong nguoi that) |
|---|---|
| Vui long cung cap thong tin ve... | Anh/chi ke em nghe... |
| Quy khach / Chung toi | Anh/chi / Ben em |
| Muc tieu chien dich, muc tieu kenh | Sau 3 thang, anh/chi mong nhat dieu gi xay ra? |
| Khach hang muc tieu, chan dung khach hang (persona) | Khach hay toi la ai, hay toi vi chuyen gi |
| Diem khac biet (USP) | Khach quen hay khen cho minh cau gi? |
| Dinh vi thuong hieu | Anh/chi muon khach nhac toi minh la nghi toi dieu gi |
| Tru cot noi dung | Cac nhom chu de video / bai dang |
| Tang nhan dien thuong hieu | Nguoi quanh khu vuc biet toi minh |
| Tuong tac thap | Dang len ma it ai tha tim, binh luan |
| Ty le chuyen doi thap | Khach hoi nhieu ma chot it |
| Trai nghiem khach hang | Khach thay the nao tu luc buoc vao toi luc ve |
| Nguon luc noi bo | Hien ben minh co ai lam duoc viec nay |
| Ngan sach marketing | Moi thang anh/chi tinh de ra khoang bao nhieu cho viec nay |
| Timeline trien khai | Khi nao anh/chi can |
| Chua xac dinh | Chua ro — ben em tu van |
| Toi uu hoa, nang tam, toan dien, da dang, vuot troi, giai phap, hanh trinh, buoc dot pha | Bo di. Noi thang viec cu the se lam |

## 4. Kho noi dau va cam xuc theo nganh

Moi dong la **cau chu DN hay noi that** — dua thang vao phan "Anh/chi dang gap chuyen nao?" (chon 5–8 cau hop nhat voi khach, chinh chu cho khop). Cot cam xuc dung de chon giong va cau "vi sao em hoi".

### Nhom 1 — Dich vu dia phuong

| Nganh (variant) | Noi dau hay gap | Cam xuc ben duoi |
|---|---|---|
| Spa / beauty (01) | Dang video deu ma luot xem le te vai tram · Khach inbox hoi gia xong seen roi im · Khach so gia voi cho 99k gan nha · Chu dung may ca ngay, khong con suc nghi noi dung · Thue 2–3 ben roi, video na na tiem nao cung vay · KTV gioi lam 1–2 nam la nghi, keo khach theo | Met; ay nay vi lam co tam ma khach khong thay; ngai len hinh |
| Nha hang / cafe (02) | Mat bang tang ma khach khong tang · App giao hang an chiet khau nang · 1 review 1 sao vi ship tre la tut · Mon moi ra 1 tuan da bi copy · Khach toi 1 lan roi khong quay lai · Mua mua, cuoi thang vang khach | Lo dong tien moi thang; bat luc voi review |
| Phong kham (03) | Khach so dau, so bi "chat chem" · Bac si kin lich, khong ai lam noi dung · Quang cao y te bi kiem soat chat, so noi sai · 1 ca that bai bi boc len group la mat uy tin | So rui ro phap ly; so mat uy tin nghe |
| Gym / yoga (04) | Hoi vien dong nam roi bo sau 1 thang · Thang sau Tet va mua mua vang · Canh tranh voi phong gia re, "tap thu 0 dong" · PT nghi keo hoi vien theo | Hut hang khi phong vang; met vi phai chay khuyen mai lien tuc |

| Studio cuoi / chup anh (06) | Mua cuoi qua tai, thang vang thi ngoi cho · Khach xin gia xong so, chon cho re hon · Concept bi copy · Khach che "khong giong anh mau" | Mua vu bap benh; ay nay khi bi so gia |
| Pet service (07) | Chu nuoi lo thu cung bi dau, bi lay benh · 1 bai che trong group la lan rat nhanh · Kho tuyen nguoi thuong thu cung that | So su co; so review xau |

## 5. Mau cau truc "mo dau dong cam"

Dat ngay sau header, truoc moi cau hoi khac:

```markdown
[2–3 cau mo dau: goi dung cai kho chung cua nganh, bang giong nguoi trong nghe]
[1 cau: brief nay de ben em hieu minh nhu nguoi trong nghe, roi lam cho dung]

## Truoc het, ben minh dang gap chuyen nao? *(tick het nhung gi dung)*
- [ ] [noi dau 1 — cau noi that]
- [ ] ... (5–8 cau, lay tu muc 4)
- [ ] Khac: ......

> Em hoi truoc chuyen nay vi [ly do dong cam, 1 cau].
```

Mau "buc tranh sau 3 thang" thay cho cau hoi muc tieu:

```markdown
**Sau 3 thang, anh/chi mong nhat dieu gi xay ra?** *(chon 1–2)*
- [ ] [canh cu the nhin thay duoc 1]
- [ ] [canh cu the 2]
> [1 cau noi that ve ky vong thoi gian — khong hua con so]
```

## 6. Vi du doi truoc / sau

| Truoc (giong AI) | Sau (giong nguoi that) |
|---|---|
| Muc tieu chinh cua kenh: □ Tang nhan dien □ Tang tuong tac □ Tang chuyen doi | Sau 3 thang, anh/chi mong nhat dieu gi? □ Khach moi toi noi "em xem video chi tren TikTok nen toi" □ Moi tuan co them tin nhan hoi lich tu video □ Mo kenh ra thay dang hoang, gui link cho khach khong ngai |
| Diem khac biet cua doanh nghiep (USP): ...... | Khach quen hay khen cho minh cau gi? Ghi dung loi khach. Vd: "Chi soi da ky roi moi tu van, khong ep mua goi" |
| Vui long cung cap ngan sach du kien | Moi thang anh/chi tinh de ra khoang bao nhieu cho viec nay? (de ben em tu van goi vua tui, khong ep goi to) |
| Muc dich: thu thap du lieu ve khach hang muc tieu | Em hoi vi moi cau khach hoi la mot y tuong video — khach hoi gi thi minh noi dung cai do |
| Nguoi xuat hien trong video: □ Founder □ Nhan vien | Ai se dung truoc may quay? ... Noi that nhe, nguoi do len hinh thay sao? □ Tu tin □ Tam on □ Ngai lam, chua quay bao gio (khong sao, ben em co cach) |
