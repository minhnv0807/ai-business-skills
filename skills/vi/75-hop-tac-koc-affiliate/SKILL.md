## Thu thap thong tin

Doc `.agents/product-marketing-context.md` va ket qua `10-tinh-kpi-nguoc` truoc (quy tac 1). Chi hoi cai con thieu, toi da 4 cau:

1. **Muc tieu chuong trinh la gi?** Nhan biet (reach) / lead / don hang / bo sung creative cho ads. Chi chon 1 muc tieu chinh.
2. **San pham, gia ban va bien loi nhuan sau phi?** Day la tran cho hoa hong va phi co dinh.
3. **Kenh nao?** TikTok Shop / Shopee / Facebook / YouTube / kenh khac. Moi kenh co co che link, ma giam gia va tracking khac nhau.
4. **Ngan sach va CPA muc tieu?** Neu chua co CPA, goi `10-tinh-kpi-nguoc` de tinh nguoc tu muc tieu doanh thu.

Thieu thong tin nhung van lam duoc → lam, ghi ro gia dinh o dau output.

## Nguyen tac

1. **Thong tin creator tu khai la `reported`.** Follower, view, GMV, demographics trong media kit chi la `reported` cho den khi co bang chung (anh chup analytics goc, bao cao tu san, don hang do ma rieng). Khong dua con so `reported` vao ke hoach nhu su that (quy tac 2, 3).
2. **Neo gia = chi phi tren moi ket qua dat chuan, khong phai gia moi follower.** Cong thuc: `phi toi da = so ket qua ky vong x CPA muc tieu`. Khong bao gia theo follower.
3. **Chon tier theo vai tro trong phieu, khong chi theo so follower.** Reach, bang chung, chot don va creative cho ads la 4 viec khac nhau — can nguoi khac nhau.
4. **Khong co bang gia thi truong trong skill nay.** Moi con so gia dua ra de minh hoa phai ghi "vi du gia dinh" hoac `[CAN SO LIEU THAT]`. Gia that lay tu bao gia nhan duoc va so sanh qua cac creator (quy tac 4, 5).
5. **Nhap truoc, ghi sau.** Skill chi soan ke hoach, danh sach va hop dong nhap. Gui loi moi, chuyen tien, dang bai, chay ads bang video creator can nguoi duyet co ten (quy tac 7).
6. **Tach nghia vu phap ly va thuc hanh tot.** Muc "Phap ly" ben duoi chi dan link ve hub, khong chep lai van ban.

## Quy trinh

### Buoc 1 — Thiet ke chuong trinh

Chot 4 thu theo thu tu:

1. **Muc tieu chinh** va **chi so dat chuan** cho muc tieu do. "Ket qua dat chuan" la thu tinh duoc 1 don vi: 1 lead co SDT hop le, 1 don khong bi hoan, 1 video dung lam duoc creative ads. Khong tinh view tho.
2. **Co cau tier theo vai tro phieu:**

| Vai tro | Tier thuong hop | Dung de | Ket qua dat chuan goi y |
|---------|----------------|---------|------------------------|
| Nhan biet (TOFU) | KOL / KOC lon, noi dung giai tri | Mo rong tep | Reach that trong tep muc tieu, luot click |
| Bang chung (MOFU) | KOC nho, nguoi dung that | Review, truoc/sau, tra loi nghi van | Luot luu, binh luan hoi mua, tin nhan |
| Chot don (BOFU) | KOC co tep mua hang, host live | Keo don truc tiep | Don khong hoan qua ma / link rieng |
| Creative cho ads | KOC bat ky co noi dung hop | Lam nguyen lieu cho `05-copy-quang-cao` | Video duoc duyet dung lam ads |

3. **Phan bo ngan sach theo vai tro** (heuristic, dieu chinh theo so that): dat phan lon vao vai tro dang ra ket qua tot nhat o du lieu cu, phan nho thu nghiem vai tro moi. Khong chia cung theo ty le.
4. **Quy mo thu nghiem truoc, nhan rong sau:** vong 1 chi thu mot nhom nho creator de co du lieu cham diem (Buoc 6), roi moi mo rong.

### Buoc 2 — Tim KOC o dau

Cac nguon, xep theo muc kiem soat chat luong:

| Nguon | Uu diem | Luu y |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Khach hang that va nguoi da dung san pham (danh sach don, review, nguoi hay tuong tac) | Da dung that, de dap ung nghia vu "da dung san pham" | Muc do chuyen nghiep khac nhau, can brief ky |
| Nguoi dang noi ve nganh / san pham tuong tu (tim theo hashtag, tu khoa, tab san pham) | Dung tep, co noi dung san | Co the da lam cho doi thu — hoi lich su hop tac |
| Muc tim creator ngay tren san (vd khu vuc affiliate / tim KOC cua TikTok Shop, Shopee Affiliate) | Co du lieu don hang tren san | Ten muc va dieu kien thay doi — kiem tra Seller Center / Seller Centre ngay lap ke hoach |
| Agency, MCN, nen tang trung gian | Nhanh, co hop dong mau | Them 1 tang phi — dung `67-agency-vendor-brief` khi quan ly nhieu KOC qua ben thu ba |
| Gioi thieu tu creator da hop tac tot | Da qua tham dinh gian tiep | Van phai tham dinh lai |

Loc so bo bang 3 cau: noi dung co hop nganh khong, tep nguoi xem co dung ICP khong (doi chieu `09-insight-khach-hang`), va co bang chung da dung san pham tuong tu khong.

### Buoc 3 — Tham dinh (tom tat)

Chi tiet cac buoc va dau hieu canh bao: `references/tham-dinh-va-hop-dong-koc.md`. Tom tat 6 hang muc moi KOC:

1. **Tang follower bat thuong** — doi chieu bieu do tang truong, khong chi con so hien tai.
2. **View / follower va tuong tac khong khop** — view cao bat thuong hoac tuong tac gan nhu bang khong so voi tep.
3. **Binh luan chung chung** — doc mau binh luan that, khong dem so.
4. **Lich su thuong hieu va an toan thuong hieu** — bai cu, phat ngon, hop tac voi doi thu.
5. **"Da dung san pham chua?"** — neu chua, gui mau va cho dung truoc khi quay (nghia vu, xem muc Phap ly).
6. **Trang thai xac thuc tai khoan / dinh danh** — xem muc Phap ly.

Moi hang muc ghi trang thai `pass` / `fail` / `unknown` (quy tac 3). **Thieu data khong phai fail.** Cham do phu truoc khi ket luan: do phu duoi 60% thi khong dua diem tong, chi liet ke nhung gi con thieu.

### Buoc 4 — Neo gia va chon mo hinh tra phi

**Neo gia — vi du gia dinh (khong phai gia thi truong):**

```
CPA muc tieu (tu 10-tinh-kpi-nguoc):   [CPA]            vi du gia dinh: 100.000d / don
Ket qua ky vong cua creator:           [N] don          vi du gia dinh: 40 don
Phi toi da = N x CPA                    = 40 x 100.000d = 4.000.000d
```

Cach dung:
- Phi toi da la **tran**, khong phai muc nen tra. Muc tieu la tra thap hon, nhat la khi ket qua ky vong la `reported`.
- Ket qua ky vong lay tu: du lieu bai cu cua chinh brand, ket qua cac creator vong truoc (Buoc 6), hoac ghi `[CAN SO LIEU THAT]` neu chua co. Khong lay tu loi creator hua.
- Neu phi creator bao cao vuot tran → thuong luong, doi mo hinh sang co phan hoa hong, hoac bo qua.
- Neu muc tieu la reach (khong co don), dung chi phi tren 1 luot tiep can dat chuan (da loc tep) thay cho don, va ghi ro day la heuristic.

**Chon mo hinh tra phi:**

| Mo hinh | Cach lam | Phu hop khi | Rui ro |
|---------|---------|-------------|--------|
| Phi co dinh | Tra theo so video, khong phu thuoc ket qua | Can doi gian, muc tieu reach / creative ads, creator chua co du lieu ban | Brand chiu rui ro ket qua |
| Phi + hoa hong | Phi nen thap + hoa hong tren don dat chuan | Creator da co lich su ban, muon chia se rui ro | Can tracking ro rang va cua so ghi nhan dung |
| Affiliate thuan | Chi hoa hong, khong phi co dinh | San pham co bien loi nhuan du, nen tang co ho tro link / ma | Chat luong khong dong deu, it kiem soat noi dung, can chong gian lan |
| Tang san pham (gifting) | Tang san pham, khong tra tien | KOC nho, san pham gia tri cao, muc tieu bang chung | Khong co cam ket dang bai neu khong ghi ro trong thoa thuan |

Nguyen tac chon: du lieu ban cua creator chua co → bat dau phi co dinh nho hoac gifting de thu; da co du lieu → phi + hoa hong; da kiem chung duoc ket qua va co co che chong gian lan → moi mo affiliate thuan o quy mo lon.

### Buoc 5 — Co che affiliate (tom tat)

Chi tiet: `references/co-che-affiliate.md`. Tom tat:

- **Mo vs nhat dinh:** chuong trinh mo cho moi creator dang ky; chuong trinh nhat dinh moi chi nhung creator da chon. Yeu cau mau (sample request) nen duyet tay. Ten tinh nang tren tung san thay doi — kiem tra Seller Center ngay lap ke hoach.
- **Tran hoa hong:** `commission toi da = bien loi nhuan sau phi san − loi nhuan muc tieu`. Phi san lay tu `76-van-hanh-san-tmdt` (`references/phi-san-tmdt.md`), khong ghi cung o day.
- **Cua so ghi nhan (attribution window):** kiem tra tren tung san, khong gia dinh.
- **Kiem tra tap trung vao it creator** (heuristic): neu 1-2 creator keo gan het doanh thu, chuong trinh dang phu thuoc vao ho.
- **Tin hieu gian lan:** tu mua qua ma, lach hoan tra, don bat thuong sau khi dang.
- **Dieu khoan mua dut quyen su dung** neu muon dung video lau dai cho ads.

### Buoc 6 — Hop dong (tom tat)

Chi tiet va checklist day du: `references/tham-dinh-va-hop-dong-koc.md`. Cac dieu khoan khong the thieu:

1. **San pham ban giao** (so video, thoi luong, kenh, thoi han giao) va **quyen duyet** truoc khi dang.
2. **Nghia vu cong bo quan he hop tac** truoc va trong khi dang noi dung.
3. **Quyen su dung noi dung:** thoi han, kenh, co cho phep chay ads qua tai khoan creator (whitelisting / Spark Ads / Partnership ads) hay khong. Template ngan nam o `06-brief-ugc-egc` muc "Hop dong quyen su dung noi dung".
4. **Doc quyen** (neu co), **quyen yeu cau go bai** khi sai su that, **dieu khoan thanh toan** (moc, hinh thuc, xu ly hoan don voi hoa hong).
5. **Xu ly du lieu ca nhan** (neu chia se danh sach hoac thong tin khach) → hub muc 2.

Hop dong la ban nhap. Nguoi co tham quyen cua brand duyet va ky; hoi luat su voi hop dong co gia tri lon hoac nganh nhay cam (quy tac 7, 8).

### Buoc 7 — Cham diem tung creator va quy tac re-book (heuristic)

Moi creator sau moi dot hop tac ghi mot dong:

| Creator | Mo hinh | Chi phi tong | Ket qua dat chuan | Chi phi / ket qua | So voi CPA muc tieu | Video dung lai duoc cho ads | Tuan thu cong bo | Ghi chu |
|---------|---------|-------------|-------------------|-------------------|--------------------|----------------------------|-----------------|---------|
| | | | `observed` | | | Co/Khong | Dat/Khong | |

Nhan nguon: so lay tu analytics goc, don hang theo ma, hoac bao cao san la `observed`; so creator tu gui chua doi chieu la `reported`.

**Quy tac re-book (heuristic, dieu chinh theo so that cua brand):**

| Tinh huong | Hanh dong |
|-----------|----------|
| Chi phi/ket qua thap hon CPA muc tieu, tuan thu cong bo dat | Re-book, tang so luong, thuong luong dieu khoan dai han |
| Gan CPA muc tieu | Re-book 1 lan nua voi goc do khac de xac nhan, chua tang ngan sach |
| Cao hon CPA muc tieu nhung video dung duoc lam creative ads tot | Mua quyen su dung, khong tang phi; danh gia lai bang chi phi creative |
| Cao hon CPA muc tieu va khong co gia tri phu | Dung hop tac |
| Vi pham cong bo, noi sai su that, hoac co dau hieu gian lan | Dung va xu ly theo hop dong, khong re-book |

Chi so nhu ER, save rate, completion rate cua tung bai: dung `03-danh-gia-hieu-suat` va chi coi la heuristic tham khao. Quyet dinh re-book dua tren chi phi tren ket qua dat chuan.

## Phap ly

Chi dan link — khong chep lai van ban. Hub: `skills/vi/references/phap-ly-marketing-vn.md`. Khong phai tu van phap ly.

- **Nghia vu cua KOC / nguoi chuyen tai:** kiem tra thong tin san pham, khong quang cao san pham chua dung, cong bo noi dung quang cao **truoc va trong khi** quang cao → xem hub muc 3 (checklist khi brand thue KOL/KOC o do).
- **Xac thuc tai khoan va dinh danh livestream:** ND 147/2024 (xac thuc tai khoan mang xa hoi) va ND 248/2026 (dinh danh nguoi livestream ban hang, **[BAO CAO]**) → xem hub muc 5. Kiem tra van ban goc tai vbpl.vn / thuvienphapluat.vn va quy che cua san.
- **Thue / khau tru thue cua creator va nguoi ban:** hub muc 6, **hoi ke toan**. Khong ghi thue suat hay nguong o day.
- **Lan ranh do:** KOC quang cao san pham chua dung, an dau hieu "hop tac / quang cao", persona gia → hub muc 7.

Tach bach: cac dieu o tren la nghia vu phap ly. Cac goi y ve cach viet cau cong bo, thoi han hop dong, quy tac re-book la **thuc hanh tot**, khong phai trich luat.
