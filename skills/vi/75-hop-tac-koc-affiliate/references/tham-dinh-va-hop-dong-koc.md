## 1. Tham dinh KOC

**Nguyen tac:** moi con so creator tu khai la `reported` (quy tac 2). Muc dich tham dinh la chuyen `reported` thanh `observed` bang bang chung, hoac ghi `unknown`. **Thieu data khong phai fail** (quy tac 3).

### 1.1 Quy trinh 6 buoc

| Buoc | Viec lam | Bang chung can xin | Ket qua |
|------|---------|-------------------|--------|
| 1 | Xem lich su tang truong theo thoi gian | Anh chup analytics goc cua creator (khong chi trang cong khai) | pass / fail / unknown |
| 2 | Doi chieu view, like, comment voi so nguoi theo doi | Danh sach 10-15 bai gan nhat, so lieu tung bai | pass / fail / unknown |
| 3 | Doc truc tiep binh luan duoi vai bai | Anh chup hoac link bai | pass / fail / unknown |
| 4 | Tra lich su hop tac va phat ngon | Link bai cu, bai hop tac thuong hieu khac, tim ten tren mang | pass / fail / unknown |
| 5 | Hoi ro da dung san pham chua, hoac gui mau | Xac nhan cua creator + thoi gian dung thu | pass / fail / unknown |
| 6 | Kiem tra trang thai xac thuc / dinh danh | Xac nhan tu creator, quy che cua san neu ban qua san | pass / fail / unknown |

Sau khi cham: in **do phu** (so hang muc co ket qua `pass` hoac `fail` / so hang muc ap dung). Do phu duoi 60% → khong dua diem tong; liet ke nhung hang muc `unknown` quan trong nhat va viec can xin them (quy tac 3).

### 1.2 Dau hieu canh bao (viet bang cach nhin, khong co nguong cung)

**Tang follower bat thuong**
- Bieu do tang truong co buoc nhay vot trong thoi gian ngan, khong gan voi bai viral hay su kien nao giai thich duoc.
- Sau buoc nhay, view cua cac bai tiep theo khong tang theo.
- Tang xong lai tut xuong sau mot thoi gian (mat theo doi hang loat).

**View / follower khong khop**
- View moi bai cao hon nhieu lan so theo doi nhung tuong tac (like, binh luan, luu) gan nhu khong co.
- Nguoc lai: so theo doi lon nhung view cac bai gan day rat thap so voi tep.
- View dung yen o cung mot muc o hau het bai, it dao dong, trong khi noi dung khac nhau.
- Doi chieu voi `reported`: tat ca so tren la `reported` cho den khi thay analytics goc.

**Binh luan chung chung**
- Nhieu binh luan giong nhau hoac chi la emoji, "hay qua", "dep qua", "oke", khong lien quan noi dung.
- Binh luan den dong loat trong thoi gian rat ngan roi im.
- Khong co cau hoi, khong co nguoi nhac den trai nghiem that. Neu nguoi xem hoi mua o dau, gia bao nhieu thi day la tin hieu tot.
- Day chi la tin hieu `inferred` — ghi ly do, khong ket luan chac chan chi dua vao mot mau.

**Lich su an toan thuong hieu**
- Phat ngon gay tranh cai, noi dung nhay cam, quang cao san pham sai su that trong qua khu.
- Hop tac gan day voi doi thu truc tiep (xem dieu khoan doc quyen o muc 2).
- Xoa hang loat bai cu truoc khi nhan hop tac (kiem tra ban luu hoac nguoi khac chup lai neu can).

**"Da dung san pham chua?"**
- Tra loi mo ho, tranh ne, chi biet thong tin tu brand.
- Hop tac nhieu san pham cung nhom cung luc, noi giong nhau ve tat ca.
- Cach xu ly: gui mau, cho thoi gian dung thu truoc khi quay. Creator chua dung thi khong ky hop dong quay san pham do (hub muc 3, muc 7).

**Trang thai xac thuc / dinh danh**
- Chua xac thuc tai khoan hoac khong cung cap duoc thong tin dinh danh khi san yeu cau (hub muc 5; kiem tra van ban goc va quy che cua san).
- Neu hop tac gom livestream ban hang: kiem tra quy che livestream cua san ngay luc lap ke hoach, vi quy dinh dinh danh co the thay doi [BAO CAO — hub muc 5].

## 2. Checklist dieu khoan hop dong

Day la checklist de lam viec voi nguoi duyet hop dong, khong phai ban hop dong hoan chinh. Mau thoa thuan ngan o `06-brief-ugc-egc` (muc "Hop dong quyen su dung noi dung") dung cho tung video; file nay bao phu cap chuong trinh. Hop dong co gia tri lon hoac nganh nhay cam → hoi luat su (hub dau trang).

### 2.1 San pham ban giao

- [ ] So luong video / bai, thoi luong, kenh dang
- [ ] Thoi han giao ban tho va ngay dang
- [ ] **Quyen duyet:** brand xem truoc khi dang, thoi gian phan hoi, so lan sua toi da
- [ ] Mau san pham gui creator va thoi gian dung thu truoc khi quay
- [ ] Noi dung bat buoc / khong duoc noi (claim co bang chung; nganh nhay cam doi chieu hub muc 3)

### 2.2 Nghia vu cong bo

- [ ] Ghi ro creator phai cong bo day la noi dung quang cao **truoc va trong khi** quang cao (hub muc 3)
- [ ] Vi tri va cach cong bo (dau video, dau bai, nhan tai tro cua nen tang)
- [ ] Creator xac nhan da dung san pham thuc su
- [ ] Brand cung cap tai lieu san pham de creator kiem tra thong tin (hub muc 3, checklist thue KOL/KOC)

### 2.3 Quyen su dung

- [ ] **Thoi han** su dung (so thang tu ngay giao / dang) — de trong `[X thang]` neu chua thuong luong
- [ ] **Kenh** duoc phep dung: kenh thuong hieu, website, ads tra phi, offline, san TMDT
- [ ] **Chinh sua:** cat, ghep, text overlay duoc, khong doi noi dung loi noi cua creator
- [ ] **Whitelisting / Spark Ads / Partnership ads:** co cho phep brand chay ads qua tai khoan creator khong; thoi han; creator co the thu hoi khi nao; ai nam quyen truy cap, quyen do duoc cap qua co che nao cua nen tang (ten tinh nang tren tung nen tang khac nhau — kiem tra tai trang huong dan cua nen tang ngay lap ke hoach)
- [ ] **Mua dut:** neu brand muon dung vo thoi han hoac them kenh, ghi dieu khoan rieng va gia rieng (xem `references/co-che-affiliate.md` muc 6)
- [ ] Hinh anh, giong noi cua creator trong quang cao: ghi ro dong y bang van ban

### 2.4 Doc quyen va go bai

- [ ] **Doc quyen:** creator khong quay cho doi thu truc tiep trong khoang thoi gian va pham vi ro rang. De trong `[X thang]`, thuong luong theo muc hop tac
- [ ] **Go bai:** brand co quyen yeu cau go hoac sua bai neu sai su that hoac vi pham quy dinh; thoi han thuc hien
- [ ] **Khong xoa bai** trong khoang thoi gian toi thieu, tru khi brand yeu cau go

### 2.5 Thanh toan

- [ ] Moc thanh toan (vd chia theo moc duyet va moc dang), hinh thuc va thoi han
- [ ] Voi hoa hong: cua so ghi nhan, cach xu ly don hoan / huy, thoi diem chot doi soat
- [ ] Ai xu ly thue theo cach nao: **hoi ke toan** (hub muc 6) — khong ghi thue suat hay nguong trong hop dong mau

### 2.6 Du lieu ca nhan

- [ ] Neu chia se danh sach khach, so dien thoai, hoac tra cuu thong tin khach: ghi muc dich, ai kiem soat, ai xu ly (hub muc 2)
- [ ] Creator khong duoc dung du lieu khach cho muc dich khac

### 2.7 Cong duyet (quy tac 7)

- [ ] Ghi ro nguoi co tham quyen cua brand duyet va ky
- [ ] Chuyen tien, dang bai, chay ads bang video creator can duyet co ten tung hanh dong

## 3. Cau mau cong bo (thuc hanh tot, khong phai nghia vu theo tung chu)

Nghia vu phap ly la cong bo **truoc va trong khi** quang cao (hub muc 3). Cac cau duoi day la **thuc hanh tot** de brand dua cho creator. Moi nen tang co nhan "Noi dung tai tro" / "Quang cao" rieng — dung them nhan do khi co.

**Dau video / dau bai**
- "Noi dung hop tac voi [Brand]."
- "#hoptac #quangcao [Brand]"

**Trong video noi**
- "Video nay la hop tac voi [Brand] — minh da dung [san pham] duoc [thoi gian]."
- "Minh duoc [Brand] gui san pham de dung thu, y kien duoi day la cua minh."

**Livestream (nhac lai giua phien)**
- Cong bo ngay luc mo phien va nhac lai dinh ky trong khi live (nghia vu phap ly: quang cao phai duoc nhan ro la quang cao **truoc va trong khi** quang cao — hub muc 3, Luat Quang cao sua doi 75/2025; hub muc 5 chi ap dung cho nghia vu cua nguoi ban / nguoi livestream ban hang tren san).

**Khong nen**
- Dau hieu qua nho hoac chi nam o cuoi bai sau dong "xem them".
- Chi dat hashtag cuoi caption ma khong co noi dung bao.
- Noi "minh tu mua" khi that ra duoc brand gui hoac tra tien.
