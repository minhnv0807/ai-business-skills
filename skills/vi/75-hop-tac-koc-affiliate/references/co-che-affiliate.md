# Co che affiliate cho chuong trinh KOC

> **Reference file** cua `75-hop-tac-koc-affiliate`. Quy tac van hanh chung: `skills/vi/references/quy-tac-van-hanh.md` (dac biet quy tac 2, 3, 4, 7). Phap ly: `skills/vi/references/phap-ly-marketing-vn.md` muc 3, 5, 6. Khong ghi % phi san, thue suat hay muc hoa hong cung o day.

Ten tinh nang, dieu kien va thoi han tren tung san (TikTok Shop, Shopee va cac san khac) **thay doi lien tuc** (quy tac 4). Moi muc co "kiem tra" ben duoi nghia la kiem tra tai Seller Center cua chinh gian hang ngay lap ke hoach va ghi ngay kiem.

---

## 1. Chuong trinh mo va chuong trinh nhat dinh

| Loai | Cach hoat dong | Phu hop | Rui ro |
|------|----------------|---------|--------|
| Mo (open plan) | Moi creator du dieu kien cua san deu dang ky va ban duoc | Can nhieu nguoi thu nhanh, san pham de ban | It kiem soat chat luong noi dung va cach noi ve san pham |
| Nhat dinh (targeted plan) | Brand moi hoac duyet tung creator, thuong voi hoa hong rieng | Creator da tham dinh, san pham can giai thich, claim nhay cam | Ton cong quan ly, so nguoi it hon |

Goi y thu tu (heuristic): bat dau nhat dinh voi nhom nho da tham dinh (`references/tham-dinh-va-hop-dong-koc.md`), doi so lieu vong 1 roi moi can nhac mo rong.

**Yeu cau mau (sample request):** nhieu san cho creator xin mau truc tiep tren nen tang. Nen duyet tay thay vi tu dong, vi mau co chi phi that. Truoc khi duyet: da qua tham dinh chua? Da co thoa thuan cong bo va quyen su dung chua? Kiem tra Seller Center de biet san co ho tro yeu cau mau, dieu kien va gioi han so luong. Mau gui di la hanh dong ghi → can nguoi duyet co ten (quy tac 7).

## 2. Tran hoa hong

Bien so (khong ghi so cung):

```
Gia ban                         = [G]
Chi phi hang + van hanh         = [C]
Phi san va cac phi lien quan    = [P]   -> lay tu 76-van-hanh-san-tmdt, references/phi-san-tmdt.md (kiem tra Seller Center)
Bien loi nhuan sau phi san      = G - C - P            (dong gop truoc hoa hong)
Loi nhuan muc tieu              = [L]   -> brand quyet dinh
Hoa hong toi da (commission)    = bien loi nhuan sau phi san - loi nhuan muc tieu
```

Cach dung:
- Dat hoa hong **thap hon** tran de co khoang tro cho khuyen mai, hoan tra va chi phi quan ly.
- Neu bien loi nhuan sau phi san nho hon hoac bang loi nhuan muc tieu → khong co khoang cho hoa hong: doi gia, doi gia von, hoac khong chay chuong trinh affiliate cho san pham nay.
- Phan thue khau tru tren san de ngoai bien dong gop (hub muc 6; **hoi ke toan**). Khong tru thue vao cong thuc nay.
- Vi du minh hoa, neu dung, phai ghi "vi du gia dinh". Khong ghi muc hoa hong "pho bien" vi nguon khong kiem chung duoc.

## 3. Cua so ghi nhan (attribution window)

- Moi san co cach ghi nhan don cho creator khac nhau: theo click vao link, theo ma, theo thoi gian tu luc click den luc mua, theo thoi diem don hoan tat. **Kiem tra** tren san, khong gia dinh.
- Hoi ro: don bi huy / hoan co bi tru hoa hong khong, hoa hong tinh vao thoi diem nao (khi dat, khi giao thanh cong, hay sau thoi han doi tra)?
- Ghi cua so ghi nhan vao hop dong de tranh tranh chap (`references/tham-dinh-va-hop-dong-koc.md` muc 2.5).
- Khi chay song song nhieu kenh (ma giam gia rieng, UTM): chon 1 nguon chot va ghi nguon do vao bang cham diem.

## 4. Kiem tra tap trung vao it creator (heuristic)

Chi so de nhin: phan doanh thu chuong trinh den tu 1-2 creator hang dau.

- Neu mot hoac hai creator chiem phan lon doanh thu: chuong trinh phu thuoc vao ho. Rui ro: ho doi y, doi nen tang, hoac dung hop tac thi doanh thu sut.
- Hanh dong: tim them it nhat vai creator thay the cung vai tro; hop dong dai han voi creator then chot (quyen uu tien, dieu khoan doc quyen); khong tang hoa hong vo toi va cho creator then chot.
- Day la heuristic. Khong ghi nguong % cung; dieu chinh theo quy mo chuong trinh va khoang trong thanh khoan cua brand. Kiem tra bang so that: `[CAN SO LIEU THAT]`.

## 5. Tin hieu gian lan

| Tin hieu | Cach nhan biet | Hanh dong |
|----------|---------------|----------|
| Tu mua qua ma / link cua chinh minh | Nhieu don cung dia chi, cung SDT, cung thiet bi, hoac nguoi mua la nguoi lien quan creator | Danh dau, doi soat, loai khoi hoa hong theo dieu khoan hop dong |
| Lach hoan tra | Ty le hoan / huy cao bat thuong cua mot creator, don hoan tra sau khi da tinh hoa hong | Doi soat voi san, giu hoa hong den het cua so doi tra theo hop dong |
| Don bat thuong sau khi dang | Don don dap vao 1 gio, 1 khu vuc, thanh toan giong nhau | Dung chay chuong trinh voi creator do, kiem tra truoc khi chi tra |
| Don tu ma giam gia lo ra ngoai | Ma rieng cua creator xuat hien tren trang tong hop ma giam gia | Doi ma, ghi vao quy dinh su dung ma |
| Noi sai su that de keo don | Binh luan hoi lai, khieu nai sau khi mua | Go bai, xu ly theo hop dong (hub muc 3, muc 7) |

Day la tin hieu `inferred`: ghi ly do, doi soat voi san truoc khi ket luan. Khong tu dong phat tien hay khoa tai khoan khi chua co bang chung.

## 6. Dieu khoan mua dut quyen su dung (buyout)

Khi video cua creator co hieu qua va brand muon dung lau dai cho ads hoac kenh khac:

- Tach **phi mua dut** khoi phi quay: ghi ro pham vi (kenh, thoi han, khu vuc, co whitelisting hay khong) va muc gia rieng.
- Mac dinh thoa thuan ban dau chi cho phep thoi han ngan va kenh han che; muon mo rong → mua dut bang phu luc.
- Gia mua dut khong ghi ban gia trong skill: lay tu bao gia nhan duoc va so voi chi phi tao video moi (`[CAN SO LIEU THAT]`).
- Quyen thu hoi: khi nao creator hoac brand co the yeu cau ngung (vi pham cong bo, noi sai su that, doi thu hoac tai khoan thay doi).
- Doi voi whitelisting / Spark Ads / Partnership ads: ghi ai cap quyen, thoi han cap quyen, va cach thu hoi quyen khi het han (kiem tra tinh nang tren nen tang).
