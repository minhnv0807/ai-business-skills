# Seeding Plan

> Quy tac van hanh chung: `skills/vi/references/quy-tac-van-hanh.md` (dac biet quy tac 2, 3, 7).

> Hien dien trong cong dong duoc la nho huu ich truoc, lo quan he voi brand sau, bang nguoi that va danh tinh that. Seeding bang noi dung gia, nguoi gia hoac gia vo la khach vua gay hai brand vua trai luat — xem phan "Red line" va `skills/vi/references/phap-ly-marketing-vn.md` muc 7.

## Thu thap thong tin

Doc `.agents/product-marketing-context.md` va insight (09) neu co. Neu thieu, hoi toi da 4 cau:

1. **San pham / dich vu can seeding — va target audience dang o group / forum / cong dong nao?**
2. **Muc tieu seeding la gi?** Awareness / lead / traffic / social proof that / lay y kien san pham
3. **Ai trong doi ngu co the tham gia bang ten that, moi tuan duoc bao nhieu gio?** Co khach that nao san long chia se khong?
4. **Da co bai seeding nao truoc day work chua — hoc duoc gi tu do? Co bai nao bi xoa hoac bi canh cao khong?**

## Nguyen tac

1. **Nguoi that, danh tinh that, mot tai khoan moi nguoi.** Moi nguoi tham gia la nguoi co that trong doi ngu hoac cong dong, dung dung tai khoan cua minh. Cong bo quan he voi brand ngay trong bai, khong de trong phan gioi thieu.
2. **Gia tri that truoc, brand sau.** Ti le Value:Promotion goi y 4:1 (heuristic — nhieu cong dong co quy dinh rieng, quy dinh cua group la tren het). Ban EN dung 9:1 theo thong le Reddit; chon theo group.
3. **Warm-up truoc khi seeding.** Tuong tac thuong ngay voi group toi thieu 2 tuan (ly tuong 2-4 tuan) truoc bai co nhac brand.
4. **Ton trong rule group.** Doc rule truoc khi dang. Uu tien group co admin active — bai de duoc duyet va de live hon.
5. **Khong spam.** Gioi han goi y (heuristic, xem rule tung group): 1 nguoi toi da 2 group/ngay; cach 3-5 ngay giua 2 lan dang cung 1 group; 1-2 bai/tuan/group.
6. **Review va chia se cua khach phai do khach viet.** Brand khong viet ho, khong gui kich ban, khong tra tien de khach khen. Neu co tang qua / uu dai cho khach, noi ro trong bai.
7. **Do luong duoc.** UTM link (khi duoc phep dang link), hoi khach "biet minh qua dau", dem DM den tu group.
8. **Seeding co tra phi qua KOC / KOL khong nam o day.** Chon, tham dinh, bao gia → `75-hop-tac-koc-affiliate`; brief quay → `06-brief-ugc-egc`. Noi dung tra phi phai cong bo la quang cao (hub muc 3).

## Quy trinh — 4 phase

### Phase 1 — Research group (1 tuan)

Tieu chi chon group (nguong goi y — heuristic, dieu chinh theo nganh):

| Tieu chi | Nguong goi y |
|----------|-------------|
| Size | Du lon de co thao luan, du nho de bai khong chim; B2B ngach: nhom nho nhieu tuong tac thang nhom lon it hoat dong |
| Activity | Co bai moi moi ngay, co thread comment that |
| Rules | Cho phep chia se / hoi dap lien quan nganh — ghi nguyen van rule |
| Admin | Active — co duyet bai, co tuong tac |
| Do khop audience | Thanh vien trung voi ICP (doi chieu 09) |

Output: bang danh sach group xep uu tien Cao / Vua / Thap + loai content phu hop tung group.

### Phase 2 — Danh sach nguoi tham gia that va warm-up (2-4 tuan)

Chi dung 4 nhom nguoi sau. Moi nhom co cach cong bo quan he rieng:

| Nhom | Ai | Cach cong bo quan he | Luu y |
|------|----|----------------------|-------|
| Trang thuong hieu | Fanpage / tai khoan chinh thuc cua brand | Ten brand hien ro, khong gia lam thanh vien thuong | Hop cho tra loi cau hoi ve san pham, thong bao chinh thuc |
| Nhan vien | Nhan vien, founder dung ten that, anh that | "Minh lam o [Brand]" trong bai hoac comment; bio ghi noi lam viec | Tra loi dung chuyen mon cua minh (CSKH tra loi CSKH, founder tra loi mo hinh kinh doanh) |
| KOC tra phi | Creator da chon va tham dinh qua `75-hop-tac-koc-affiliate` | Nhan "Quang cao" / "Noi dung tai tro" + cau cong bo hop tac dau bai (hub muc 3) | Brief qua `06-brief-ugc-egc`; hop dong ghi nghia vu cong bo |
| Khach that duoc moi | Khach da mua, san long chia se | Khach tu noi minh la khach; neu co qua tang / uu dai, khach noi ro | **Moi, khong kich ban:** khong viet ho, khong chi dinh noi dung, khong tra tien de khen |

Moi nguoi tham gia can:

- Profile that: ten that, avatar that, bio ghi noi lam viec (voi nhan vien).
- Lich su hoat dong trong group: join, tra loi cau hoi 3-5 lan/tuan, react.
- Khong nhac brand trong giai doan warm-up.
- Linh vuc chuyen mon that — noi dung dang phai khop voi nhung gi ho thuc su biet.

Neu khong ai trong doi co thoi gian, dung chay plan seeding: chuyen muc tieu sang KOC tra phi co cong bo (`75`, `06`). Khong tim cach "lap" them nguoi tham gia de lam day lich.

### Phase 3 — Noi dung tham gia (ongoing)

Viet san 4 loai bai — moi bai 150-250 chu, giong nguoi that dang noi (khong dung giong fanpage):

| Loai | Muc tieu | Cach nhac brand |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| 1. Hoi that / tham do | Hoc tu cong dong, lay ngon ngu nhu cau | KHONG nhac brand. Chi hoi dieu nguoi hoi that su muon biet — khong tao cau hoi de dan dat ai do |
| 2. Chia se kinh nghiem (education) | Cho gia tri that, xay uy tin cho chinh nguoi chia se | Cong bo quan he truoc; nhac san pham chi khi do that su la cau tra loi |
| 3. Tra loi thread co san | Giup nguoi da hoi | Cong bo quan he ngay trong comment, bang loi thuong |
| 4. Cau chuyen cua khach (do khach viet) | Social proof that | Khach tu viet va tu dang, hoac duoc trich dan co xin phep bang van ban. Brand khong viet ho |

Dang tot trong group: cau hoi mo, chia se kinh nghiem co so lieu that, mini guide, case study ngan, post-mortem co ca diem chua duoc. Review nhan gia (khach hai long) luon co ca diem chua hoan hao — nhung viec nay do chinh khach noi, khong phai brand dinh huong.

Kich ban tra loi di kem moi bai:

- Chuan bi san cau tra loi trung thuc cho 3-5 cau hoi du kien tu thanh vien that (gia, dia chi, co hieu qua khong, mua o dau, so voi cach khac thi sao).
- Tra loi comment trong 2-4 gio dau — bai co tuong tac som thuong duoc phan phoi tot hon.
- Neu ai hoi "ban co lam o do khong?" → tra loi thang. Khong ne tranh.
- Chi nhac san pham khi thanh vien that da mo ta van de ma san pham giai quyet, va cong bo quan he cung luc.
- Luon co gia tri trong comment — khong comment "inbox minh nhe" tran lan.

### Phase 4 — Do luong

| Chi so | Cach do |
|--------|---------|
| Reach / tuong tac bai | Screenshot so react, comment, share tung bai |
| DM den tu group | Hoi "ban biet minh qua dau" khi khach inbox |
| Traffic | UTM link rieng cho tung group (chi khi group cho phep link) |
| Lead / don | Dem lead ghi nguon "group [ten]" trong CRM |
| Bai bi xoa / canh cao / ban | Ghi lai kem rule bi vi pham de danh gia rui ro group do |

## Red line — KHONG lam khi seeding

Cac dieu duoi day la lan ranh do ve phap ly va uy tin: xem `skills/vi/references/phap-ly-marketing-vn.md` muc 7 (Luat Quang cao sua doi 75/2025, kiem tra van ban goc tai vbpl.vn / thuvienphapluat.vn) va muc 3 (nghia vu cong bo).

- KHONG tao persona ao, nick ao, tai khoan clone hoac tai khoan phu de dong vai khach hang.
- KHONG dung comment mua / thue / viet san de dong vai khach hang that, hoac de tao hoi thoai gia giua cac tai khoan cua minh.
- KHONG "seeding" ma che giau duong dan thuong mai hoac quan he hop tac voi brand.
- KHONG viet review thay khach, hay gui kich ban de khach chep lai.
- KHONG an cong bo quan he khi nguoi dang la nhan vien, KOC tra phi hoac co loi ich lien quan.
- KHONG spam link san pham truc tiep (dac biet bai dau tien trong group).
- KHONG post promotional khong co gia tri.
- KHONG copy-paste cung noi dung sang nhieu group.
- KHONG tag nguoi khong lien quan.
- KHONG cai nhau voi admin. Bai bi xoa → doc rule, dieu chinh, di tiep.

Rui ro can quan ly: bai bi admin xoa / tai khoan bi ban (mat cong warm-up — tuan thu rule de tranh); bi cong dong phat hien la quang cao khong cong bo (brand risk, phap ly — cong bo quan he tu dau, neu bi hoi thang thi tra loi minh bach); group doi rule (doc lai rule moi thang).
