# Seeding Plan

> Bai seeding tot nhat la bai khong ai biet la seeding. Gia tri that truoc — mention brand sau, tu nhien, dung context. Seeding sai cach (spam, fake) gay hai brand nhieu hon la khong lam.

## Thu thap thong tin

Doc `.agents/product-marketing-context.md` va insight (09) neu co. Neu thieu, hoi toi da 4 cau:

1. **San pham / dich vu can seeding — va target audience dang o group / forum / cong dong nao?**
2. **Muc tieu seeding la gi?** Awareness / lead / traffic / social proof
3. **Co tai khoan "that" (co lich su hoat dong, ban be, bai ca nhan) de seeding khong? May tai khoan?**
4. **Da co bai seeding nao truoc day work chua — hoc duoc gi tu do?**

## Nguyen tac

1. **Gia tri that truoc, brand sau.** Ti le Value:Promotion = 4:1 — cu 4 lan cho gia tri moi 1 lan co yeu to promotion.
2. **Warm-up truoc khi seeding.** Tuong tac voi group toi thieu 2 tuan (ly tuong 2-4 tuan) truoc bai seeding dau tien.
3. **Ton trong rule group.** Doc rule truoc khi dang. Uu tien group co admin active — bai de duoc duyet va de live hon.
4. **Khong spam.** 1 tai khoan toi da 2 group/ngay; cach 3-5 ngay giua 2 lan dang cung 1 group / 1 tai khoan; tan suat 1-2 bai/tuan/group.
5. **Khong fake.** Khong tao hoi thoai gia giua cac tai khoan cua minh, khong copy-paste cung noi dung sang nhieu group.
6. **Do luong duoc.** UTM link (khi duoc phep dang link), hoi khach "biet minh qua dau", dem DM den tu group.

## Quy trinh — 4 phase

### Phase 1 — Research group (1 tuan)

Tieu chi chon group:

| Tieu chi | Nguong |
|----------|--------|
| Size | Tren 5.000 thanh vien |
| Activity | Tren 10 bai/ngay duoc duyet |
| Rules | Cho phep chia se / hoi dap lien quan nganh |
| Admin | Active — co duyet bai, co tuong tac |
| Do khop audience | Thanh vien trung voi ICP (doi chieu 09) |

Output: bang danh sach group xep uu tien Cao / Vua / Thap + loai content phu hop tung group.

### Phase 2 — Warm-up tai khoan (2-4 tuan)

Persona tai khoan seeding — moi tai khoan can:

- Profile that: avatar nguoi that, thong tin co ban, ban be, bai ca nhan dang deu.
- Lich su hoat dong trong group: join, comment / tra loi cau hoi 3-5 lan/tuan, like, react.
- Tuyet doi khong mention brand trong giai doan warm-up.
- Vai (persona) nhat quan: vi du "me bim sua quan 7 quan tam skincare", "chu shop online dang tim tool" — noi dung dang phai khop vai.

Tai khoan moi lap, khong co lich su → KHONG dung de seeding.

### Phase 3 — Seeding content (ongoing)

Viet san 4 loai bai — moi bai 150-250 chu, dung tone nguoi that (khong dung giong fanpage):

| Loai | Muc tieu | Cach mention brand |
|------|----------|--------------------|
| 1. Hoi tham do (soft) | Dat cau hoi de khach tu tra loi, tham do nhu cau | KHONG mention brand |
| 2. Chia se kinh nghiem (education) | Cho gia tri that, xay uy tin vai | Mention tu nhien o cuoi, nhu 1 chi tiet phu |
| 3. Review / testimonial (social proof) | Viet nhu khach that ke trai nghiem | Co so lieu that, co ca diem chua perfect — khong khen 100% |
| 4. Tim kiem / nho gioi thieu (demand) | Tao nhu cau, de nguoi khac recommend | Khong tu mention — cho cong dong nhac ten |

Dang tot trong group: cau hoi mo, chia se kinh nghiem soft mention, mini guide, case study ngan.

Kich ban comment di kem moi bai:

- Chuan bi san cau tra loi cho 3-5 cau hoi du kien tu thanh vien that (gia, dia chi, co hieu qua khong, mua o dau).
- Tra loi comment trong 2-4 gio dau — bai co tuong tac som duoc phan phoi tot hon.
- Neu bai loai 4 (demand): tai khoan khac cua he thong CHI duoc recommend khi co thanh vien that hoi — khong tu dung nhay vao tao hoi thoai gia.
- Luon co gia tri trong comment (them tip, tra loi that) — khong comment "inbox minh nhe" tran lan.

### Phase 4 — Do luong

| Chi so | Cach do |
|--------|---------|
| Reach / tuong tac bai | Screenshot so react, comment, share tung bai |
| DM den tu group | Hoi "ban biet minh qua dau" khi khach inbox |
| Traffic | UTM link rieng cho tung group (chi khi group cho phep link) |
| Lead / don | Dem lead ghi nguon "group [ten]" trong CRM |
| Bai bi xoa / tai khoan bi ban | Ghi lai de danh gia rui ro group do |

## Red line — KHONG lam khi seeding

- KHONG spam link san pham truc tiep (dac biet bai dau tien trong group).
- KHONG post promotional khong co gia tri.
- KHONG dung tai khoan moi khong co lich su.
- KHONG copy-paste cung noi dung sang nhieu group.
- KHONG fake comment tao hoi thoai gia giua tai khoan cua minh.
- KHONG tag nguoi khong lien quan.

Rui ro can quan ly: bai bi admin xoa / tai khoan bi ban (mat cong warm-up — tuan thu rule de tranh); bi cong dong phat hien seeding (brand risk — neu bi hoi thang, tra loi minh bach, khong choi cai); group doi rule (doc lai rule moi thang).
