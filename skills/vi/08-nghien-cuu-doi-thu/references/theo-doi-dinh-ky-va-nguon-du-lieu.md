## Che do THEO DOI DINH KY (Competitor Watch)

Bao cao day du o tren lam 1-2 lan/nam. Con **theo doi dinh ky** la nhip chay hang thang — nhe hon, nhanh hon, va co gia tri hon vi no bat duoc **thay doi theo thoi gian** chu khong chi chup 1 khoanh khac.

> Nguyen tac: theo doi 1 lan/thang co insight tot hon theo doi lien tuc ma khong rut ra duoc gi. Danh 80% nang luong xay the manh cua minh, 20% cho viec nhin doi thu.

### Ky luat theo doi

- **Deu dan hon day du.** Snapshot 30 phut moi thang tot hon bao cao 20 trang moi nam.
- **Khong phan ung voi tung dong thai cua doi thu.** Doi thu giam gia khong co nghia minh phai giam gia.
- **Khong copy** — phan tich vi sao no hieu qua roi moi quyet dinh co adapt hay khong.
- Doi thu moi noi vao watch list ngay khi thay ho xuat hien >= 2 lan trong tep cua minh.

## Nguon du lieu — uu tien nguon mien phi

Khong can tra tien cho bao cao thi truong de lam duoc phan lon viec nay.

### Nguon mien phi (dung truoc)

| Nguon | Xem duoc gi | Thoi gian |
|-------|------------|-----------|
| Meta Ads Library (`facebook.com/ads/library`) | Ads dang chay, creative, offer, thoi gian ads da chay | 30 phut |

**Tong thoi gian theo doi 1 doi thu day du: khoang 90 phut.** Snapshot rut gon hang thang: 30 phut.

### Nguon co phi (chi dung khi that su can)

Kantar (xu huong tieu dung, thi phan) · Statista (so lieu thi truong, co ban mien phi gioi han) · Vietnam Report (xep hang doanh nghiep VN) · cong cu social listening thuong mai. Chi dau tu khi da khai thac het nguon mien phi ma van thieu du lieu quyet dinh.

### Khi thieu du lieu — 5 cach lam thay

1. **Doc comment duoi bai doi thu** → lay noi dau va ngon ngu that cua khach (dua sang `09-insight-khach-hang`).
2. **Seeding cau hoi trong group nganh** → quan sat phan ung, khong can khao sat chinh thuc.
3. **Phong van 10 khach tieu bieu** → hoi ve **hanh vi da lam**, khong hoi **y kien** ("Lan gan nhat ban mua o dau?" chu khong phai "Ban thay the nao?").
4. **Chay A/B test nho 200-500K/ngay** → de thi truong tra loi thay vi ngoi doan message nao dung.
5. **Phan tich content viral cua doi thu** → biet goc nhin nao dang duoc khach quan tam ngay luc nay.
