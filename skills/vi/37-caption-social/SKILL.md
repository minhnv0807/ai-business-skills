# Caption Social

> Caption organic cho bai dang tu nhien tren Facebook / Instagram / TikTok. Caption tot = hook du manh de dung ngon tay, body du thuyet phuc, CTA du ro de khach biet lam gi. Viet cho nguoi doc tren dien thoai, dang luot nhanh. Ads copy tra phi (co chinh sach quang cao, gioi han ky tu ads) → dung `05-copy-quang-cao`.

## Thu thap thong tin

Doc Brand Voice Document (35) va content brief (36) neu co — brief da co angle, hook direction, CTA thi dung luon, khong hoi lai. Neu thieu, hoi toi da 4 cau:

1. **San pham / dich vu / chu de bai viet la gi — va co so lieu / testimonial / case study that khong?**
2. **Tang pheu nao?** TOFU (nhan biet) / MOFU (tin tuong) / BOFU (chot don)
3. **Kenh nao?** Facebook Page / Facebook Group / Instagram / TikTok
4. **CTA mong muon va do dai muc tieu?** (comment / click / DM / mua — ngan <100 chu / vua 100-200 / dai >200)

## Nguyen tac

1. **Cau dau = hook.** Tuyet doi khong bat dau bang ten brand hoac "Xin chao" — nguoi doc luot qua trong 1 giay.
2. **1 caption = 1 message.** Khong nhoi nhieu benefit vao 1 bai.
3. **So lieu cu the hon tinh tu mo ho.** "Tang 47% doanh thu" tot hon "tang manh".
4. **Viet cho mobile.** Doan ngan 1-3 cau, co khoang trang giua cac doan — khong viet block dai.
5. **BOFU: CTA phai ro va co urgency.** Khong de khach tu suy luan lam gi tiep.
6. **Doc to len truoc khi chot.** Nghe cung nhu doc bao cao la chua dung tone.
7. **Doi chieu brand voice.** Check vocabulary + banned words trong Brand Voice Document (35) truoc khi giao. Banned mac dinh: "Hay cung chung toi", "Khong the bo lo", "Sieu hot", "Dung bo lo co hoi".

## Quy trinh

### Buoc 1 — Doc brief va brand voice

Lay tu 36-content-brief: angle, insight goc, key message, CTA. Lay tu 35-brand-voice: tone, vocabulary, cau truc cau. Neu chua co brief — thu thap thong tin theo 4 cau tren.

### Buoc 2 — Viet hook theo tang pheu

Hook dung doc lap, khong can context. Chon huong theo tang:

| Tang | Huong hook | Vi du dang |
|------|-----------|------------|
| TOFU | Pain / Curiosity / Contrarian | Cau hoi cham noi dau, statement nguoc |
| MOFU | Social proof / Ket qua cu the | So lieu, testimonial ngan, truoc/sau |
| BOFU | Offer / Urgency | Con so uu dai ro, deadline, so luong gioi han |

### Buoc 3 — Viet 2 variant A/B

Hai variant phai khac nhau that su — khong chi doi vai chu:

- **Variant A**: 1 huong hook (vi du pain) + 1 cau truc body (vi du list).
- **Variant B**: hook khac huong (vi du curiosity hoac social proof) + cau truc khac (vi du storytelling).

Moi variant gom: Hook (cau dau) → Body (2-4 doan ngan, moi doan 1-3 cau) → CTA (cau hanh dong cu the).

### Buoc 4 — Ghi chu chon variant

Ghi ro: dung A khi nao (context / audience nao), dung B khi nao, va cach mix neu muon (hook A + body B hoac nguoc lai).

### Buoc 5 — Hashtag va platform adaptation

Hashtag 5-10, sap xep: branded → niche → broad. Dieu chinh theo kenh:

| Kenh | Dieu chinh |
|------|-----------|
| Facebook Page | Caption dai duoc, xuong dong nhieu, 3-5 hashtag cuoi bai |
| Facebook Group | Tone nguoi that chia se — khong giong bai fanpage, han che hashtag |
| Instagram | Caption ngan, visual keo chinh, hashtag co the de block rieng |
| TikTok | Caption 1-2 dong, chua keyword de search, hook nam trong video — caption bo tro |
