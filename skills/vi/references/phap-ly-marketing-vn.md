## 2. Du lieu ca nhan — Luat 91/2025 + ND 356/2025

**Da kiem:** Luat 91/2025/QH15 thong qua 2025-06-26, hieu luc 2026-01-01. ND 356/2025/ND-CP ky 2025-12-31, hieu luc 2026-01-01, huong dan luat va **thay the ND 13/2023/ND-CP**. ND 356 yeu cau bo nhiem nguoi/bo phan bao ve du lieu ca nhan **bang van ban**. ND 330/2026/ND-CP la nghi dinh xu phat rieng ve an ninh mang va du lieu ca nhan, hieu luc 2026-08-19. Kiem tra van ban goc tai vbpl.vn / thuvienphapluat.vn.

**Checklist marketer** (thuc hanh tot theo tinh than luat — doi chieu dieu khoan cu the voi van ban goc truoc khi dua vao hop dong / chinh sach):

- [ ] Moi form thu SDT / email / Zalo ghi ro **muc dich** (vd "gui uu dai qua Zalo") va link chinh sach du lieu.
- [ ] O dong y nhan marketing **khong tick san**, tach rieng khoi o dong y dieu khoan dich vu.
- [ ] Luu **log dong y**: thoi diem, nguon form, noi dung da dong y.
- [ ] Co cach rut lai dong y (huy dang ky email, "STOP" Zalo) va xu ly that.
- [ ] Chi upload danh sach khach len Custom Audience / Customer Match khi da co co so dong y cho muc dich quang cao.
- [ ] Chi dinh bang van ban nguoi/bo phan phu trach bao ve du lieu (nghia vu theo ND 356).
- [ ] Hop dong voi agency / ben cung cap CRM ghi ro ai la ben kiem soat, ai la ben xu ly du lieu.

Khong ghi muc phat. Neu khach hoi "phat bao nhieu" → "xem ND 330/2026 — hoi luat su".

## 3. Quang cao va nguoi chuyen tai (KOL/KOC) — Luat QC sua doi 75/2025

**Da kiem:** Luat 75/2025/QH15 sua doi, bo sung Luat Quang cao, thong qua 2025-06-16, hieu luc 2026-01-01.

- **Dieu 2 khoan 8** dinh nghia "nguoi chuyen tai san pham quang cao": ca nhan / to chuc dung hinh anh, ten tuoi cua minh de quang cao, gioi thieu, xac nhan san pham tren mang.
- **Dieu 15a** — nghia vu nguoi chuyen tai:
  - kiem tra thong tin nguoi quang cao va tai lieu ve san pham;
  - **khong** quang cao san pham minh chua su dung / chua hieu ro;
  - thong bao ro day la noi dung quang cao **truoc va trong khi** quang cao.
- Quang cao sai su that gay thiet hai: bi xu phat, buoc cai chinh, boi thuong (muc phat: khong ghi).
- Mang xa hoi phai co tinh nang giup nguoi dung phan biet noi dung quang cao.
- Nghi dinh huong dan: [DU THAO] — kiem tra trang thai ban hanh.

Kiem tra van ban goc tai vbpl.vn / thuvienphapluat.vn.

**Checklist khi brand thue KOL/KOC/creator:**

- [ ] Gui creator tai lieu san pham + giay to lien quan (cong bo, kiem nghiem neu nganh can) — de creator lam duoc nghia vu kiem tra.
- [ ] Creator **da dung that** san pham truoc khi quay (gui mau du som).
- [ ] Dong cong bo quang cao xuat hien **dau video / dau bai va trong suot** (vd: "#hoptac voi [brand]", nhan "Quang cao" / "Noi dung tai tro" cua nen tang).
- [ ] Moi claim cong dung co bang chung; nganh nhay cam (thuc pham chuc nang, my pham, y te, tai chinh) doi chieu `74-copy-hai-lop/references/bien-gioi-va-nguon.md` muc "Luat va rui ro VN".
- [ ] Hop dong ghi nghia vu cong bo + quyen go bai neu sai — xem `75-hop-tac-koc-affiliate`.
