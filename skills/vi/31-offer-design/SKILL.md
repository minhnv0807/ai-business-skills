# Offer Design

Offer la "thu minh ban": ket qua, goi thanh phan, bao dam, bonus, ly do han che, cach thanh toan. Copy tot khong cuu duoc offer yeu; pricing dung nhung offer mo ho van kho ban.

## Thu thap thong tin

Doc `.agents/product-marketing-context.md` neu co. Neu thieu, hoi toi da 4 cau: san pham/dich vu ban gi, khach hang nao, gia hien tai/du kien, ly do khach con do du. Neu user co landing page, sales page, proposal, pricing sheet, hay lay do lam dau vao. Neu can chi tiet, doc `references/offer-framework.md`.

## Khi nao dung skill nay

Dung khi user can lam goi ban hap dan hon:

- Dich vu agency, tu van, coaching, khoa hoc, info product, membership, high-ticket B2B.
- Goi san pham can bonus, guarantee, payment plan, upsell/downsell.
- Offer dang co traffic/leads nhung conversion thap.

Khong dung skill nay de chi tinh gia. Neu chi can tier/gia/break-even, dung `17-pricing-strategy` truoc.

## Workflow

### 1. Chuan doan offer hien tai

Cham 5 diem, moi diem 1-5:

| Diem | Cau hoi |
|------|---------|
| Ket qua | Khach mua xong dat duoc dieu gi cu the? |
| Bang chung | Ho co tin minh lam duoc khong? |
| Toc do | Bao lau thay gia tri dau tien? |
| No luc | Khach phai tu lam nhieu khong? |
| Rui ro | Neu khong thanh cong, ai chiu rui ro? |

Uu tien sua diem thap nhat truoc.

### 2. Xay value equation

Tang hap dan bang 4 don bay:

- Tang **dream outcome**: noi ket qua ro hon, gan voi tien/thoi gian/danh tieng/suc khoe.
- Tang **perceived likelihood**: proof, case study, demo, guarantee, quy trinh.
- Giam **time delay**: quick win, onboarding nhanh, first milestone.
- Giam **effort/sacrifice**: template, done-for-you, support, automation.

### 3. Lap cau truc offer

Mot offer day du gom:

1. Core deliverable: thu chinh khach tra tien.
2. Bonus: chi them khi xu ly phan doi that.
3. Guarantee/risk reversal: cam ket trung thuc, khong hua qua kha nang.
4. Scarcity/urgency: chi dung khi co gioi han that.
5. Name: ten goi de nho, noi dung, khong hype.
6. Price/payment: gia, tra gop, deposit, upsell/downsell.

### 4. Tao ladder

Neu phu hop, de xuat 3 bac:

| Bac | Vai tro |
|-----|--------|
| Entry | Giam rao can, lay trust, dung cho user moi |
| Core | Goi chinh can ban nhieu nhat |
| Premium | Done-for-you / toc do cao / support sau |

### 5. Kiem tra dao duc

Loai bo fake scarcity, guarantee khong the thuc hien, bonus phong to gia tri ao, claim qua muc, va ngon ngu "lam giau nhanh".

## Checklist chat luong

- Ket qua chinh ro hon tinh nang.
- Bonus xu ly phan doi that, khong dung de lam day goi.
- Guarantee dung kha nang van hanh.
- Scarcity co ly do that va co the kiem chung.
- Gia va payment plan khong pha margin.
- Output co hanh dong tiep theo cho copy, landing page, email, sales.
