# Offer Framework

## Bang chuan doan nhanh

| Dau hieu | Nguyen nhan thuong gap | Cach sua |
|----------|------------------------|----------|
| Nhieu nguoi hoi nhung it mua | Offer chua ro ket qua | Viet lai ket qua theo KPI cu the |
| Khach noi "de suy nghi" | Rui ro cao, proof yeu | Them proof, guarantee, demo, pilot |
| Khach che dat | Gia tri cam nhan thap | Tang bonus xu ly phan doi, them support |
| Chot duoc nhung refund cao | Hua qua nhieu hoac onboarding yeu | Giam claim, them milestone dau |
| Ban duoc goi re, khong len goi cao | Ladder thieu ly do nang cap | Tach premium bang speed/support/done-for-you |

## 8 kieu guarantee

| Kieu | Phu hop | Canh bao |
|------|--------|----------|
| Money-back | San pham so, khoa hoc, template | Can dieu kien ro |
| Milestone | Coaching, tu van, agency | Cam ket quy trinh, khong cam ket doanh thu neu khong kiem soat |
| Service credit | SaaS, dich vu B2B | De van hanh hon refund |
| First-result | Tool, training, onboarding | Can xac dinh "result" nho |
| Satisfaction | Dich vu premium | Can scope va so lan revision |
| Pilot-to-paid | B2B high-ticket | Dung khi sales cycle dai |
| Cancellation promise | Subscription | Giam so hai bi khoa hop dong |
| No guarantee | Luxury, highly customized, enterprise | Phai co proof va positioning manh |

## Bonus dung cach

Bonus tot phai tra loi mot phan doi:

- "Toi khong co thoi gian" -> template, checklist, done-for-you setup.
- "Toi khong biet bat dau" -> onboarding call, roadmap 7 ngay.
- "Toi so khong hop nganh" -> industry examples, variant guide.
- "Toi so lam sai" -> review session, QA checklist.

Khong dung bonus chi de tang "gia tri ao".

## Scarcity hop le

Dung khi co gioi han that:

- Capacity: chi nhan X khach vi can delivery.
- Cohort: dong dang ky theo lich hoc/live.
- Inventory: hang vat ly co so luong.
- Seasonal: gan voi su kien that.
- Price increase: co ngay tang gia va ly do that.
- Bonus expiry: bonus het han vi ton thoi gian/nguon luc.

Khong dung countdown gia, slot ao, claim "chi hom nay" lap lai moi ngay.

## Name formula

Ten offer tot nen co 1 trong 4 mau:

- Outcome + audience: "90-Day Founder Pipeline Sprint"
- Mechanism + outcome: "AI Sales Page Engine"
- Stage + result: "First 100 Leads System"
- Category + differentiator: "Done-With-You TikTok Shop Launch"

Tranh: revolutionary, game-changing, secret, hack, 10x neu khong co proof.
